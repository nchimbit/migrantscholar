# STEM UK
The British Council Women in STEM Scholarship qualifies female migrant students with a £28,000 funding amount for a master's degree in a STEM field. 
This scholarship is one of the many [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded) available to migrant women in the UK, providing opportunities for them to pursue higher education and careers in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics. 

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for these scholarships, applicants must hold a valid visa category such as Tier 4 student visa, Tier 2 work visa, or have permission to stay in the UK as a refugee or under humanitarian protection. Migrant women with [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-eligibility/refugees) status are also encouraged to apply.

## Application Steps
Here are the steps to apply for these scholarships:
1. Check your eligibility for the scholarship and ensure you meet the criteria.
2. Review the application requirements and deadlines for the scholarship.
3. Prepare your application documents, including transcripts, CV, and personal statement.
4. Submit your application through the official website or email.
5. Ensure you have a valid visa category, such as a [Tier 4 student visa](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/universities/uk-visa), to study in the UK.
6. Apply for multiple scholarships to increase your chances of being awarded one.
7. Follow up with the scholarship provider to confirm receipt of your application.
