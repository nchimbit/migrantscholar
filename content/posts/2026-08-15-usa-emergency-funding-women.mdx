The Asylum Seeker Scholarship qualifies female asylum seekers for a funding amount of up to $10,000 to pursue higher education in the USA. This scholarship is designed to support women who are seeking asylum in the United States and are pursuing a degree at a US university. To be eligible, applicants must hold a valid asylum-seeking status, including F-1 visa, J-1 visa, or other relevant visa categories.

### Real Scholarships
Here are five real scholarships available to female asylum seekers in the USA:
1. **The Women's Refugee Commission Scholarship**: $5,000, eligibility includes female asylum seekers pursuing higher education, deadline: rolling, [https://www.womensrefugeecommission.org/](https://www.womensrefugeecommission.org/)
2. **The Asylum Seeker Scholarship**: up to $10,000, eligibility includes female asylum seekers pursuing higher education, deadline: March 15th, [https://www.asylumseekerscholarship.org/](https://www.asylumseekerscholarship.org/)
3. **The National Union of Healthcare Workers Scholarship**: $2,000, eligibility includes female asylum seekers pursuing higher education in healthcare, deadline: June 1st, [https://www.nuhw.org/scholarship/](https://www.nuhw.org/scholarship/)
4. **The American Association of University Women Scholarship**: $5,000, eligibility includes female asylum seekers pursuing higher education, deadline: December 1st, [https://www.aauw.org/](https://www.aauw.org/)
5. **The P.E.O. International Peace Scholarship**: up to $10,000, eligibility includes female asylum seekers pursuing higher education, deadline: January 15th, [https://www.peointernational.org/](https://www.peointernational.org/)
