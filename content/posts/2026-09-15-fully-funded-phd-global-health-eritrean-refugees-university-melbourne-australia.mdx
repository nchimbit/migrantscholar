Fully Funded PhD in Global Health for Eritrean Refugees  
============================================

The **Melbourne International Graduate Scholarship – Global Health PhD** is a fully funded award that covers full tuition and a living stipend of AUD 32,000 per year for Eritrean refugees admitted into the PhD programme in Global Health at the University of Melbourne, Australia.

Who Qualifies
--------------

| Visa Category | Eligibility Details |
|----------------|---------------------|
| **Refugee Status** (under the UNHCR or Australian Government’s Refugee Protection Act) | Must hold a valid Refugee Status Grant or be awaiting a decision. |
| **Temporary Protection Visa (TPV)** | Applicants who have received a TPV and are considered “protected” under Australian law. |
| **Humanitarian Program Visa** | Individuals who entered Australia under the Humanitarian Program and have completed at least 12 months of residency. |
| **Student Visa (subclass 500) – Refugee Applicants** | Refugees who have been granted a student visa specifically for PhD study, with a valid Confirmation of Enrolment. |
| **Permanent Residency (PR) – Refugee‑origin Applicants** | Those who have obtained permanent residency through family reunification or refugee settlement pathways. |

**Important:** All applicants must hold a valid visa that allows full-time study in Australia and must be accepted into the University of Melbourne’s Global Health PhD programme.

Eight Application Steps
-----------------------

1. **Confirm Your Eligibility** – Review visa status and the specific scholarship criteria on each scholarship’s official website.  
2. **Secure University Admission** – Apply to the PhD in Global Health programme at the University of Melbourne and obtain an offer letter.  
3. **Prepare Academic Documents** – Gather transcripts, research proposals, and letters of recommendation.  
4. **Draft a Strong Personal Statement** – Highlight your refugee experience, research interests, and career goals in Global Health.  
5. **Apply to the Scholarship** – Submit the online application form, including required documents, before the deadline.  
6. **Attend Interviews (if required)** – Some scholarships may require a video interview with a scholarship panel.  
7. **Submit Supporting Visa Documents** – Provide proof of visa status, such as a Refugee Status Grant letter or TPV documentation.  
8. **Await Decision & Confirm Acceptance** – Once approved, confirm your scholarship acceptance and finalize your enrollment.
