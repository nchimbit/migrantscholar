Migrant students can apply for fully funded master's programs at the University of London, with a funding amount of up to £18,000, through various scholarships such as the University of London Scholarships. The scholarships are open to migrant students, including refugees and asylum seekers, who meet the eligibility criteria.

## Who Qualifies
* Migrant students with a valid visa category, including Tier 4 (General) student visa
* Refugees with a valid protection claim or a granted refugee status
* Asylum seekers with a pending asylum claim
* Students who meet the academic eligibility criteria, including a Bachelor's degree with a minimum grade of 2:1 or equivalent
* Students who meet the language proficiency requirements, including an IELTS score of at least 6.5 or equivalent

## What the Scholarship Covers
The fully funded master's programs at the University of London cover:
* Tuition fees up to £18,000
* Living costs, including accommodation and food, up to £11,000
* Travel costs, including flights and visa fees, up to £1,000
* Other expenses, including books and equipment, up to £1,000

## How to Apply
1. Check the eligibility criteria and ensure you meet the requirements.
2. Choose your master's program and university.
3. Apply for the scholarship through the University of London website.
4. Submit your application, including all required documents.
5. Pay the application fee, if required.
6. Wait for the application to be processed and a decision to be made.
7. Receive and review the scholarship offer, if successful.
8. Accept the scholarship offer and complete any additional requirements.
