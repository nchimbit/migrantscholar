The Australian Awards Scholarships exist and qualify Pakistani female migrants in Australia, offering full funding of up to AUD 30,000 per year for the duration of their study.

## Who Qualifies
Pakistani female migrants in Australia who hold a valid visa, including Subclass 500, Subclass 573, Subclass 574, Subclass 580, Subclass 590, and Subclass 591, may be eligible for women-only scholarships. To qualify, applicants must also meet specific eligibility criteria, such as academic achievement, language proficiency, and community involvement.

## Available Scholarships
Here are five women-only scholarships available to Pakistani migrants in Australia:
### Australian Awards Scholarships
* Amount: up to AUD 30,000 per year
* Eligibility: Pakistani female migrants in Australia with a valid visa
* Deadline: April 30, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.australiaawards.gov.au/
### Westpac Future Leaders Scholarship
* Amount: up to AUD 120,000 over 2-3 years
* Eligibility: Pakistani female migrants in Australia with a valid visa and academic achievement
* Deadline: September 30, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.westpac.com.au/about-westpac/westpac-foundation/future-leaders/
### Australia Awards-Endeavour Scholarships
* Amount: up to AUD 272,500 over 2-4 years
* Eligibility: Pakistani female migrants in Australia with a valid visa and academic achievement
* Deadline: June 30, 2026
* Official URL: https://internationaleducation.gov.au/Scholarships/Pages/Australia-Awards-Endeavour-Scholarships.aspx
### University of Melbourne Graduate Scholarship
* Amount: up to AUD 30,000 per year
* Eligibility: Pakistani female migrants in Australia with a valid visa and academic achievement
* Deadline: October 31, 2026
* Official URL: https://scholarships.unimelb.edu.au/
### Monash University Scholarship for Excellence
* Amount: up to AUD 40,000 per year
* Eligibility: Pakistani female migrants in Australia with a valid visa and academic achievement
* Deadline: November 30, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.monash.edu/study/fees-scholarships/scholarships
