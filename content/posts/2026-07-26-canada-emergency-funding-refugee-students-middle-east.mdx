The World University Service of Canada (WUSC) Student Refugee Program exists and qualifies refugee students from the Middle East with up to $15,000 in funding for their studies in Canada. 
As a Middle Eastern refugee student, you may be eligible for various emergency funding opportunities in Canada. To find the right scholarship, you should first check your eligibility based on your visa category, which may include but is not limited to, [refugee claimants](https://migrantscholar.com/by-eligibility/refugees), [protected persons](https://migrantscholar.com/by-eligibility/refugees), or [convention refugees](https://migrantscholar.com/by-eligibility/refugees).

## Who Qualifies
To qualify for emergency funding in Canada as a Middle Eastern refugee student, you typically need to hold a valid Canadian study permit and have a certain visa category. The most common visa categories that qualify for funding include [Protected Persons](https://migrantscholar.com/by-eligibility/refugees) and [Refugee Claimants](https://migrantscholar.com/by-eligibility/refugees). Some scholarships also accept students with a [Temporary Resident Visa](https://migrantscholar.com/by-eligibility/temporary-resident) or a [ Convention Refugee ](https://migrantscholar.com/by-eligibility/refugees).

## Application Steps
To apply for these scholarships and emergency funding opportunities, follow these steps:
1. Check your eligibility for each scholarship
2. Gather required documents, such as your study permit and [proof of refugee status](https://migrantscholar.com/by-eligibility/refugees)
3. Review the application deadlines and requirements
4. Prepare a personal statement or essay as required by each scholarship
5. Submit your application package, which may include transcripts and letters of recommendation
6. Follow up with the institution or scholarship provider to confirm receipt of your application
7. Wait for the review and decision process
8. If selected, accept the award and review any conditions or requirements
