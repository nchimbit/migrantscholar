The University of Manchester, UK offers fully funded healthcare scholarships for women migrants. Eligible women migrants can receive up to £30,000 in funding to pursue a Master's degree in healthcare. The scholarship is designed to support women migrants in their pursuit of higher education in the healthcare field.

## Who Qualifies
* Women migrants holding a valid visa category of Refugee, Humanitarian Protection, or Asylum Seeker
* Women migrants with a Bachelor's degree in a relevant field, such as nursing, midwifery, or public health
* Women migrants with a strong academic record and a passion for pursuing a career in healthcare
* Women migrants who can demonstrate a commitment to improving healthcare outcomes in their communities

## What the Scholarship Covers
The fully funded healthcare scholarship for women migrants covers the following expenses:
* Tuition fees: up to £18,000 per year
* Living expenses: up to £12,000 per year
* Travel expenses: up to £1,000 per year
* Research expenses: up to £2,000 per year
* Other expenses, such as accommodation and equipment, may also be covered

## How to Apply
1. Check the official scholarship website for the latest information and eligibility criteria
2. Review the application requirements and prepare the necessary documents
