The Commonwealth Scholarship, which is worth between £1,197 and £1,866 per month, is available to Turkish nationals pursuing PhD programs in environmental science in the UK, with the goal of promoting sustainable development and addressing environmental challenges.

## Who Qualifies
Turkish nationals who hold a valid passport and have been offered a place at a PhD program in environmental science at a UK university may be eligible for the Commonwealth Scholarship. To be eligible, applicants must also meet certain visa categories, including the Student visa route, which requires them to demonstrate English language proficiency and have sufficient funds to cover living expenses. Other eligible visa categories include the Graduate visa route and the Tier 5 visa route.

## Scholarships
The following scholarships are available to Turkish nationals pursuing PhD programs in environmental science in the UK:
### Commonwealth Scholarship
* Amount: £1,197-£1,866 per month
* Eligibility: Turkish nationals with a first-class honours degree and an unconditional offer from a UK university
* Deadline: Varies
* Official URL: https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk
### British Chevening Scholarship
* Amount: Full tuition fees and a monthly stipend
* Eligibility: Turkish nationals with at least two years of work experience and a first-class honours degree
* Deadline: November
* Official URL: https://www.chevening.org
### Turkish Ministry of National Education Scholarship
* Amount: Full tuition fees and a monthly stipend
* Eligibility: Turkish nationals with a first-class honours degree and an unconditional offer from a UK university
* Deadline: June
* Official URL: https://www.meb.gov.tr
### UK Research and Innovation Scholarship
* Amount: £15,609 per year
* Eligibility: Turkish nationals with a first-class honours degree and an unconditional offer from a UK university
* Deadline: January
* Official URL: https://www.ukri.org
### NERC Scholarship
* Amount: £15,609 per year
* Eligibility: Turkish nationals with a first-class honours degree and an unconditional offer from a UK university
* Deadline: January
* Official URL: https://nerc.ukri.org

To apply for these scholarships, follow these steps:
1. Choose a PhD program in environmental science at a UK university and apply for admission.
2. Check the eligibility criteria for each scholarship and make sure you meet the requirements.
3. Gather all required documents, including transcripts, certificates, and language proficiency test results.
4. Submit your application through the online portal or by post, depending on the scholarship.
5. Wait for the confirmation email and follow up with the scholarship provider if necessary.
6. If shortlisted, prepare for an interview or assessment.
7. Receive the award letter and accept the offer.
8. Apply for a Student visa and travel to the UK to start your PhD program.

The following documents are required for the application:
* Passport
* Transcripts
* Certificates
* Language proficiency test results (e.g. IELTS)
* CV
* Personal statement
* Reference letters
