# UK Aid
The Theodora Iglesias Trust Scholarship exists and qualifies female asylum seekers in the UK pursuing healthcare careers with funding of up to £500.

## Who Qualifies
To qualify for emergency funding for female asylum seekers in the UK pursuing healthcare careers, applicants must have a valid visa category such as refugee status, humanitarian protection, or limited leave to remain. They must also be enrolled or intend to enroll in a healthcare-related course at a UK university.
