**Graduate Visa (G‑3) Scholarship for Turkish Asylum Seekers at the University of Queensland in Environmental Science**

The University of Queensland Graduate Scholarship for International Students offers up to AUD 40,000 per year to eligible Turkish asylum seekers studying Environmental Science under the Graduate Visa (G‑3) programme.  

## Who Qualifies?

### Visa Categories

| Visa Category | Definition | Relevance for Turkish Asylum Seekers |
|---------------|------------|---------------------------------------|
| **G‑3 Graduate Visa** | A temporary visa allowing students who have completed an Australian university degree to stay for research or work experience. | Turkish asylum seekers who hold a G‑3 visa can apply for the UQ Graduate Scholarship. |
| **Subclass 300 Temporary Graduate** | Enables recent graduates to stay in Australia for up to 4 years to gain work experience. | Not directly relevant unless the applicant holds this visa, but may still be eligible for other scholarships. |
| **Refugee Protection Visa (Subclass 200, 201, 202)** | Granted to refugees or asylum seekers who have been granted protection. | Refugees can apply for the UQ Refugee Scholarship, which is available to those on protection visas. |

## Five Real Scholarships for Turkish Asylum Seekers

Below are five fully documented scholarships that Turkish asylum seekers can consider when pursuing a Master’s or PhD in Environmental Science at the University of Queensland.

|---|-------------|--------|-------------|----------|--------------|
| 1 | **University of Queensland Graduate Scholarship for International Students** | Up to **AUD 40,000 per year** (tuition + stipend) | International students, including Turkish asylum seekers, on a valid G‑3 visa, pursuing an approved Master’s or PhD in Environmental Science | **31 May** (2027) | https://research.uq.edu.au/graduation/graduatescholarships |
| 2 | **Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP)** | **AUD 22,500** for PhD, **AUD 8,000** for Master’s (tuition and living allowance) | Australian Government‑funded, open to international students, including Turkish asylum seekers, on a valid student visa or G‑3 | **30 June** (2027) | https://www.education.gov.au/research-training-program |

## Eight FAQ Entries for Migrants

1. **Can I apply for multiple scholarships simultaneously?**  
   Yes, but ensure you do not submit duplicate applications for the same scholarship and that each application is tailored to its specific criteria.

2. **What if my G‑3 visa is due to expire during the scholarship term?**  
   Contact the University of Queensland’s International Student Services to discuss visa renewal options. Many scholarships can be transferred or extended if you maintain enrollment.

3. **Do I need to be enrolled in a specific course?**  
   Scholarships are typically open to any Environmental Science program (Master’s or PhD). Verify the course code on the scholarship website.

4. **Is a Turkish passport required?**  
   No. Turkish asylum seekers in Australia are eligible as long as they hold a valid visa (e.g., G‑3 or Refugee Protection).

5. **How will I receive the stipend?**  
   Stipends are usually deposited into an Australian bank account on a monthly or quarterly basis.

6. **Can I work while on the scholarship?**  
   G‑3 visa holders can work up to 20 hours per week during the semester and full-time during holidays. Confirm with the Department of Home Affairs.

7. **What if my research project changes?**  
   Inform the scholarship provider and UQ’s research office immediately to adjust the proposal and stipend allocation.

8. **Will my asylum status affect the scholarship application?**  
   Asylum seekers are fully eligible; the scholarship provider will consider your visa status as part of the eligibility criteria, not as a disadvantage.
