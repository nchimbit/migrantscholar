# UK Scholarships
The Chevening Scholarship is available to Turkish women in healthcare, offering full funding of up to £18,000 for a master's degree in the UK. 
This prestigious award enables talented individuals to pursue higher education and career advancement in the field of healthcare. 
To qualify for the Chevening Scholarship, applicants must be Turkish citizens, have at least two years of work experience, and meet the English language proficiency requirements. 
The scholarship covers tuition fees, living expenses, and travel costs, making it an excellent opportunity for [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded) seekers.

## Who Qualifies
Turkish women in healthcare who wish to apply for the Chevening Scholarship must meet specific eligibility criteria, including:
having a bachelor's degree from a recognized institution
meeting the English language proficiency requirements
having at least two years of work experience in the healthcare sector
being a citizen of Turkey
having a valid passport
meeting the visa requirements, including the Tier 4 student visa category.
The scholarship is open to individuals who are passionate about pursuing a career in healthcare and are willing to return to Turkey after completing their studies.
