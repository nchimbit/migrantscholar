## Who Qualifies
Asylum seekers in the UK, including those of Pakistani nationality, may be eligible for emergency funding under the following visa categories:
* Section 95 of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999: support for asylum seekers
* Section 98 of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999: support for failed asylum seekers
* Section 4 of the Immigration and Asylum Act 1999: support for destitute asylum seekers
Eligibility criteria include:
* Being an asylum seeker or having had an asylum claim refused
* Having no other means of supporting yourself
* Being destitute or at risk of becoming destitute

## What the Scholarship Covers
The emergency funding provided by the UK Government's Asylum Support scheme covers essential living costs, including:
* Accommodation: funding for housing, including rent or other accommodation costs
* Food and toiletries: a weekly allowance to cover basic needs
* Healthcare: access to NHS healthcare services
* Other essential costs: funding for other essential items, such as clothing and travel costs

## How to Apply
To apply for emergency funding under the Asylum Support scheme, follow these steps:
1. Check your eligibility: ensure you meet the eligibility criteria outlined above
2. Gather required documents: see the Required Documents section below
3. Contact the National Asylum Support Service (NASS): phone or email the NASS to request an application form
4. Complete the application form: fill in the form accurately and thoroughly
5. Submit the application form: return the completed form to the NASS
6. Wait for a decision: the NASS will review your application and make a decision
7. Receive funding: if your application is successful, you will receive emergency funding to cover essential living costs
8. Review and revise: your funding will be reviewed regularly, and you may need to provide updated information to continue receiving support

## Required Documents
To apply for emergency funding under the Asylum Support scheme, you will need to provide the following documents:
* Valid passport
* Asylum application documentation
* Proof of address
* Proof of income (if applicable)
* Proof of destitution or risk of destitution
* Other supporting documentation (e.g., medical evidence, police reports)
