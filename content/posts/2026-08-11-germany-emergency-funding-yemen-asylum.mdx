The DAAD Scholarship qualifies asylum seekers from Yemen, with funding up to €750, to support their studies in Germany. As an asylum seeker from Yemen in Germany, it can be challenging to access education due to financial constraints. However, there are several emergency funding options available to support your academic pursuits. 

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for emergency funding as an asylum seeker from Yemen in Germany, you typically need to hold a specific visa category, such as a residence permit for asylum seekers (Aufenthaltsgestattung) or a temporary residence permit (Aufenthaltstitel). The specific requirements may vary depending on the scholarship, but generally, you should be:
- Recognized as an asylum seeker or refugee
- Holding a valid residence permit
- Enrolled in or accepted to a German university
- Meeting the language requirements (German or English, depending on the program)

## Scholarships
As an asylum seeker from Yemen, you can explore the following real scholarships:
### DAAD Scholarship
- Amount: Up to €750
- Eligibility: Asylum seekers and refugees enrolled in German universities
- Deadline: Varies by program
- Official URL: https://www.daad.de/en/
### Deutschlandstipendium
- Amount: €300 per month
- Eligibility: Students, including asylum seekers, demonstrating excellent academic performance
- Deadline: Varies by university
- Official URL: https://www.deutschlandstipendium.de/en/
### Friedrich Ebert Foundation Scholarship
- Amount: Up to €750
- Eligibility: International students, including asylum seekers, with a strong academic record
- Deadline: October 31st and April 30th
- Official URL: https://www.fes.de/en/
### Heinrich Böll Foundation Scholarship
- Amount: Up to €750 for master's and up to €1,200 for PhD
- Eligibility: International students, including asylum seekers, pursuing a master's or PhD
- Deadline: March 1st and September 1st
- Official URL: https://www.boell.de/en
### Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung Scholarship
- Amount: Up to €1,200
- Eligibility: International students, including asylum seekers, demonstrating academic excellence and leadership skills
- Deadline: January 15th and July 15th
- Official URL: https://www.kas.de/en/
