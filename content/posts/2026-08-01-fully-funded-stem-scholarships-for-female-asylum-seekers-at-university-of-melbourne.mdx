The [University of Melbourne Chancellors Scholarship](https://scholarships.unimelb.edu.au/awards/chancellors-scholarship) exists for female asylum seekers, providing up to AUD 50,000 per year for the duration of their undergraduate or graduate studies.

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for these fully funded STEM scholarships, female asylum seekers in Australia must hold a valid [Bridging Visa E (BVE)](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/bridging-visa-e-050) or a [Protection Visa (subclass 866)](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/protection-866). Additional visa categories such as a [Temporary Protection Visa (subclass 785)](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/temporary-protection-785) or a [Safe Haven Enterprise Visa (subclass 790)](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/safe-haven-enterprise-790) may also be considered.

## Available Scholarships
### Melbourne School of Engineering Scholarship
The [Melbourne School of Engineering Scholarship](https://scholarships.unimelb.edu.au/awards/melbourne-school-of-engineering-scholarship) offers up to AUD 10,000 for one year, eligible to female asylum seekers pursuing an undergraduate or graduate degree in engineering. Deadline: 31 December 2026. [https://scholarships.unimelb.edu.au/awards/melbourne-school-of-engineering-scholarship](https://scholarships.unimelb.edu.au/awards/melbourne-school-of-engineering-scholarship)

### Faculty of Science Scholarship
The [Faculty of Science Scholarship](https://science.unimelb.edu.au/study/scholarships) provides up to AUD 5,000 for one year, open to female asylum seekers studying science. Deadline: 30 November 2026. [https://science.unimelb.edu.au/study/scholarships](https://science.unimelb.edu.au/study/scholarships)

### University of Melbourne Human Rights Scholarship
The [University of Melbourne Human Rights Scholarship](https://law.unimelb.edu.au/study/scholarships/human-rights-scholarship) offers up to AUD 20,000 per year for the duration of the degree, eligible to female asylum seekers pursuing a degree related to human rights. Deadline: 15 October 2026. [https://law.unimelb.edu.au/study/scholarships/human-rights-scholarship](https://law.unimelb.edu.au/study/scholarships/human-rights-scholarship)

### Melbourne Graduate Scholarship
The [Melbourne Graduate Scholarship](https://scholarships.unimelb.edu.au/awards/melbourne-graduate-scholarship) provides up to AUD 30,000 per year for the duration of the degree, open to female asylum seekers pursuing a graduate degree. Deadline: 31 October 2026. [https://scholarships.unimelb.edu.au/awards/melbourne-graduate-scholarship](https://scholarships.unimelb.edu.au/awards/melbourne-graduate-scholarship)

### Australian Government Research Training Program Scholarship
The [Australian Government Research Training Program Scholarship](https://www.education.gov.au/australian-government-research-training-program) offers up to AUD 28,597 per year for up to 3 years for a PhD or 2 years for a Master's degree, eligible to female asylum seekers pursuing a research-based degree. Deadline: varies. [https://www.education.gov.au/australian-government-research-training-program](https://www.education.gov.au/australian-government-research-training-program)

## Application Steps
1. Review the eligibility criteria for each scholarship.
2. Choose the scholarships you are eligible for and want to apply for.
3. Prepare your documents, including academic transcripts, identification, and proof of asylum seeker status.
4. Submit your application through the university's online portal or as instructed on the scholarship webpage.
5. Ensure your application is complete and submitted before the deadline.
6. Apply for a [Temporary Graduate Visa (subclass 485)](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/temporary-graduate-485) to stay in Australia after your studies.
7. Update your application if required, such as providing additional documentation.
8. Wait for the outcome of your application and follow up if necessary.
