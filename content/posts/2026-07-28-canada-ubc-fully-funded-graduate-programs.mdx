The University of British Columbia Award, which qualifies international students, offers up to $30,000 per year for fully funded graduate programs. 

## Who Qualifies
International students who hold a valid study permit, or are applying for one, under the Student Direct Stream or other visa categories, are eligible to apply for fully funded graduate programs at the University of British Columbia in Canada. The university welcomes students from diverse backgrounds, including [refugees](https://migrantscholar.com/by-eligibility/refugees) and students with [disabilities](https://migrantscholar.com/by-eligibility/students-with-disabilities). 

## Available Scholarships
The following are some fully funded graduate programs available to international students at the University of British Columbia:

### University of British Columbia Graduate Scholarship
The University of British Columbia Graduate Scholarship offers up to $18,000 per year for two years, and is eligible to international students pursuing a Master's or PhD degree. The application deadline is January 15, 2027. For more information, visit [https://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/university-brITish-columbia-graduate-scholarship](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/university-british-columbia-graduate-scholarship).

### Four Year Doctoral Fellowship
The Four Year Doctoral Fellowship offers up to $18,000 per year for four years, and is eligible to international students pursuing a PhD degree. The application deadline is January 15, 2027. For more information, visit [https://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/four-year-doctoral-fellowship](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/four-year-doctoral-fellowship).

### Killam Awards for International Students
The Killam Awards for International Students offers up to $30,000 per year for two years, and is eligible to international students pursuing a Master's or PhD degree. The application deadline is January 15, 2027. For more information, visit [https://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/killam-award-for-international-students](https://www.grad.ubc.ca/awards/killam-award-for-international-students).

### Pierre Elliott Trudeau Foundation Scholarship
The Pierre Elliott Trudeau Foundation Scholarship offers up to $60,000 per year for three years, and is eligible to international students pursuing a PhD degree. The application deadline is December 21, 2026. For more information, visit [https://www.trudeaufoundation.ca/en/programs/scholarships](https://www.trudeaufoundation.ca/en/programs/scholarships).

### Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarship
The Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarship offers up to $50,000 per year for three years, and is eligible to international students pursuing a PhD degree. The application deadline is November 1, 2026. For more information, visit [https://vanier.gc.ca/en/home-accueil.html](https://vanier.gc.ca/en/home-accueil.html).

## Application Process
To apply for fully funded graduate programs at the University of British Columbia, follow these steps:
1. Check your eligibility for the desired program and scholarship.
2. Review the [scholarship deadlines](https://migrantscholar.com/deadlines) and application requirements.
3. Prepare your application documents, including transcripts, letters of recommendation, and a personal statement.
4. Submit your application through the university's online portal.
5. Upload your supporting documents, including language proficiency test results and passport copy.
6. Pay the application fee, which is currently $100 for international students.
7. Wait for the application review process, which can take several months.
8. Receive an admission decision, which may include a scholarship offer.

## Documents Checklist
To apply for fully funded graduate programs at the University of British Columbia, you will need to submit the following documents:
* Transcripts from all previous institutions attended
* Letters of recommendation from academic or professional references
* Personal statement or research proposal
* Language proficiency test results (e.g. TOEFL or IELTS)
* Passport copy
* Study permit or visa application (if applicable)

## Frequently Asked Questions
1. What is the application deadline for fully funded graduate programs at the University of British Columbia?
The application deadline varies depending on the program and scholarship, but most deadlines fall between November and January.
2. Can I apply for multiple scholarships at the same time?
Yes, you can apply for multiple scholarships at the same time, but you can only receive one award.
3. Do I need to take an English language proficiency test to apply for a graduate program at the University of British Columbia?
Yes, all international students must demonstrate English language proficiency through a test such as TOEFL or IELTS.
4. Can I work part-time while studying at the University of British Columbia?
Yes, international students with a valid study permit can work part-time on or off campus.
5. How do I apply for a study permit or visa to study in Canada?
You can apply for a study permit or visa through the Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) website.
6. What is the cost of living in Vancouver, British Columbia?

7. Can I bring my family with me to Canada while I study?
Yes, international students with a valid study permit can bring their family members to Canada as dependents.
8. How do I find accommodation in Vancouver, British Columbia?
You can find accommodation in Vancouver through the University of British Columbia's housing service or through private rental websites.
