The Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarship is available to Turkish nationals, providing a fully funded Master's program in Canada with a funding amount of $50,000 per year. Turkish students who are pursuing a Master's degree in a Canadian university can apply for this scholarship, which aims to attract top talent from around the world. The scholarship is awarded for three years and is valued at $150,000.

## Who Qualifies
* Turkish nationals with a valid passport
* Candidates who are pursuing a Master's degree in a Canadian university
* Students who have achieved a first-class average in their previous two years of study
* Candidates who are enrolled in a doctoral program and have completed no more than 20 months of doctoral studies as of May 1, 2027
* Students who have not already received a doctoral-level scholarship or fellowship from CIHR, NSERC, or SSHRC to undertake or complete a doctoral program

## What the Scholarship Covers
The Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarship provides a funding amount of $50,000 per year for three years, which covers the following expenses:
* Tuition fees: The scholarship covers the full tuition fees for the Master's program, which can range from $8,000 to $14,000 per year, depending on the university.
* Living expenses: The scholarship provides $50,000 per year, which can be used to cover living expenses, such as accommodation, food, and transportation.
* Research expenses: The scholarship also covers research expenses, such as travel and equipment costs, up to $10,000 per year.
* Health insurance: The scholarship provides health insurance coverage for the duration of the program.
