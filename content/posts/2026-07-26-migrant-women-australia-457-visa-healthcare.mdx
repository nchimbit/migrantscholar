## Who Qualifies
* Female migrants on a 457 visa
* Migrant women who have been living in Australia for at least 12 months
* Migrant women who are currently enrolled in a healthcare-related course in Australia
* Migrant women who have a strong passion for pursuing a career in the healthcare industry
* Migrant women who meet the eligibility criteria for the specific scholarship

## What the Scholarship Covers
The AHPRA scholarship covers a range of expenses, including:
* Tuition fees for a healthcare-related course
* Living expenses, such as accommodation and food
* Travel expenses, such as flights and transportation
* Equipment and material expenses, such as textbooks and medical equipment
* Professional development expenses, such as conference fees and training courses
