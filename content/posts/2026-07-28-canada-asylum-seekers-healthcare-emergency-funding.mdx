The Pierre Elliott Trudeau Foundation Scholarship exists and qualifies asylum seekers, with funding of up to $80,000 over three years, pursuing healthcare careers in Canada. As an asylum seeker, you may be eligible for various scholarships to support your journey in the healthcare field. 
To find the best [Canada scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/countries/Canada) you must first determine your eligibility. 

## Who Qualifies
To qualify for emergency funding as an asylum seeker in Canada, you typically need to hold a valid refugee claimant document or have been granted refugee status. Common visa categories for asylum seekers in Canada include:
- Claimant
- Protected Person
- Convention Refugee

## Scholarships
### Ontario Graduate Scholarship
The Ontario Graduate Scholarship offers $15,000 per year, with eligibility limited to students pursuing graduate studies in Ontario. Deadline varies, check the [official University of Toronto website](https://www.sgs.utoronto.ca/funding/ontario-graduate-scholarship-ogs/).
### Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarships
The Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarships provides $50,000 per year, with eligibility open to international students pursuing PhD studies in Canada. Deadline is November 1, check the [official Government of Canada website](https://www.canada.ca/en/research-funding-programs/vanier.html).
### Banting Postdoctoral Fellowships
The Banting Postdoctoral Fellowships offers $70,000 per year, with eligibility limited to postdoctoral researchers. Deadline is October 1, check the [official Government of Canada website](https://www.banting.fellowships-bourses.gc.ca/en/home-accueil.html).
### CIHR Fellowship
The CIHR Fellowship provides up to $60,000 per year, with eligibility open to students pursuing health research in Canada. Deadline varies, check the [official CIHR website](https://cihr-irsc.gc.ca/e/51356.html).
### Queen Elizabeth II Graduate Scholarship
The Queen Elizabeth II Graduate Scholarship offers $15,000 per year, with eligibility limited to students pursuing graduate studies in Ontario. Deadline varies, check the [official University of Toronto website](https://www.sgs.utoronto.ca/funding/queen-elizabeth-ii-graduate-scholarship-ontario-qqes/).
