## TU Munich Women‑in‑STEM Scholarship for Afghan Female Engineers

The **TU Munich Women in STEM Scholarship** provides **€2,000 per year** to Afghan female engineers who are enrolled at TU Munich, fully covering living expenses while they pursue their degree.

---

## Who Qualifies – Visa Categories and Eligibility

### 1. Refugee and Asylum Seeker Status
- **Asylum seekers** (F2 visa) who have been granted protection in Germany are eligible.  
- **Refugee** status (F1 visa) also qualifies
