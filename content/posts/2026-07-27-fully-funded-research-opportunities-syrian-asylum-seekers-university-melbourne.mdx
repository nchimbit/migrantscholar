The University of Melbourne, Australia offers fully funded research opportunities to Syrian asylum seekers, providing a funding amount of AU$32,400 per year for up to 3.5 years. To be eligible, applicants must hold a valid Australian visa and meet specific academic and language requirements. The scholarship is designed to support asylum seekers in pursuing higher education and research in their field of study.

## Who Qualifies
* Hold a valid Australian visa, including but not limited to:
  * Permanent Protection visa (subclass 866)
  * Temporary Protection visa (subclass 785)
  * Safe Haven Enterprise visa (subclass 790)
* Be a Syrian national
* Meet the academic and language requirements for the course
* Not have already completed a research degree at the same level or higher

## What the Scholarship Covers
The University of Melbourne Research Scholarship provides a funding package that includes:
* A living allowance of AU$32,400 per year, paid fortnightly
* A relocation allowance of up to AU$2,000 for students moving from interstate or overseas
* A thesis allowance of up to AU$2,000 for research expenses
* Full fee remission for up to 3.5 years
* Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) for international students

## How to Apply
1. Check the eligibility criteria and ensure you meet all requirements
2. Review the research areas and find a supervisor who is willing to supervise your research
3. Prepare your application, including all required documents
4. Submit your application online through the University's website
5. Wait for the application to be processed and reviewed
6. Receive an offer and accept the scholarship
7. Enroll in the course and commence your research
8. Maintain satisfactory academic progress and comply with the terms and conditions of the scholarship

## Required Documents
* Valid Australian visa
* Proof of Syrian nationality
* Academic transcripts and certificates
* English language proficiency test results (e.g. IELTS)
* Research proposal
* Supervisor's statement of support
* CV and personal statement
