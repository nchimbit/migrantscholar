2. **Oxford-Weidenfeld and Hoffmann Scholarships**: £17,668 per year, eligible for Turkish migrants, deadline January 2027, [https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/fees-and-funding/graduate-scholarships](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/fees-and-funding/graduate-scholarships)
