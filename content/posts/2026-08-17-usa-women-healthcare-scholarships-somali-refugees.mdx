## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for women-only healthcare scholarships, Somali refugee women in the USA typically need to hold a valid visa, such as F-1, J-1, or asylum status. They must also meet specific requirements, such as being enrolled in a US university, having a strong academic record, and demonstrating financial need. Visa categories that may be eligible include:
- F-1 student visa
- J-1 exchange visitor visa
- Asylum status
- Refugee status
