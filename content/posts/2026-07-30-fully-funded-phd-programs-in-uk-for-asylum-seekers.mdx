The Commonwealth Scholarship Commission in the UK offers the Commonwealth PhD Scholarship, which is a fully funded programme providing up to £1,236 per month for asylum seekers and refugees pursuing a PhD in healthcare in the UK.

## Who Qualifies
Asylum seekers in the UK who hold a visa under the categories of Refugee, Humanitarian Protection, or Discretionary Leave to Remain may be eligible for these scholarships. Additionally, individuals with Limited Leave to Remain or those who have been granted Leave to Remain under Article 3 of the European Convention on Human Rights may also qualify. 

## Top 5 Fully Funded PhD Programs in Healthcare
### Wellcome Trust International Master's Fellowships
* Amount: Up to £120,000 for 30 months
* Eligibility: Asylum seekers and refugees with a bachelor's degree in a healthcare-related field
* Deadline: Check the [official website](https://wellcome.org/grant-funding/schemes/international-masters-fellowships)
### Commonwealth PhD Scholarship 
* Amount: Up to £1,236 per month
* Eligibility: Asylum seekers and refugees with a master's degree in a healthcare-related field
* Deadline: Check the [official website](https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/scholarships/commonwealth-phd-scholarships-for-least-developed-countries-and-fragile-states/)
### British Council GREAT Scholarships
* Amount: Up to £10,000 for one year
* Eligibility: Asylum seekers and refugees with a bachelor's degree in a healthcare-related field
* Deadline: Check the [official website](https://www.britishcouncil.org/study-uk/scholarships/great-scholarships)
### UK Government Chevening Scholarships
* Amount: Full tuition fee waiver and a monthly stipend
* Eligibility: Asylum seekers and refugees with a bachelor's degree in a healthcare-related field
* Deadline: Check the [official website](https://www.chevening.org/scholarships/)
### NIHR Fellowships 
* Amount: Up to £250,000 for 3 years
* Eligibility: Asylum seekers and refugees with a master's degree in a healthcare-related field
* Deadline: Check the [official website](https://www.nihr.ac.uk/funding-and-support/funding-for-training-and-career-development/fellowships/)
