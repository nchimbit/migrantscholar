University of Melbourne Scholarships
The University of Melbourne offers fully funded healthcare scholarships to Indian migrants, providing up to AUD 110,000 per year for the duration of their studies. These scholarships are available for Indian migrants who are pursuing a Master's or PhD degree in healthcare at the University of Melbourne. The scholarship covers full tuition fees, living expenses, and other study-related costs.

## Who Qualifies
* Indian migrants holding a valid Australian visa, including but not limited to:
  * Skilled Independent visa (subclass 189)
  * Skilled Nominated visa (subclass 190)
  * Skilled Regional (Provisional) visa (subclass 489)
  * Student visa (subclass 500)
* Indian migrants who have applied for a visa and are awaiting a decision
* Indian migrants who are currently residing in Australia and are pursuing a Master's or PhD degree in healthcare at the University of Melbourne
* Candidates who meet the University of Melbourne's academic and language proficiency Requirements

## What the Scholarship Covers
The Melbourne Graduate Scholarship provides up to AUD 110,000 per year for the duration of the student's studies, covering:
* Full tuition fees for the Master's or PhD program
* Living expenses, including accommodation and food
* Other study-related costs, including books, equipment, and travel
* Health insurance for international students
* Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) for international students

## How to Apply
1. Check the eligibility criteria and ensure you meet the requirements
2. Apply for admission to the University of Melbourne's Master's or PhD program in healthcare
3. Submit your application for the Melbourne Graduate Scholarship
4. Provide all required documents, including academic transcripts, language proficiency test results, and proof of visa status
5. Complete the online application form and submit it before the deadline
6. Receive and review the scholarship offer, including the terms and conditions
7. Accept the scholarship offer and complete any additional requirements
8. Receive the scholarship payment and commence your studies at the University of Melbourne

## Required Documents
* Academic transcripts and degree certificates
* Language proficiency test results (IELTS or TOEFL)
* Proof of visa status ( Australian visa or proof of application)
* Proof of health insurance (Overseas Student Health Cover)
* Passport and identification documents
* Personal statement and research proposal (for PhD applicants)
