Afghan refugees in Australia can apply for the Australia Awards Scholarships, which provide fully funded opportunities to pursue higher education in STEM fields, with a funding amount of up to AU$30,000 per year. The scholarships are designed to support refugees in achieving their academic goals and contributing to their communities. Please check the official scholarship website for the latest information on the funding amount and eligibility criteria.

## Who Qualifies
* Hold a valid Australian visa, such as a Permanent Humanitarian Visa or a Temporary Protection Visa
* Be an Afghan refugee or have Afghan nationality
* Meet the specific eligibility criteria for the Australia Awards Scholarships, including academic requirements and English language proficiency
* Demonstrate a commitment to contributing to their community and country of origin
* Not be a citizen of Australia or have permanent residence in Australia

## What the Scholarship Covers
The Australia Awards Scholarships provide a comprehensive funding package that covers:
* Tuition fees: up to AU$30,000 per year
* Living allowance: a stipend to cover living expenses, such as accommodation, food, and transportation
* Health insurance: Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) for the duration of the scholarship
* Travel costs: a one-time payment to cover travel expenses to and from Australia
* Establishment allowance: a one-time payment to cover establishment costs, such as accommodation and study materials

## How to Apply
1. Check the official scholarship website for the latest information on eligibility criteria and application deadlines.
2. Review the application guidelines and requirements, including the necessary documents and English language proficiency.
3. Submit an online application through the Australia Awards Scholarships online portal.
4. Upload all required documents, including academic transcripts, English language test results, and a valid passport.
5. Receive a confirmation email with a unique application ID and instructions on the next steps.
6. Wait for the application to be reviewed and assessed by the scholarship selection committee.
7. Receive an email notification of the application outcome, including any further instructions or requirements.
8. If successful, receive a scholarship offer and complete any additional requirements, such as accepting the offer and providing additional documentation.

## Required Documents
* Valid Australian visa
* Afghan passport or proof of Afghan nationality
* Academic transcripts and certificates
* English language test results (IELTS or TOEFL)
* Proof of residency and community involvement
* Two academic references
* A personal statement outlining academic goals and community contributions
