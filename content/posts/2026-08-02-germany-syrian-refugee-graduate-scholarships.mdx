The Albert Einstein German Academic Refugee Initiative (DAFI) scholarship exists and qualifies Syrian refugees with a funding amount of up to 750 euros per month. This initiative is a part of the [DAAD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/universities/daad) program, which offers various [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-funding/fully-funded) to refugees. 

## Who Qualifies
Syrian refugees with a visa category of asylum seeker, refugee, or subsidiary protection are eligible to apply for these scholarships. The visa categories include but are not limited to, residence permit, EU Blue Card, and settlement permit. They can apply for [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.com/by-eligibility/refugees) in various fields, including STEM. 

## Available Scholarships
### German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD)
* Amount: up to 750 euros per month
* Eligibility: Syrian refugees with a bachelor's degree
* Deadline: varies
* Official URL: https://www.daad.de 
### Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung (KAS)
* Amount: up to 1,200 euros per month
* Eligibility: Syrian refugees with a master's degree
* Deadline: varies
* Official URL: https://www.kas.de 
### Friedrich-Ebert-Stiftung (FES)
* Amount: up to 1,000 euros per month
* Eligibility: Syrian refugees with a PhD
* Deadline: varies
* Official URL: https://www.fes.de 
### Heinrich Böll Foundation
* Amount: up to 1,200 euros per month
* Eligibility: Syrian refugees with a bachelor's or master's degree
* Deadline: varies
* Official URL: https://www.boell.de 
### Friedrich Naumann Foundation
* Amount: up to 1,000 euros per month
* Eligibility: Syrian refugees with a bachelor's or master's degree
* Deadline: varies
* Official URL: https://www.freiheit.org 

These scholarships can be used to pursue [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-level/phd) or other graduate programs in Germany.
