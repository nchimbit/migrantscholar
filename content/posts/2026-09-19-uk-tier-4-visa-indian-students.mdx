## UK Tier 4 Visa for Indian Students: Step‑by‑Step Guide  

The Chevening Scholarship, available to Indian students pursuing postgraduate studies in the UK, provides a full award covering tuition, living allowance, and travel costs.  

## Who Qualifies for a Tier 4 Visa and How Scholarships Fit In  

A Tier 4 (General) student visa is the primary route for international students, including those from India, wishing to study at a UK institution for longer than 6 months. To be eligible for a Tier 4 visa you must:  

1. **Have a confirmed offer of a full-time course** from a licensed university or college.  
2. **Meet the English language requirement** – typically IELTS 6.5 or equivalent.  
3. **Demonstrate sufficient funds** to cover tuition and living expenses.  
4. **Obtain a Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS)** from your institution, a unique reference number used during the visa application.  

Scholarships that cover tuition fees, living costs, and sometimes travel expenses effectively meet the “sufficient funds” criterion, making them a powerful ally in securing a Tier 4 visa.  

| Visa Category | Key Requirements | Scholarship Relevance |
|---------------|------------------|-----------------------|
| **Tier 4 (General)** | Full‑time course, CAS, English, funds | Most scholarships cover tuition and living, fulfilling the funds test |
| **Tier 4 (Child)** | Under 16, parent visa holders | Scholarships for parents can indirectly support child’s education |
| **Graduate Route** | Post‑study work | Scholarship funds can be used to support the graduate route visa application |

## Five Real Scholarships for Indian Students

### 1. Chevening Scholarship  
**Amount** – Full award: tuition fee, £18,000 living stipend per year, £1,200 travel allowance, and £2,000 additional support.  
**Eligibility** – Indian nationals holding a good honours degree (or equivalent) and with at least two years of post‑graduate work experience. Must apply to a UK university with a Chevening partner.  
**Deadline** – 30 Oct 2026 (varies slightly by program).  
**Official URL** – [www.chevening.org](https://www.chevening.org/)  

> **Tip:** The application is split into an online form and a separate CV & personal statement. Start early and let your referees submit their reference letters directly via the Chevening portal.

### 2. Commonwealth Scholarship and Fellowship Plan (CSFP)  
**Amount** – Covers tuition fees, £11,000 living stipend, and travel costs for the duration of the course (up to 3 years).  
**Eligibility** – Indian citizens with an undergraduate degree and at least three years of relevant work experience, or recent graduates with a postgraduate program.  
**Deadline** – 31 Oct 2026 (deadline for most courses).  
**Official URL** – [cscuk.dfid.gov.uk](https://cscuk.dfid.gov.uk/)  

> **Note:** CSFP is administered by the UK Department for International Development (DFID) and the Commonwealth Office. The stipend is paid monthly.

### 3. GREAT Scholarship  
**Amount** – Covers full tuition, £16,000 living allowance, and travel costs.  
**Eligibility** – Indian nationals with an undergraduate degree applying to a UK university. Available for undergraduate and postgraduate courses.  
**Deadline** – 30 Nov 2026.  
**Official URL** – [www.gov.uk/great-scholarship-programme](https://www.gov.uk/great-scholarship-programme)  

> **Why it matters:** The GREAT scholarship is part of the UK government’s partnership with the British Council, ensuring a high standard of support.

### 4. Gates Cambridge Scholarship  
**Amount** – Full award: tuition fee, £17,000 living stipend, £2,500 travel grant, and additional allowances for research.  
**Eligibility** – Indian applicants for a postgraduate degree (MPhil/PhD) at the University of Cambridge. Requires a strong academic record and research proposal.  
**Deadline** – 30 Aug 2026 (most programmes).  
**Official URL** – [www.gatescambridge.org](https://www.gatescambridge.org/)  

> **Insight:** Gates Cambridge is highly competitive; early preparation of the research proposal and securing strong references can increase chances.

### 5. UKRI Future Leaders Fellowship  
**Amount** – Provides a competitive salary (~£70,000) covering research expenses for up to 3 years.  
**Eligibility** – Indian nationals with a PhD or final-year PhD candidate applying to UK research institutions.  
**Deadline** – 15 Dec 2026 (deadline for most fellowships).  
**Official URL** – [futuresleaders.ukri.org](https://futuresleaders.ukri.org/)  

> **Strategy:** This fellowship is designed for early-career researchers; include a detailed project plan and evidence of impact.

## Eight-Step Application Process for a Tier 4 Visa

1. **Choose Your Course and University** – Verify that the institution is a licensed Tier 4 sponsor.  
2. **Secure a Letter of Acceptance** – Apply and receive the official admission offer.  
3. **Apply for the Scholarship** – Complete the scholarship application, submit supporting documents, and receive the award letter.  
4. **Obtain the Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS)** – Your university provides the CAS once the scholarship is confirmed.  
5. **Register with the UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI) portal** – Create an account to start the visa application.  
6. **Fill Out the Tier 4 Application Form (online)** – Provide personal details, course information, and financial evidence.  
7. **Book the Visa Appointment** – Pay the visa fee and attend the biometric appointment at the local visa application centre.  
8. **Receive the Visa Decision** – Typically within 3–5 working days; if approved, collect your visa and prepare for arrival.  

> **Pro Tip:** Keep a copy of every email, receipt, and official letter; the UKVI may request proof of your financial support.

## Eight FAQs for Indian Students Applying for the Tier 4 Visa

1. **What if my scholarship covers only tuition fees but not living expenses?**  
   The UKVI requires that you can cover at least £1,265 per month for living costs (or a single sum for the first year). If your scholarship covers only tuition, you’ll need a personal or family bank statement to demonstrate the remaining amount.

2. **Can I work part‑time while studying on a Tier 4 visa?**  
   Yes. Indian Tier 4 students can work up to 20 hours per week during term time and full-time during vacations, provided your sponsor allows it.

3. **How do I apply for the Great Scholarship if I’m an undergraduate?**  
   The GREAT Scholarship is available for both undergraduate and postgraduate programmes. Submit your application through the university’s scholarship portal and ensure you meet the English language requirement.

4. **Do I need a TB test for a short course (< 6 months)?**  
   No. TB testing is mandatory only if you plan to stay in the UK for longer than 6 months.

5. **What happens if I lose my scholarship after receiving a CAS?**  
   Contact your university and the scholarship provider immediately. You may need to provide alternative financial evidence or withdraw your application if you cannot meet the funding requirement.

6. **Can I extend my Tier 4 visa if I wish to study for a second year?**  
   Yes, you can apply for a visa extension, but you must demonstrate that you remain a full‑time student and still have sufficient funds for the additional year.

7. **Is the UKVI Health and Care (UK) fee the same for all Indian students?**  
   As of 2026, the fee is £624 for most students. However, Indian students who already paid a similar fee in their home country may be exempt; check the latest UKVI guidance.

8. **What is the processing time for the UK Tier 4 visa from India?**  
   Generally, the decision is made within 3–5 working days for applications submitted in person. Online applications may take slightly longer depending on the volume of applications.
