## ANU Women in STEM Scholarship for Iraqi Migrant Women

The Australian National University offers the **ANU Women in STEM Scholarship**, a fully funded award of **AUD 20,000 per year** for Iraqi migrant women with a valid student visa to pursue a Master’s or PhD in STEM disciplines.

### Who Qualifies

| Visa Category | Eligibility |
|---|---|
| **Student Visa (Subclass 500)** | Full-time enrolment in a recognised ANU STEM course; proof of Iraqi citizenship or residency status; valid health insurance (OSHC). |
| **Temporary Graduate Visa (Subclass 485)** | Recent ANU graduate, female, with Iraqi nationality, intending to study or work in STEM for at least 12 months. |
| **Skilled Independent Visa (Subclass 189)** | Iraqi migrant female with a STEM occupation on the ANU list, nominated for a post‑graduate degree. |
| **Humanitarian and Refugee Visa** | Iraqi women who have been granted humanitarian status in Australia and are enrolled in a STEM program at ANU. |
| **Employer Sponsored Visa (Subclass 186/187)** | Iraqi female employee of an Australian STEM organisation, sponsoring a post‑graduate scholarship at ANU. |
