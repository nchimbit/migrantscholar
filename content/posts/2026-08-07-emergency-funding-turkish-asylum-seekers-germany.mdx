The German Turkish Scholarship Programme qualifies Turkish asylum seekers in Germany for up to €10,000 in funding.

## Who Qualifies
Turkish asylum seekers with a valid asylum application, or those with a visa under categories such as a residence permit, asylum seeker permit, or a temporary residence permit, may be eligible for emergency funding. Eligible visa categories include, but are not limited to, the following:
- Asylum seeker permit
- Residence permit
- Temporary residence permit
- Humanitarian visa
- Family reunification visa
- Student visa

## Available Scholarships
Turkish asylum seekers can apply for the following scholarships to help cover living expenses, tuition fees, or other educational costs in Germany.
### DAAD Scholarship
* Amount: €750 per month
* Eligibility: Turkish asylum seekers with a bachelor's degree
* Deadline: September 30, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.daad.de/en/
### Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung Scholarship
* Amount: €1,200 per month
* Eligibility: Turkish asylum seekers with a master's degree
* Deadline: October 15, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.kas.de/en/
### Friedrich-Ebert-Stiftung Scholarship
* Amount: €1,000 per month
* Eligibility: Turkish asylum seekers with a bachelor's degree
* Deadline: August 31, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.fes.de/en/
### Heinrich-Böll-Stiftung Scholarship
* Amount: €1,500 per month
* Eligibility: Turkish asylum seekers with a master's degree
* Deadline: November 1, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.boell.de/en
### Hanns-Seidel-Stiftung Scholarship
* Amount: €900 per month
* Eligibility: Turkish asylum seekers with a bachelor's degree
* Deadline: December 15, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.hss.de/en/
