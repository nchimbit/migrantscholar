# Emergency Aid
The Heinrich Böll Foundation Scholarship qualifies Turkish asylum seekers in Germany for funding of up to €750 per month, and is one of the few organizations providing emergency funding to asylum seekers. Turkish nationals who have been granted asylum in Germany and are currently enrolled in a German university can apply for this scholarship. The Heinrich Böll Foundation Scholarship provides financial assistance to students who are facing financial difficulties and are in need of emergency funding.

## Who Qualifies
Turkish asylum seekers in Germany who hold a valid visa, such as a residence permit or an asylum seeker card, are eligible to apply for emergency funding. The following visa categories qualify for emergency funding: 
- Subsidiary protection 
- Asylum seeker 
- Refugee 
- Residence permit for humanitarian reasons 
- Temporary residence permit
