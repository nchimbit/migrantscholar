DAAD Scholarships
Syrian refugees can apply for fully funded German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD) scholarships, valued at up to €1,200 per month, to study in STEM fields in Germany. The scholarships aim to support Syrian refugees in continuing their higher education. Please check the official scholarship website for the latest information on the funding amount.

## Quick Facts
| Award Name | Funding Amount | Deadline | Eligibility | Study Level | IELTS Required | Official Website |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DAAD Scholarship | up to €1,200 per month | Please check the official scholarship website | Syrian refugees | Master's, PhD | Not always required | [www.daad.de](http://www.daad.de) |

## Who Qualifies
* Syrian refugees with a Bachelor's degree or equivalent in a STEM field
* Applicants with a strong academic record and a clear motivation for studying in Germany
* Refugees with a recognized asylum or refugee status in their current country of residence
* Candidates who have not been residing in Germany for more than 15 months at the time of application
* Language proficiency in English (and German, if applicable) is required, but some programs may not require IELTS

## What the Scholarship Covers
The DAAD scholarship includes:
* A monthly stipend of up to €1,200 for master's students and up to €1,500 for PhD students
* A one-time payment of €1,500 for travel and accommodation expenses
* Health, accident, and personal liability insurance
* A possible additional payment for study and research costs, such as tuition fees, if applicable
* Participation in a pre-academic language course, if necessary

## How to Apply
1. Check the official DAAD website for the latest information on the scholarship program and the application deadline.
2. Choose a suitable course of study in a STEM field at a German university.
3. Review the eligibility criteria and ensure you meet all the requirements.
4. Gather all required documents, including academic certificates, language proficiency test results, and a passport.
5. Fill out the online application form and upload the required documents.
6. Submit your application through the DAAD online portal.
7. Wait for the application to be reviewed and processed by the DAAD selection committee.
8. If selected, you will be contacted by the DAAD with further instructions on the next steps.

## Required Documents
* A completed online application form
* A CV in English or German
* A motivation letter explaining why you want to study in Germany and how the scholarship will help you achieve your goals
* Academic certificates and transcripts, including a Bachelor's degree or equivalent in a STEM field
* Language proficiency test results, such as IELTS or TOEFL (if required)
* A copy of your passport
* Proof of recognized asylum or refugee status in your current country of residence

## Frequently Asked Questions
Q: What is the maximum duration of the DAAD scholarship?
A: The maximum duration of the DAAD scholarship depends on the study program, but it is usually up to 2 years for master's students and up to 3 years for PhD students.
Q: Can I apply for the DAAD scholarship if I am already studying in Germany?
A: No, you are not eligible to apply for the DAAD scholarship if you have been residing in Germany for more than 15 months at the time of application.
Q: Do I need to speak German to apply for the DAAD scholarship?
A: Some programs may require German language proficiency, but many programs are taught in English, so language proficiency in English may be sufficient.
Q: Can I apply for the DAAD scholarship if I am not a Syrian refugee?
A: No, this scholarship program is specifically designed for Syrian refugees.
Q: How do I know if my asylum or refugee status is recognized?
A: You should check with the relevant authorities in your current country of residence to confirm your asylum or refugee status.
Q: What happens if I am selected for the DAAD scholarship but do not receive my visa in time?
A: The DAAD will provide guidance and support to help you with the visa application process, but it is your responsibility to ensure you have a valid visa to enter Germany.
