The Helmut-Schmidt-Programme qualifies Afghan refugees for a funding amount of up to €850 per month, and it is one of the most popular [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded) for international students, including refugees, in Germany.

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for STEM scholarships in Germany as an Afghan refugee, applicants typically need to hold a valid visa, such as a refugee visa or a temporary residence permit, and meet specific eligibility criteria, including academic achievements and language proficiency. Visa categories for Afghan refugees in Germany include subsidiary protection, asylum, and temporary residence permits. ## Available Scholarships
### Deutschlandstipendium
* Amount: €300 per month
* Eligibility: Open to all international students, including Afghan refugees, pursuing a STEM degree
* Deadline: Varies by university
* Official URL: https://www.bmbf.de/en/deutschlandstipendium.html

### Helmut-Schmidt-Programme
* Amount: up to €850 per month
* Eligibility: Open to Afghan refugees and other international students from developing countries
* Deadline: October 31st
* Official URL: https://www.daad.de/en/study-and-research-in-germany/scholarships/helmut-schmidt-programme/

### Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung Scholarship
* Amount: €1,200 per month
* Eligibility: Open to international students, including Afghan refugees, pursuing a STEM degree
* Deadline: July 15th
* Official URL: https://www.kas.de/en/web/begabtenfoerderung-und-stipendienprogramme
