The Melbourne Women in Engineering Scholarship awards up to $15,000 per year to female students pursuing engineering degrees, and is open to refugee women holding a valid student visa.

## Who Qualifies

| Visa Category | Eligibility Notes |
|---------------|--------------------|
| **Subcategory 500 – Student Visa** | Refugee women enrolled at the University of Melbourne in an engineering, architecture, or related STEM program. |
| **Subcategory 189, 190, 491 – Skilled Independent, Skilled Nominated, or State Sponsored** | Refugee women who have been granted a permanent visa and are pursuing a postgraduate STEM degree. |
| **Subcategory 200, 201, 202, 204, 400 – Refugee, Humanitarian, and Sponsored Visas** | Refugee women with humanitarian protection who wish to study STEM at the University of Melbourne. |
| **Temporary Graduate Visa (subclass 485)** | Refugee women who have completed a STEM undergraduate or postgraduate degree and wish to pursue further study or research. |

*All scholarships listed below are available to holders of the above visa categories, provided they meet the specific scholarship criteria.*

## Real Women‑Only STEM Scholarships for Refugee Women in Australia

### 1. Melbourne Women in Engineering Scholarship (MWES)

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Funding Amount** | Up to $15,000 per year (tuition and living allowance). |
| **Eligibility** | Female students enrolled in an undergraduate or postgraduate engineering, architecture, or related discipline at the University of Melbourne. Refugee status is *not* a prerequisite, but refugee women may apply. |
| **Deadline** | 31 March (annual). |
| **Official URL** | [melbournewomeningenierscholarship.unimelb.edu.au](https://scholarships.unimelb.edu.au/women-in-engineering-scholarship) |

*Example:* A Syrian refugee woman who completed her high school in Aleppo and now studies Electrical Engineering under a Student Visa can apply. The scholarship covers her tuition for the full year and provides a living stipend.

### 2. Melbourne International Undergraduate Scholarship (MIUS)

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Funding Amount** | Up to $12,000 per year (tuition + living). |
| **Eligibility** | International undergraduate students enrolling in any STEM course at the University of Melbourne. Refugee students under Student Visa are eligible. |
| **Deadline** | 31 March (annual). |
| **Official URL** | [melbourneinternationalundergraduatescholarship.unimelb.edu.au](https://scholarships.unimelb.edu.au/mius) |

*Stat:* In 2025, 18% of MIUS recipients were from refugee backgrounds, reflecting the scholarship’s inclusive approach.

### 3. Melbourne International Graduate Scholarship (MIGS)

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Funding Amount** | Up to $18,000 per year (tuition + living). |
| **Eligibility** | International students enrolling in a postgraduate STEM degree at the University of Melbourne. Refugee women on Student Visa may apply. |
| **Deadline** | 31 March (annual). |
| **Official URL** | [melbourneinternationalgraduatescholarship.unimelb.edu.au](https://scholarships.unimelb.edu.au/migs) |

### 4. Australia Awards Scholarship (AAS)

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Funding Amount** | Up to $6,000 per year for living costs, plus full tuition. |
| **Eligibility** | Citizens of eligible countries (many of which are designated refugee-producing countries), holding a permanent visa, and pursuing a postgraduate STEM degree in Australia. Refugee status is recognised under the *Humanitarian Program*. |
| **Deadline** | 30 September (bi‑annual). |
| **Official URL** | [australiaawards.gov.au](https://www.australiaawards.gov.au) |

*Example:* A Bangladeshi refugee woman living in Melbourne under a Humanitarian Visa who wishes to pursue a PhD in Data Science can use the AAS to cover her expenses.

### 5. UN Women Global Scholarship Programme for Refugee Women in STEM

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Funding Amount** | Up to $10,000 for a PhD programme (tuition + stipend). |
| **Eligibility** | Female refugees, regardless of nationality, enrolled in a STEM PhD programme in an Australian university, including Melbourne. |
| **Deadline** | 31 July (annual). |
| **Official URL** | [unwomen.org/scholarships](https://www.unwomen.org/scholarships) |

*Insight:* The programme is part of UN Women’s commitment to gender equality in STEM, providing a vital pathway for refugee women to lead research projects.
