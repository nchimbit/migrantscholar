The Australia Awards Scholarships exist and qualify skilled Bangladeshi migrants in the STEM field with funding up to AUD 30,000 per year. 
The Australian government offers various visa options for skilled migrants, including those in the STEM field, to live and work in Australia. 
As a skilled Bangladeshi migrant, you can apply for different visa categories, including the Skilled Independent visa, Skilled Nominated visa, and Temporary Skilled Migration visa. 

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for these visa categories, you must meet specific requirements, such as having a bachelor's or higher degree in a STEM field, having relevant work experience, and meeting the English language proficiency requirements. 
You can check your eligibility for these visa categories on the Australian Government's Department of Home Affairs website.

## Scholarships
### Australian Government Research Training Program
The Australian Government Research Training Program provides funding up to AUD 28,597 per year for international students, including skilled Bangladeshi migrants, pursuing a research-based master's or PhD degree in Australia. 
Eligibility criteria include being a citizen of a participating country, having a bachelor's degree with honors, and meeting the English language proficiency requirements. 
The deadline for applications varies depending on the university. 
For more information, visit the [official website](https://www.education.gov.au/australian-government-research-training-program).

### Australia Awards Scholarships
