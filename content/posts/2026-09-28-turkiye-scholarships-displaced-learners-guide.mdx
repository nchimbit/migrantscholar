## Eligibility and Important Requirements

Türkiye Scholarships are open to foreign nationals who meet the following broad criteria. Exact requirements can change, so verify each point on the official portal.

| Category | Typical Requirement | Notes |
|----------|---------------------|-------|
| **Nationality** | Any foreign national | Turkish citizens are not eligible for the scholarship. |
| **Displacement status** | Recognized refugee, asylum seeker, or displaced person | Proof of status (UNHCR registration, Turkish residence permit, etc.) is usually required. |
| **Academic record** | High‑school diploma for undergraduate; bachelor’s degree for master’s; master’s degree for PhD | Minimum grades or GPA may be specified by the university. |
| **Language proficiency** | Turkish (for most programs) or English (for English‑taught programs) | Official test scores (e.g., TOEFL, IELTS, TÖMER) may be required. |
| **Health** | Valid health insurance covering the duration of study | Some programs may require a medical examination. |
| **Financial** | No private funding needed; scholarship covers tuition and living costs | Proof of financial need may be requested. |
| **Other** | Good moral character, no criminal record | Some universities may conduct background checks. |

**Important**: If you are a refugee or asylum seeker, you may need to provide additional documentation such as a residence permit issued by the Turkish authorities or a UNHCR registration card.

## What Funding or Support May Be Available

Türkiye Scholarships are designed to be comprehensive. While exact figures are not published in this guide, the scholarship typically covers:

- **Tuition fees** for the entire duration of the program.
- **Monthly living stipend** to cover accommodation, food, and transportation.
- **Health insurance** valid in Turkey for the duration of the study.
- **Travel allowance** for the first and last semester (or year) to cover travel to and from Turkey.
- **Book allowance** or a stipend for academic materials (if applicable).

The scholarship may also provide additional support such as:

- **Orientation sessions** for new international students.
- **Academic advising** and mentorship programs.
- **Cultural integration activities** to help students adapt to Turkish society.

Because the exact amounts and conditions can vary by program and year, always check the official website for the most recent information.

## How to Prepare an Application

Below is a step‑by‑step outline that separates **general preparation** from the **official application process**.

### 1. Preliminary Self‑Assessment

| Step | Action | Tips |
|------|--------|------|
| **Check eligibility** | Review the eligibility overview above. | If you’re unsure about your displacement status, contact the Turkish embassy or a local NGO. |
| **Choose a field of study** | Look at universities offering programs in your area of interest. | Use the university search tool on the scholarship portal. |
| **Research language requirements** | Determine whether the program is taught in Turkish or English. | If you need to improve language skills, enroll in a preparatory course. |

### 2. Gather Academic Credentials

- **High‑school diploma** (undergraduate applicants) or **bachelor’s degree** (master’s applicants) or **master’s degree** (PhD applicants).
- **Official transcripts** with grades and course descriptions.
- **Degree certificates** translated into Turkish or English if necessary.

### 3. Secure Language Proficiency Proof

- **Turkish**: TÖMER test or equivalent.
- **English**: TOEFL, IELTS, or other accepted tests.

### 4. Obtain Displacement Documentation

- **UNHCR registration card** or **Turkish residence permit**.
- **Proof of refugee status** if applicable.

### 5. Prepare Personal Documents

- **Passport** (valid for at least 6 months beyond the study period).
- **Photographs** (passport‑size, recent).
- **Curriculum Vitae** or resume.
- **Statement of Purpose** (outline your academic goals and why you chose Turkey).

### 6. Request Recommendation Letters

- **Academic references** from teachers or professors.
- **Professional references** if you have work experience.

### 7. Health and Insurance

- **Medical examination** if required by the university.
- **Health insurance** that meets Turkish standards.

### 8. Register on the Scholarship Portal

- Create an account on https://www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr/.
- Complete the **profile** with personal and academic details.

### 9. Submit the Application

- **Fill out the online application form** for the chosen program.
- **Upload all required documents** in PDF or JPEG format.
- **Pay any application fee** if applicable (some programs may waive it for displaced learners).

### 10. Follow Up

- **Check your email** for confirmation of receipt.
- **Track application status** through the portal.
- **Respond promptly** to any requests for additional information.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Who is eligible for Türkiye Scholarships as a displaced learner?

Displaced learners who are recognized refugees, asylum seekers, or internally displaced persons with valid documentation (e.g., UNHCR registration, Turkish residence permit) and meet the academic and language requirements can apply. Always verify your specific status on the official portal.

### 2. How do I apply for a Türkiye Scholarship?

Create an account on https://www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr/, complete the online application form for your chosen program, upload all required documents, and submit. Check the portal for any program‑specific steps or additional forms.

### 3. What does the scholarship cover?

The scholarship typically includes tuition, a monthly living stipend, health insurance, and a travel allowance. Exact amounts and conditions vary by program and year; consult the official website for details.

### 4. Do I need a Turkish visa to study in Turkey?

Yes. Once you receive an admission offer and scholarship confirmation, you must apply for a student visa at the Turkish embassy or consulate in your country. Follow the embassy’s instructions and provide all required documents, including the scholarship award letter.

### 5. Can I study in a program taught in English?

Yes. Many Turkish universities offer English‑taught master’s and PhD programs. You will need to provide proof of English proficiency (e.g., TOEFL, IELTS) and meet any additional language requirements set by the university.

### 6. How long does the application process take?

The timeline varies by program and year. Generally, it takes several weeks from application submission to decision. After acceptance, obtaining a visa can add additional weeks. Plan ahead and start the process early.
