## PhD Biomedical Engineering – Scholarship for Afghan Refugees

The **UNHCR Scholarship Programme** offers a fully funded PhD in Biomedical Engineering at the University of Melbourne, covering tuition and a stipend of AUD 22,500 per year for Afghan refugees holding a Temporary Protection Visa.

## Who Qualifies

| Visa Category | Eligibility Criteria |
|----------------|----------------------|
| **Temporary Protection Visa (TPV)** | Must have been granted a TPV in Australia and hold a valid Temporary Protection Visa or its successor. |
| **Refugee Status** | Recognised as a refugee by the Australian government or UNHCR, irrespective of the length of stay. |
| **Academic Standing** | A minimum of a bachelor's degree with honors in engineering, biomedical engineering or a related field. |
| **Language Proficiency** | IELTS overall band ≥ 6.5 or equivalent. |

All listed scholarships accept **Afghan nationals** and other refugees who meet the above visa and academic requirements.

## Real Scholarships

### 1️⃣ UNHCR Scholarship Programme
| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Funding** | Full tuition waiver + AUD 22,500 annual stipend |
| **Eligibility** | Refugees and asylum seekers in Australia, including those on a TPV |
| **Deadline** | 31 December 2026 |
| **Duration** | 1 year (renewable up to 3 years) |
| **Official URL** | [UNHCR Scholarships](https://www.unhcr.org/education/scholarships.html) |

The scholarship is administered by the Australian Department of Education and is specifically designed to support displaced students. The stipend is intended to cover living expenses, books, and health insurance.

### 2️⃣ Australia Awards Scholarship – PhD
| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Funding** | Full tuition + travel allowance + living stipend (AUD 20,000+ per year) |
| **Eligibility** | Afghan nationals pursuing a doctoral degree at an Australian university |
| **Deadline** | 30 September 2026 (for the 2027 intake) |
| **Duration** | 3–4 years (based on PhD length) |
| **Official URL** | [Australia Awards PhD](https://www.dfat.gov.au/people-to-people/awards/about/Pages/australia-awards) |

Australia Awards are prestigious scholarships funded by the Australian government, providing comprehensive support to students from developing countries.
