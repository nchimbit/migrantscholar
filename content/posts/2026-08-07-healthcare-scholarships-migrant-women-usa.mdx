Migrant women in the USA can access various healthcare scholarships to pursue their educational goals.

## Who Qualifies
Migrant women with various visa categories, including F1, J1, H1B, green card, and asylum status, can apply for healthcare scholarships in the USA. 
These scholarships are designed to support migrant women pursuing healthcare-related fields, such as [nursing scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/fields/nursing) or [medical scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/fields/medicine). 
To be eligible, migrant women must meet specific requirements, including academic performance, language proficiency, and community involvement.
