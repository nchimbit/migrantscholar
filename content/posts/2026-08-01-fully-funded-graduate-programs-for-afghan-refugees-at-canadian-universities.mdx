The [Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarship](https://www.canada.ca/en/research-funding-programs.html) exists for Afghan refugees, providing $50,000 per year for three years, and is available to those with a [study permit](https://migrantscholar.com/visa-categories/study-permit) or [permanent residence](https://migrantscholar.com/visa-categories/permanent-residence) in Canada.

## Who Qualifies
To qualify for these graduate programs, Afghan refugees must have a valid [study permit](https://migrantscholar.com/visa-categories/study-permit), [work permit](https://migrantscholar.com/visa-categories/work-permit), or [permanent residence](https://migrantscholar.com/visa-categories/permanent-residence) in Canada. They must also meet the eligibility criteria for each specific scholarship program.

### University of Toronto Scholarship
The University of Toronto offers the [Lester B. Pearson International Scholarship](https://www.utoronto.ca/academics/pearson-scholarship), valued at $80,000 for four years, to international students, including Afghan refugees, who demonstrate academic excellence and leadership abilities. Deadline: January 17, 2027. [Official website](https://www.utoronto.ca/).

### University of British Columbia Scholarship
The University of British Columbia offers the [Donald A. Wehrung International Student Award](https://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/scholarships-awards-international-students/), valued at $25,000 per year, to international undergraduate students, including Afghan refugees, who demonstrate academic excellence and financial need. Deadline: December 1, 2026. [Official website](https://you.ubc.ca/).

### University of Toronto Graduate Scholarship
The University of Toronto offers the [Ontario Graduate Scholarship](https://www.sgs.utoronto.ca/awards/ontario-graduate-scholarship/), valued at $15,000 per year, to graduate students, including Afghan refugees, who demonstrate academic excellence. Deadline: March 15, 2027. [Official website](https://www.sgs.utoronto.ca/).

### York University Scholarship
York University offers the [York University Academic Excellence Scholarship](https://futurestudents.yorku.ca/scholarships), valued at $20,000 per year, to international undergraduate students, including Afghan refugees, who demonstrate academic excellence. Deadline: February 1, 2027. [Official website](https://futurestudents.yorku.ca/).

### McGill University Scholarship
McGill University offers the [McGill University Major Entrance Scholarship](https://www.mcgill.ca/scholarships-aid/entrance-scholarships), valued at $12,000 per year, to international undergraduate students, including Afghan refugees, who demonstrate academic excellence. Deadline: January 10, 2027. [Official website](https://www.mcgill.ca/).

## Application Steps
1. Research and choose a graduate program at a [Canadian university](https://migrantscholar.com/countries/Canada).
2. Check the eligibility criteria for each [fully funded scholarship](https://migrantscholar.com/by-funding/fully-funded).
3. Review the [scholarship deadlines](https://migrantscholar.com/deadlines) and make a timeline for applications.
4. Prepare and submit all required documents, including transcripts, letters of reference, and language proficiency test scores.
5. Submit the application to the chosen university and scholarship program.
6. Apply for a [study permit](https://migrantscholar.com/visa-categories/study-permit) or [permanent residence](https://migrantscholar.com/visa-categories/permanent-residence) in Canada.
7. Receive and review the offer of admission and scholarship award.
8. Accept the offer and begin preparing for studies in Canada, including applying for [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-level/phd) if applicable.
