The Melbourne School of Engineering Scholarship exists for migrant women, providing full tuition fee waiver and a stipend of $30,600 per annum for women pursuing [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-level/phd) in STEM fields at University of Melbourne, Australia. This scholarship is highly competitive and is available to women who are either Australian citizens, Australian permanent residents, or [international students](https://migrantscholar.com/countries/Australia) on a valid visa category, including subclass 500, 570, 571, 572, 573, 574, 575, 576, 580, 581, and 485 visas.

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for the Melbourne School of Engineering Scholarship and other [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-funding/fully-funded), migrant women must meet specific criteria, including being a woman, meeting the visa requirements, and being enrolled in a STEM-related field such as engineering, mathematics, or computer science. The visa categories eligible for these scholarships include, but are not limited to, Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485), Student visa (subclass 500), and Skilled Independent visa (subclass 189). For more information on [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.com/by-eligibility/refugees), please visit our website.

## Available Scholarships
The following scholarships are available to migrant women pursuing [STEM scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-field/stem):
### Women in Engineering Scholarship
* Amount: $10,000 per annum
* Eligibility: Female students enrolled in an engineering course
* Deadline: 31 December 2026
* Official URL: https://eng.unimelb.edu.au/study/scholarships
### Melbourne Graduate Scholarship
* Amount: Full tuition fee waiver and a stipend of $30,600 per annum
* Eligibility: High-achieving students enrolled in a graduate course
* Deadline: 31 October 2026
* Official URL: https://www.unimelb.edu.au/scholarships
### Australia Awards Scholarship
* Amount: Full tuition fee waiver, living allowance, and return airfare
* Eligibility: International students from participating countries
* Deadline: 29 April 2026
* Official URL: https://www.australiaawards.gov.au/
### Research Training Program Scholarship
* Amount: Full tuition fee waiver and a stipend of $28,597 per annum
* Eligibility: Domestic and international students enrolled in a research course
* Deadline: 31 August 2026
* Official URL: https://www.unimelb.edu.au/scholarships
### University of Melbourne Human Rights Scholarship
* Amount: Full tuition fee waiver and a stipend of $30,600 per annum
* Eligibility: International students from developing countries
* Deadline: 31 January 2027
* Official URL: https://www.unimelb.edu.au/scholarships
