## Scholarships for Migrant Professionals
The following are five real scholarships available to migrant professionals, including Indian nationals:
### Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarship
* Amount: $50,000 per year for three years
* Eligibility: Doctoral students
* Deadline: November 1
* Official URL: https://vanier.gc.ca/en/home-accueil.html
### Banting Postdoctoral Fellowships
* Amount: $70,000 per year for two years
* Eligibility: Postdoctoral researchers
* Deadline: October 1
* Official URL: https://banting.fellowships-bourses.gc.ca/en/home-accueil.html
### CIHR Fellowship
* Amount: $40,000 to $60,000 per year for up to three years
* Eligibility: Master's, doctoral, and postdoctoral students
* Deadline: Varies
* Official URL: https://cihr-irsc.gc.ca/e/193.html
### Ontario Graduate Scholarship
* Amount: $15,000 per year for two or three years
* Eligibility: Master's and doctoral students
* Deadline: Varies
* Official URL: https://osap.gov.on.ca/en/Aid/Grants/SGDO.htm
### Quebec Merit Scholarship for Foreign Students
* Amount: $25,000 to $43,000 per year for up to three years
* Eligibility: Master's, doctoral, and postdoctoral students
* Deadline: Varies
* Official URL: https://www.mfa.gouv.qc.ca/en/programs/scholarships/

## Application Steps

2. Review the application requirements, including transcripts, letters of reference, and language proficiency tests.
