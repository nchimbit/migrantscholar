## Who This Guide Is For

This guide is written for refugees, asylum seekers, and international students who are interested in pursuing higher education in Turkey and are exploring the Türkiye Scholarships program. It is not a substitute for legal advice; visa regulations and scholarship criteria can change, so always consult the official sources and, if possible, a qualified immigration or education adviser.

## Eligibility and Important Requirements

### General Eligibility
- **Nationality**: The scholarship is open to citizens of any country, including those who are refugees or asylum seekers.  
- **Academic Level**: Applicants can apply for undergraduate, master’s, or doctoral studies, depending on the program they choose.  
- **Language Proficiency**: Most programs require proficiency in Turkish or English, depending on the language of instruction.  
- **Health and Character**: Applicants must meet health and character requirements set by the Turkish Ministry of National Education.

### Refugee‑Specific Considerations
- Refugees who have been granted protection status in Turkey may have additional documentation requirements, such as a residence permit or a refugee status certificate.  
- Some universities may offer additional support services for refugee students, such as language courses or counseling.

> **Tip**: Because eligibility criteria can vary by university and program, check the official Türkiye Scholarships website for the most up‑to‑date information.

## What Funding or Support May Be Available

Türkiye Scholarships is a fully funded program that typically covers:
- Tuition fees (if applicable).  
- Monthly stipend for living expenses.  
- Health insurance.  
- Round‑trip airfare to Turkey (for some programs).  

> **Important**: The exact scope of funding can differ by study level and university. Always verify the details on the official website before applying.

## How to Prepare an Application

### 1. Identify Your Target Program
- Browse the list of universities and courses offered under Türkiye Scholarships.  
- Note the language of instruction and any specific prerequisites.

### 2. Gather Academic Records
- Official transcripts, diplomas, and certificates.  
- If your documents are not in Turkish or English, arrange for certified translations.

### 3. Prepare Language Test Scores
- Take the required language test (e.g., TOEFL, IELTS for English; Turkish language tests for Turkish‑taught programs).  
- Keep official score reports ready.

### 4. Draft a Strong Personal Statement
- Explain your academic goals, why you chose Turkey, and how the scholarship will help you.  
- Highlight any experiences related to your refugee status that demonstrate resilience or community engagement.

### 5. Secure Letters of Recommendation
- Obtain at least two academic or professional references.  
- Provide contact details and a brief background of each recommender.

### 6. Compile Supporting Documents
- Passport copy.  
- Refugee status certificate or residence permit (if applicable).  
- Health insurance proof.  
- Any additional documents requested by the specific university.

### 7. Submit the Application
- Most applications are submitted online through the Türkiye Scholarships portal.  
- Pay attention to file formats, size limits, and submission deadlines.

> **Caution**: The application process may involve multiple stages, including an initial online submission, a university review, and a final decision by the scholarship committee. Keep track of each stage and respond promptly to any requests for additional information.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Submitting incomplete applications** – Double‑check that every required field and document is uploaded.  
2. **Missing deadlines** – Keep a calendar of all submission dates; late applications are usually not accepted.  
3. **Using unofficial translations** – Only certified translations are accepted for academic records.  
4. **Ignoring language requirements** – Even if a program is taught in Turkish, some courses may require English proficiency.  
5. **Failing to update contact information** – Ensure your email and phone number are current so the scholarship committee can reach you.  
6. **Overlooking visa steps** – Admission is only the first step; you must still secure a student visa before traveling.

## Why Turkey Is an Attractive Destination for Refugee Students

Turkey’s higher education system is one of the most accessible and diverse in the region. Public universities offer a wide range of disciplines—from engineering and medicine to humanities and social sciences—often at a lower cost than many Western European institutions. The country’s strategic location bridges Europe, Asia, and the Middle East, providing students with a unique cultural experience and a gateway to further academic or professional opportunities.

For refugees, Turkey’s commitment to education is reflected in policies that encourage integration through language courses, orientation programs, and counseling services. Many universities have dedicated offices that assist international students with housing, banking, and cultural adaptation, ensuring a smoother transition into academic life.

## The Structure of Türkiye Scholarships

The scholarship program is organized into three main tiers:

- **Undergraduate**: Designed for students who have completed secondary education and wish to pursue a bachelor’s degree.  
- **Master’s**: Targeted at students with a bachelor’s degree who aim to specialize in a particular field.  
- **Doctoral**: For candidates who have already earned a master’s degree and intend to conduct research at the highest level.

Each tier has its own set of requirements and benefits, but all share the core features of full tuition coverage, a monthly stipend, health insurance, and, in some cases, airfare. The program also encourages academic excellence by offering additional support for students who maintain high grades.

## Additional Support for Refugee Students

While the scholarship covers many costs, universities often provide extra resources to help refugee students settle in:

- **Language Courses**: Introductory Turkish or English classes tailored to academic needs.  
- **Counseling Services**: Professional support for mental health and academic guidance.  
- **Mentorship Programs**: Pairing new students with senior peers or alumni who can share experiences and advice.  
- **Housing Assistance**: Information on university dormitories, shared apartments, or subsidized housing options.  

These services are not mandatory but can significantly enhance the overall experience and help students focus on their studies.

## Practical Steps for Visa Application

After receiving an admission offer, the next critical step is obtaining a student visa. The process typically involves:

1. **Collecting the Admission Letter**: The university will issue an official letter confirming your enrollment.  
2. **Completing the Visa Application Form**: Available at the Turkish consulate or embassy in your country.  
3. **Submitting Supporting Documents**: Passport, admission letter, proof of scholarship, health insurance, and any required photographs.  
4. **Paying the Visa Fee**: Fees vary by country and are payable at the consulate.  
5. **Attending an Interview**: Some consulates require a brief interview to verify the purpose of your stay.  
6. **Waiting for Processing**: Processing times differ but generally range from a few days to a few weeks.  

It is advisable to start this process as soon as you receive your admission letter, as visa processing can take time and delays may affect your travel plans.

## Managing Living Expenses and Budgeting

Even with a stipend, living in a new country can be challenging. Here are some practical tips:

- **Create a Monthly Budget**: Track income (stipend) and expenses (rent, food, transport, study materials).  
- **Use Student Discounts**: Many public transport systems, museums, and bookstores offer reduced rates for students.  
- **Cook at Home**: Preparing meals can save money compared to eating out.  
- **Share Accommodation**: Renting a room in a shared apartment often reduces costs.  
- **Seek Part‑Time Work**: Some universities allow students to work part‑time on campus, providing additional income and work experience.  

Planning ahead and staying organized can help you make the most of the stipend and avoid financial stress.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Can refugees apply for Türkiye Scholarships even if they are not currently in Turkey?
Yes, refugees who are outside Turkey can apply, provided they meet the scholarship’s eligibility criteria and can provide the necessary documentation, such as a valid passport and proof of refugee status.

### 2. Do I need a Turkish language certificate if the program is taught in English?
Not necessarily. If the program’s instruction language is English, you will need to meet the English proficiency requirement. However, some universities may still require a basic Turkish language test for orientation or campus life.

### 3. How long does the visa process take after I receive an admission offer?
The processing time can vary depending on the Turkish consulate or embassy in your country. Generally, it takes a few weeks, but it is advisable to start the visa application as soon as you receive your admission letter.

### 4. Is the scholarship renewable for multiple years?
For master’s and PhD programs, the scholarship can be renewed annually, provided you meet the academic performance and progress requirements set by the scholarship committee.

### 5. What happens if I fail to maintain the required academic performance?
If you do not meet the minimum academic standards, the scholarship may be revoked, and you may be required to withdraw from the program. Check the specific terms on the official website.

### 6. Can I bring family members with me to Turkey while studying?
Family members may apply for dependent visas, but they are not covered by the scholarship stipend. You will need to arrange separate accommodation and financial support for them.
