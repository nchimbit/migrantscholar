The Australia Awards Scholarships exist and qualify Turkish migrants with a full funding amount of AUD 30,911 per year for STEM fields. 
Turkish migrants who are interested in pursuing higher education in Australia can apply for various scholarships, including [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-funding/fully-funded) and [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-level/phd). 

## Who Qualifies
Turkish migrants with a valid Australian visa, such as a Permanent Resident, Temporary Graduate, or Skilled Migrant, are eligible to apply for these scholarships. The visa categories that qualify for these scholarships include, but are not limited to, Subclass 189, Subclass 190, Subclass 491, and Subclass 494.
