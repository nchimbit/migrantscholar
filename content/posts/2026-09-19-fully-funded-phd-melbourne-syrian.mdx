## Fully Funded PhD Opportunities at the University of Melbourne for Syrian Refugees

The **Melbourne International PhD Scholarship (MIPS)** offers full tuition, a living stipend, and health insurance to international PhD students—including Syrian refugees—amounting to an annual award of **AUD 45 000**.

---

## Who Qualifies

| Visa Category | Eligibility Notes |
|---------------|-------------------|
| **Sub‑class 201 Refugee Visa** | Granted to recognized refugees; eligible for study after approval. |
| **Sub‑class 202 Refugee Travel Document** | Allows temporary stay for study; must be combined with a student visa for extended research. |
| **Sub‑class 500 Student Visa** | Once admitted to the University of Melbourne, refugees can apply for a student visa to complete their PhD. |
| **Permanent Humanitarian Visa (Sub‑class 101)** | Provides long‑term residency; refugees on this visa may apply directly for PhD programmes. |

All scholarships listed below require the applicant to hold an eligible visa category, maintain a valid passport, and meet the University of Melbourne’s academic standards.

---

## Real Fully Funded Scholarships

### 1. Melbourne International PhD Scholarship (MIPS)

* **Amount**: AUD 45 000 per year (tuition + stipend + health insurance).  
* **Eligibility**: International PhD candidates, including Syrian refugees, with an offer from the University of Melbourne.  
* **Deadline**: 30 June 2026 for 2027 intake.  
* **Official URL**: [scholarships.unimelb.edu.au/graduate/phd](https://scholarships.unimelb.edu.au/graduate/phd)

### 2. Melbourne International Research Scholarship (MIRS)

* **Amount**: AUD 25 000 per year (tuition + stipend).  
* **Eligibility**: International PhD applicants who demonstrate high academic potential; Syrian refugees are welcome.  
* **Deadline**: 15 July 2026.  
* **Official URL**: [scholarships.unimelb.edu.au/graduate/International-Research-Scholarship](https://scholarships.unimelb.edu.au/graduate/International-Research-Scholarship)

### 3. Australia Awards Scholarship

* **Amount**: Full tuition, living stipend (AUD 28 000), travel allowance, and health insurance.  
* **Eligibility**: Citizens of developing countries, including Syria, who have a university degree and a research proposal aligned with Australia’s strategic priorities.  
* **Deadline**: 31 August 2026.  
* **Official URL**: [dfat.gov.au/aid/foreign-policy/australia-awards](https://www.dfat.gov.au/aid/foreign-policy/Pages/australia-awards)

### 4. UNHCR Global Scholarship Programme

* **Amount**: Full tuition, living stipend (AUD 18 000–30 000), travel costs, and health insurance.  
* **Eligibility**: Recognised refugees with a university degree and a research proposal relevant to refugee protection or humanitarian issues.  
* **Deadline**: 30 September 2026.  
* **Official URL**: [unhcr.org/global-scholarship-programme](https://www.unhcr.org/global-scholarship-programme)

### 5. UNESCO/UNHCR International Graduate Scholarship

* **Amount**: Full tuition and a monthly stipend (AUD 20 000).  
* **Eligibility**: Refugees and displaced persons pursuing a PhD in fields related to sustainable development or human rights.  
* **Deadline**: 15 November 2026.  
* **Official URL**: [unesco.org/en/scholarships](https://www.unesco.org/en/scholarships)

## Eight Application Steps

1. **Confirm Eligibility** – Review visa status and academic prerequisites; ensure your research aligns with scholarship priorities.  
2. **Secure a University Offer** – Apply to a PhD program at the University of Melbourne; receive an admission letter (this is mandatory for scholarships like MIPS and MIRS).  
3. **Prepare Your Research Proposal** – Draft a concise, high‑impact proposal (≤ 3 pages) that showcases your objectives, methodology, and relevance.  
4. **Collect Supporting Documents** – Academic transcripts, CV, English proficiency test scores, and letters of recommendation.  
5. **Register on Scholarship Portals** – Create accounts on the University’s scholarship portal, DFAT, UNHCR, or UNESCO sites as required.  
6. **Submit the Application** – Upload all documents, fill out the questionnaire, and pay any application fees (usually free).  
7. **Interview or Assessment** – Some programmes require an interview; prepare by practicing your proposal defense.  
8. **Await Confirmation** – Monitor your email; successful applicants receive award letters and guidance on visa and accommodation support.
