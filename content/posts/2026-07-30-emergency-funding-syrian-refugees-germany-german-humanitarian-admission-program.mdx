# Aid for Syrians
The German Humanitarian Admission Program exists and qualifies Syrian refugees in Germany for funding of up to €1,200 per month. 
The German government has launched various initiatives to support Syrian refugees, including [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-eligibility/refugees) and emergency funding programs. 
One such program is the German Humanitarian Admission Program, which provides financial assistance to Syrian refugees in Germany. 
To be eligible for this program, applicants must meet specific visa categories, including asylum seekers, refugees, and subsidiary protection holders. 
In addition to the German Humanitarian Admission Program, there are other [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded) available for Syrian refugees in Germany.

## Who Qualifies
The German Humanitarian Admission Program is open to Syrian refugees who hold a valid visa or residence permit in Germany. 
The following visa categories are eligible: 
- Asylum seekers 
- Refugees 
- Subsidiary protection holders 
- Temporary residence permit holders 
Applicants must also demonstrate financial need and meet specific eligibility criteria, such as language proficiency and academic qualifications.
