The DAAD Scholarship is a fully funded master's program available to Syrian refugees, providing a monthly stipend of 750 euros to cover living expenses in Germany.

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for these scholarships, applicants must hold a valid visa, specifically a visa for humanitarian reasons, a temporary residence permit, or a residence permit for the purpose of asylum. This includes those with subsidiary protection, asylum seekers, or those with a pending asylum application. It's essential to review the visa categories, including the " tolerated stay" status, to determine your eligibility.

## Available Scholarships
### DAAD Scholarship
* Amount: 750 euros per month
* Eligibility: Syrian refugees with a bachelor's degree
* Deadline: October 31, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.daad.de/en/
### Deutschlandstipendium
* Amount: 300 euros per month
* Eligibility: Syrian refugees enrolled in a German university
* Deadline: July 15, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.deutschlandstipendium.de/en/
### Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung Scholarship
* Amount: 1000 euros per month
* Eligibility: Syrian refugees pursuing a master's degree in Germany
* Deadline: January 15, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.kas.de/en/
### Friedrich-Ebert-Stiftung Scholarship
* Amount: 750 euros per month
* Eligibility: Syrian refugees with a strong academic record
* Deadline: November 30, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.fes.de/en/
### Heinrich-Böll-Stiftung Scholarship
* Amount: 1000 euros per month
* Eligibility: Syrian refugees pursuing a master's degree in a relevant field
* Deadline: March 1, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.boell.de/en/

## Application Steps
1. Review the eligibility criteria for each scholarship.
2. Choose the scholarship that best suits your academic and professional goals.
3. Prepare the required documents, including transcripts and language certificates.
4. Fill out the application form, usually available on the official website.
5. Submit your application, following the guidelines provided.
6. Wait for the selection committee to review your application.
7. If selected, you will be notified and required to confirm your acceptance.
8. Upon confirmation, you will receive instructions on how to proceed with the scholarship.

## Documents Checklist
* Valid passport

* Academic transcripts

* Letters of recommendation
* Bachelor's or master's degree certificate

## Frequently Asked Questions
1. What is the average processing time for a DAAD scholarship application?
Answer: The processing time varies, but it usually takes 2-3 months.
2. Can I apply for multiple scholarships simultaneously?
Answer: Yes, you can apply for multiple scholarships, but you must meet the eligibility criteria for each one.
3. What language proficiency level is required for German universities?
Answer: The required language proficiency level is usually B2 or C1 in German or English, depending on the program.
4. How can I find a suitable master's program in Germany?
Answer: You can search for programs on the DAAD website or consult with the German Academic Exchange Service.
5. Can I bring my family with me if I receive a scholarship?
Answer: It depends on the scholarship and the specific regulations; check with the scholarship provider for more information.
6. How do I apply for a residence permit as a scholarship holder?
Answer: You can apply for a residence permit at the local immigration office in Germany.
7. What are the living costs in Germany, and how can I cover them?
Answer: The living costs in Germany vary, but you can cover them with your scholarship stipend, part-time jobs, or personal savings.
8. Can I extend my scholarship if I need more time to complete my studies?
Answer: It's possible to extend your scholarship, but you must provide a valid reason and meet the extension criteria.
