The Fulbright Program for Afghan Women qualifies Afghan women for fully funded scholarships to pursue higher education in STEM fields in the USA, with funding amount of up to $100,000.

## Who Qualifies
The Fulbright Program and other fully funded STEM scholarships in the USA are available to Afghan women who are migrants, refugees, or asylum seekers, including those with F-1, J-1, or asylum seeker visas. These scholarships aim to support women in pursuing higher education in STEM fields, promoting diversity and inclusion in these fields. ## Scholarships
### AAUW International Fellowships
* Amount: $18,000-$30,000
* Eligibility: Women from Afghanistan pursuing graduate or postgraduate studies in STEM fields
* Deadline: November 15
* Official URL: https://www.aauw.org/resources/programs/fellowships/international/
### P.E.O. International Peace Scholarship
* Amount: up to $12,500
* Eligibility: Women from Afghanistan pursuing graduate studies in STEM fields
* Deadline: December 15
* Official URL: https://www.peointernational.org/about-peo-international-peace-scholarship-ips
### Fulbright Foreign Student Program
* Amount: up to $100,000
* Eligibility: Afghan women pursuing higher education in STEM fields
* Deadline: varies by country
* Official URL: https://foreign.fulbrightonline.org/
### National Science Foundation Graduate Research Fellowship Program
* Amount: $34,000
* Eligibility: Afghan women pursuing graduate studies in STEM fields
* Deadline: October 24
* Official URL: https://www.nsf.gov/funding/pgm_summ.jsp?pims_id=6201
### Microsoft Research Graduate Women's Scholarship
* Amount: $20,000
* Eligibility: Women from Afghanistan pursuing graduate studies in STEM fields
* Deadline: October 15
* Official URL: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/academic-program/microsoft-research-graduate-womens-scholarship/
