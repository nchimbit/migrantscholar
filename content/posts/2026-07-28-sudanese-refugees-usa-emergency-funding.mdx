To qualify for emergency funding, Sudanese refugees in the USA must hold a valid visa, such as an F-1, F-2, J-1, J-2, or asylum status. 1. Review the eligibility criteria for each scholarship to ensure you qualify.
2. Gather all required documents, including your visa, passport, and proof of enrollment.
3. Submit your application, including all supporting documents, before the deadline.
4. Wait for the review process to be completed, which may take several weeks.
5. If selected, receive your award and use it to support your education or emergency needs.
6. Report any changes to your eligibility status, such as a change in visa status.
7. Renew your application, if necessary, to continue receiving funding.
