# UBC Scholarships
The University of British Columbia Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program offers African migrants in Canada a funding amount of up to $25,000 per year, who are pursuing a Master's degree at the University of British Columbia. 

## Who Qualifies
African migrants with a valid Canadian study permit, work permit, or permanent resident visa, such as those with a Temporary Resident Visa, Study Permit, or Express Entry visa, can apply for the University of British Columbia scholarships. 

### University of British Columbia Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program
Amount: $25,000 per year
Eligibility: African migrants pursuing a Master's degree at the University of British Columbia
Deadline: December 11, 2026
Official URL: https://www.ubc.ca/study/international/scholarships-awards-scholars/mastercard-foundation-scholars-program

### Canada Graduate Scholarships-Master's Program
Amount: $17,500
Eligibility: African migrants pursuing a Master's degree in Canada
Deadline: December 1, 2026
Official URL: https://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/Students-Etudiants/CGSM-BESCM_eng.asp

### Ontario Graduate Scholarship
Amount: $15,000
Eligibility: African migrants pursuing a Master's or PhD degree in Ontario
Deadline: October 1, 2026
Official URL: https://www.ontario.ca/page/ontario-graduate-scholarship

### Pierre Elliott Trudeau Foundation Scholarship
Amount: $60,000
Eligibility: African migrants pursuing a PhD degree in Canada
Deadline: December 21, 2026
Official URL: https://www.trudeaufoundation.ca/en/programs/scholarships

### Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarships
Amount: $50,000 per year
Eligibility: African migrants pursuing a PhD degree in Canada
Deadline: November 1, 2026
Official URL: https://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/Students-Etudiants/VanierCGS/Vanier/index_eng.asp

## Application Steps
1. Check the eligibility criteria for each scholarship.
2. Gather all required documents, including transcripts and language proficiency test scores.
3. Submit an application for admission to the University of British Columbia.
4. Complete the scholarship application form and submit all required documents.
5. Ensure that the application is complete and submitted by the deadline.
6. Wait for the scholarship committee to review the application.
7. Receive an email notification of the scholarship decision.
8. Accept the scholarship offer and complete any additional requirements.

## Documents Checklist
- Transcripts from all previous institutions attended
- Language proficiency test scores, such as IELTS or TOEFL
- Study permit or work permit
- Passport
- Proof of financial support
