## Who Qualifies
* Yemeni refugees with valid refugee status (including asylum status, refugee status, or temporary protected status)
* Individuals who have been granted humanitarian parole
* Yemeni nationals who have been granted special immigrant visas (SIVs)
* Family members of eligible individuals, including spouses, unmarried children under 21, and parents of unmarried children under 21

## What the Scholarship Covers
The emergency funding for Yemeni refugees in the USA covers various expenses, including:
* Housing and transportation costs
* Food and clothing expenses
* Medical expenses, including doctor visits, hospital stays, and prescription medication
* Education and training expenses, including tuition, fees, and books
* Employment-related expenses, including job training, resume building, and interview preparation

## How to Apply
1. Check your eligibility for emergency funding by contacting the U.S. Department of State or the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services.
2. Gather required documents, including proof of refugee status, identification, and income verification.
3. Complete the application form, which can be obtained from the U.S. Department of State or the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services.
4. Submit the application form and supporting documents to the relevant agency.
5. Wait for a decision on your application, which may take several weeks or months.
6. If approved, receive funding and use it for eligible expenses.
7. Report any changes in your circumstances, including income, employment, or family status.
8. Renew your funding as needed, by submitting a new application and supporting documents.

## Required Documents
* Proof of refugee status (including asylum status, refugee status, or temporary protected status)
* Identification (including passport, driver's license, or state ID)
* Income verification (including pay stubs, tax returns, or proof of public benefits)
