## Who Qualifies
To qualify for emergency funding, Afghan refugees must have a valid visa, such as an F-1, J-1, or asylum visa. The following visa categories are eligible: F-1, J-1, F-2, J-2, Asylee, Refugee, or Special Immigrant Visa (SIV). Each scholarship has its own set of eligibility criteria, but generally, Afghan refugees with a strong academic record and a desire to pursue higher education in the USA are eligible. Some scholarships also require English language proficiency, which can be demonstrated through [English language tests](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/tests/english-language-tests).

## Application Steps
To apply for emergency funding, Afghan refugees can follow these steps:
1. Research available scholarships and their eligibility criteria
2. Review and meet the eligibility requirements for each scholarship
3. Gather required documents, including academic transcripts and language proficiency test scores
4. Submit applications before the deadline
5. Prepare for and take required tests, such as the GRE or GMAT
6. Secure letters of recommendation from academic or professional mentors
7. Write and submit a personal statement or essay
8. Follow up with the scholarship provider to confirm receipt of the application

## Documents Checklist
To apply for emergency funding, Afghan refugees will need to provide the following documents:
* Academic transcripts
