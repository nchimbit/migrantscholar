### DAAD Scholarship
* Amount: up to €750 per month
* Eligibility: Indian nationals with a bachelor's degree in engineering
* Deadline: varies depending on the course

## Application Steps
1. Research and choose a master's program in engineering at a German university.
2. Check the eligibility criteria for the chosen scholarship.
3. Prepare and submit the application documents, including a resume, statement of purpose, and transcripts.
