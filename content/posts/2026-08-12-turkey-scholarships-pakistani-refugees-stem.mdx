The Turkey Government Scholarship exists and qualifies Pakistani refugees in STEM fields with a funding amount covering tuition fees, accommodation, and living expenses. Pakistanis in Turkey, especially those with a [scholarship for refugees](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-eligibility/refugees) background, can apply for various scholarships offered by the Turkish government. 

## Who Qualifies
The Turkey Government Scholarship is open to Pakistani refugees in Turkey who hold a valid visa, specifically those with international protection status, temporary protection, or humanitarian residence permit. Eligible candidates include those who have completed their previous degree from a Turkish university or have proficiency in Turkish. The scholarships are also open to international students, including [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded) for graduate programs. 

## Available Scholarships
### Turkish Government Scholarship
* Amount: Covers tuition fees, accommodation, and living expenses
* Eligibility: Pakistani refugees with a valid visa, international students
* Deadline: February 20, 2027
* Official URL: https://www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr/

### Kadir Has University Scholarship
* Amount: 100% tuition fee waiver
* Eligibility: Pakistani refugees, international students in STEM fields
* Deadline: June 15, 2027
* Official URL: https://www.khas.edu.tr/en

### Boğaziçi University Scholarship
* Amount: 50% tuition fee waiver
* Eligibility: Pakistani refugees, international students in graduate programs
* Deadline: April 30, 2027
* Official URL: https://www.boun.edu.tr/

### İzmir Institute of Technology Scholarship
* Amount: Covers tuition fees and living expenses
* Eligibility: Pakistani refugees, international students in PhD programs
* Deadline: January 15, 2027
* Official URL: https://www.iyte.edu.tr/

### Middle East Technical University Scholarship
* Amount: 75% tuition fee waiver
* Eligibility: Pakistani refugees, international students in undergraduate programs
* Deadline: May 31, 2027
* Official URL: https://www.metu.edu.tr/

## Application Steps
1. Check the official website of the scholarship for application details and eligibility criteria.
2. Prepare all required documents, including a valid passport, visa, and academic transcripts.
3. Fill out the online application form and submit it before the deadline.
4. Take the required language proficiency test, such as Turkish or English.
5. Submit a motivational letter and a personal statement.
6. Provide two letters of recommendation from academic or professional referees.
7. Apply for a student visa, if required.
8. Confirm the application and wait for the result.
