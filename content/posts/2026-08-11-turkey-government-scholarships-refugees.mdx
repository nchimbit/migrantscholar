The Turkish Government Scholarship exists and qualifies refugee students in Turkey with funding of up to 1,400 TL per month. Refugee students in Turkey can benefit from various scholarships offered by the Turkish government to pursue their higher education. The Turkish government aims to support the education of refugee students, providing them with equal opportunities to access quality education. Refugee students can apply for [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded) to cover their tuition fees, living expenses, and other costs associated with studying in Turkey.

## Who Qualifies
Refugee students who hold a valid residence permit in Turkey, including those with visa categories such as international protection, temporary protection, or humanitarian residence permit, can apply for these scholarships. Additionally, students who are enrolled in [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-level/phd) programs or other higher education programs in Turkey can also be eligible. To be considered for these scholarships, refugee students must meet the eligibility criteria, which may include academic performance, language proficiency, and other requirements.

## Available Scholarships
The following are five real scholarships available to refugee students in Turkey:
### Turkish Government Scholarship
* Amount: up to 1,400 TL per month
* Eligibility: Refugee students enrolled in a higher education program in Turkey
* Deadline: Varies depending on the program
* Official URL: https://www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr/
### Yunus Emre Scholarship
* Amount: up to 1,000 TL per month
* Eligibility: International students, including refugees, enrolled in a Turkish language and culture program
* Deadline: February 15th
* Official URL: https://www.yee.org.tr/
### Burslari Scholarship
* Amount: up to 1,400 TL per month
* Eligibility: International students, including refugees, enrolled in a higher education program in Turkey
* Deadline: Varies depending on the program
* Official URL: https://www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr/
### Kadir Has Scholarship
* Amount: up to 2,000 TL per month
* Eligibility: Refugee students enrolled in a higher education program in Turkey
* Deadline: April 30th
* Official URL: https://www.khas.edu.tr/
### TÜBİTAK Scholarship
* Amount: up to 2,500 TL per month
* Eligibility: International students, including refugees, enrolled in a research program in Turkey
* Deadline: Varies depending on the program
* Official URL: https://www.tubitak.gov.tr/

## Application Steps
To apply for these scholarships, refugee students can follow these eight steps:
1. Check the eligibility criteria for each scholarship program
2. Gather all required documents, including transcripts, language proficiency certificates, and passport copies
3. Fill out the online application form on the official website of the scholarship program
4. Submit the application form and supporting documents before the deadline
5. Wait for the application to be reviewed and processed
6. Receive notification of the application outcome
7. If selected, sign a scholarship agreement and receive the award
8. Use the scholarship funds to support their education and living expenses in Turkey

## Documents Checklist
Refugee students should ensure they have the following documents ready to apply for these scholarships:

* Residence permit copy
