Fully Funded Nursing Masters for Refugees in Germany  
====================================================

The **BMBF Scholarship for Refugees in Germany** offers a fully funded Master’s in Nursing program, covering tuition, a monthly stipend of €1 200, and travel costs, to eligible refugees holding a recognized refugee status or subsidiary protection.  

## Who qualifies  
The scholarship is open to individuals who:

- possess a valid **refugee status** (Geflüchteter) or **subsidiary protection** (unterstützter Schutz) in Germany,
- hold a **residence permit for studies** (Studienaufenthalt) or an **EU Blue Card** if they are from a non‑EU country,
- have completed a **Bachelor’s degree in Nursing** or a related health science field,
- meet the language requirements of the host university (usually B2 in German or C1 in English).

## Eight steps to apply

1. **Research the universities** that offer a Master’s in Nursing in your language of choice and confirm they accept the scholarship you aim to apply for.  
2. **Gather your academic transcripts** from your undergraduate program and get them officially translated into German or English.  
3. **Prepare the language test results** (DSH, TestDaF, IELTS, TOEFL) and include them in your application dossier.  
4. **Write a compelling motivation letter** that explains why you want to study nursing in Germany and how the scholarship will help you.  
5. **Collect proof of your refugee status** (Ausweis, Aufenthaltstitel, or a letter from the Federal Office for Migration and Refugees).  
6. **Complete the scholarship application form** on the scholarship’s official website, attaching all required documents.  
7. **Submit the application before the deadline**; keep copies of everything you send and confirm receipt with the scholarship office.  
8. **Prepare for an interview** (many scholarships require a brief video or phone interview to assess motivation and language skills).

## Documents checklist

- Official university transcript (German/English translation)  
- Bachelor’s degree diploma (German/English translation)  
- Language proficiency certificates (DSH, TestDaF, IELTS, TOEFL, or equivalent)  
- Passport and refugee or subsidiary protection documents  
- Proof of residence permit for studies or EU Blue Card
