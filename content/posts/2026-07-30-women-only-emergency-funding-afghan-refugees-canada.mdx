# Funding
The [Margaret McNamara Memorial Fund](https://www.mmmf-grants.org/) scholarship exists for women from developing countries, including Afghan refugees in Canada, and provides up to $15,000 in funding. 

## Who Qualifies
Afghan refugee women in Canada who hold a [Student Visa](https://migrantscholar.com/visa-categories/student-visa) or [Refugee Visa](https://migrantscholar.com/visa-categories/refugee-visa) are eligible to apply for women-only emergency funding. Additionally, women with [Work Visa](https://migrantscholar.com/visa-categories/work-visa) or [Family Visa](https://migrantscholar.com/visa-categories/family-visa) may also be considered if they can demonstrate a strong need for financial assistance.
