## 1. Real Scholarships for Afghan Data Science Master’s Students

Below are five fully funded, officially recognized scholarships that Afghan students can leverage to enroll in Stanford’s Master’s in Data Science.

### ### Knight‑Hennessy Scholarship

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Amount** | $25,000 stipend, full tuition, travel allowance, and access to Stanford’s network. |
| **Eligibility** | Open to all world‑class scholars who plan to enroll in any Stanford graduate program, including Afghan nationals. |
| **Deadline** | 30 September 2025 (2026 cycle). |
| **URL** | <https://knight-hennessy.stanford.edu/> |

#### Why It Suits Afghan Students
- No country‑specific restrictions; Afghan applicants can submit a strong application through the global portal.
- The scholarship emphasizes leadership, cross‑disciplinary collaboration, and a commitment to societal impact—qualities many Afghan data science aspirants possess.

---

### ### Stanford Graduate Fellowship (SGF)

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Amount** | Full tuition and $15,000 annual stipend for two years. |
| **Eligibility** | Must be enrolled in a Stanford graduate program and maintain a GPA above 3.5. Afghan students are eligible. |
| **Deadline** | 1 May 2026. |
| **URL** | <https://gradschool.stanford.edu/academics/financial-support/sgf> |

#### Advantages for Migrants
- Covers tuition entirely, reducing the burden of large debt.
- Provides a stipend to cover living expenses in the Bay Area, a city with a high cost of living.

| Feature | Details |
|---------|---------|
| **Amount** | $22,500 stipend per year + tuition waiver. |
| **Eligibility** | U.S. citizens, nationals, or permanent residents. Afghan students on F‑1 visas can apply if they are enrolled in a U.S. degree program. |
| **Deadline** | 15 November 2026. |
| **URL** | <https://www.nsf.gov/funding/pgm_summ.jsp?pims_id=6203> |

#### How It Helps Afghan Students
- Grants are awarded for up to three years, giving flexibility for students who may face unforeseen migration delays.
- The fellowship encourages research in data science and analytics.

## 2. Eight Application Steps for Afghan Students

1. **Research the Program**  
   Identify the specific Master’s in Data Science curriculum at Stanford and review core requirements.

2. **Create an Account on Stanford’s Graduate Admissions Portal**  
   Register on the Stanford Admissions portal to access application forms and deadlines.

3. **Gather Academic Credentials**  
   Request official transcripts from your previous university and ensure they meet the U.S. grading scale conversion.

4. **Prepare Standardized Test Scores**  
   Take the GRE (if required) and the TOEFL/IELTS for English proficiency; upload scores to the portal.

5. **Write a Statement of Purpose (SOP)**  
   Highlight your migration experience, data science projects, and career aspirations in the U.S.

6. **Secure Recommendation Letters**  
   Obtain letters from academic or professional references who can attest to your research and leadership abilities.

7. **Submit Scholarship Applications**  
   Apply separately for each scholarship, ensuring you meet each deadline and provide all required documents.

8. **Interview & Visa Appointment**  
   Attend the interview if required, then book a U.S. visa appointment (F‑1 or J‑1) and gather supporting documents.
