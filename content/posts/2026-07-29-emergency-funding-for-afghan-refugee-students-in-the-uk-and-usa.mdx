## Who Qualifies
To qualify for emergency funding, Afghan refugee students in the UK and USA typically need to hold specific visa categories, such as the UK's Refugee Leave or Humanitarian Protection, or the USA's Refugee Admission or Special Immigrant Visa. 
These visa categories are crucial for accessing [Canada scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/countries/Canada) and other international funding opportunities. 
Additionally, students may need to demonstrate financial hardship and be enrolled in or accepted to a UK or US university.
