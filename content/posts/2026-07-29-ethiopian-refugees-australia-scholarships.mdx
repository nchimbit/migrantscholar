## Who Qualifies
Ethiopian refugees with a valid Australian visa, such as a Permanent Humanitarian Visa (subclass 201), a Protection Visa (subclass 866), or a Temporary Protection Visa (subclass 785), may be eligible for Australian Government Scholarships. 
Refugees must meet specific eligibility criteria, including language proficiency, academic requirements, and residency obligations. 
It is essential to review the eligibility criteria for each scholarship program to determine which ones they may be eligible for, such as [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-level/phd) or [Canada scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/countries/Canada).

## Frequently Asked Questions
1. What is the eligibility criteria for Australian Government Scholarships?
Answer: The eligibility criteria vary depending on the scholarship program, but generally, applicants must hold a valid Australian visa, meet language proficiency requirements, and have a good academic record.
2. Can I apply for multiple scholarship programs at the same time?
Answer: Yes, applicants can apply for multiple scholarship programs, but they must meet the eligibility criteria for each program.
3. What is the difference between a Permanent Humanitarian Visa and a Protection Visa?
Answer: A Permanent Humanitarian Visa is a permanent visa that allows refugees to live and work in Australia, while a Protection Visa is a temporary visa that provides protection to refugees who are in Australia and cannot return to their home country.
4. How do I apply for admission to an Australian university or higher education institution?
Answer: Applicants can apply for admission through the university's website or through a registered education agent.
5. What is the deadline for submitting the scholarship application?
Answer: The deadline varies depending on the scholarship program, but it is usually between March and October each year.
6. Can I apply for a scholarship if I have a Temporary Protection Visa?
Answer: Yes, applicants with a Temporary Protection Visa may be eligible for certain scholarship programs, but they must meet the eligibility criteria and have a good academic record.
7. How long does it take to receive the outcome of the scholarship application?
Answer: The processing time varies depending on the scholarship program, but it can take several months to receive the outcome.
8. What happens if I am awarded a scholarship and then my visa is cancelled?
Answer: If the visa is cancelled, the scholarship award may be revoked, and the recipient may need to return any funds received.
