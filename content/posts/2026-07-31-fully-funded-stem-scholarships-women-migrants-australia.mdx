The Australian Government's [Women in STEM and Entrepreneurship](https://www.australiaawards.gov.au/News/Pages/Women-in-STEM-and-Entrepreneurship-grants.aspx) scholarship exists and qualifies women migrants in Australia with up to $100,000 in funding.
The Women in STEM and Entrepreneurship program supports women in STEM fields to pursue higher education and entrepreneurship opportunities.
Australia offers various fully funded STEM scholarships for women migrants, including [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded) to support their education and career goals.

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for fully funded STEM scholarships in Australia, women migrants must hold a valid Australian visa, such as a permanent resident visa, temporary graduate visa, or a bridging visa.
Eligible visa categories include:
- Permanent Resident visa
- Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485)
- Bridging visa
- Skilled Independent visa (subclass 189)
- Skilled Nominated visa (subclass 190)
Women with [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-eligibility/refugees) background are also encouraged to apply.
