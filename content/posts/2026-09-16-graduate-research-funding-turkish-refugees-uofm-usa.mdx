# Graduate Research Grants for Turkish Refugees at U of M  

**The University of Michigan Global Scholars Program** awards up to **$18,000 per year** to international graduate students, including Turkish refugees, covering tuition and living expenses.  

## Who Qualifies  
The scholarships listed below are available to Turkish refugees currently residing in the United States on any of the following visa categories:

1. **F‑1** (Student) – holding a valid U‑S student visa.  
2. **B‑1/B‑2** (Visitor) – temporary visitors who have been granted a U‑S visa for academic purposes.  
3. **D P** (Special immigrant status for refugees and asylum seekers).  
4. **K‑1** (Fiancé(e) of a U‑S citizen) – if the applicant is pursuing graduate study while in the U‑S.  
5. **H‑1B** (Specialty occupation) – holders who are also enrolled in graduate programs.  

Applicants must be enrolled or accepted into a graduate program at the University of Michigan (U of M) and demonstrate strong academic credentials.  

## Five Real Scholarships for Graduate Research Funding  

### 1. University of Michigan Global Scholars Program  
- **Amount**: Up to **$18,000** per academic year (tuition waiver + $6,000 stipend).  
- **Eligibility**: International graduate students admitted to U of M, including refugees, with an excellent academic record.  
- **Deadline**: **September 1, 2026** (Rolling review).  
- **Official URL**: https://scholars.umich.edu/global-scholars  

### 2. Fulbright Foreign Student Program (FFSP)  
- **Amount**: Up to **$30,000** per academic year for tuition, living stipend, and travel.  
- **Eligibility**: Turkish nationals or refugees with a U S student visa (F‑1) applying to a graduate program at an accredited U S university.  
- **Deadline**: **May 15, 2026**.  
- **Official URL**: https://foreign.fulbrightonline.org/about/foreign-students  

### 3. U.S. Department of State International Graduate Fellowship Program (IGFP)  
- **Amount**: **$20,000** per year (tuition credit + stipend).  
- **Eligibility**: Graduate students from developing countries (Turkey qualifies) who hold a valid F‑1 visa and are enrolled in a U S program. Refugee status is accepted.  
- **Deadline**: **August 31, 2026**.  
- **Official URL**: https://www.state.gov/international-graduate-fellowship-program-igfp/  

### 4. National Science Foundation Graduate Research Fellowship Program (NSF GRFP)  
- **Amount**: **$34,000** stipend + tuition credit per year for up to **3 years**.  
- **Eligibility**: Graduate students in STEM fields, holding an F‑1 visa, and including refugees from Turkey.  
- **Deadline**: **December 1, 2026**.  
- **Official URL**: https://www.nsf.gov/funding/pgm_summ.jsp?pims_id=6203  

### 5. Ford Foundation Fellowship Programs (Ford Graduate Fellowship)  
- **Amount**: **$20,000** stipend per year.  
- **Eligibility**: Graduate students from underrepresented backgrounds, including refugees, who are U S citizens or legal residents.  
- **Deadline**: **October 31, 2026**.  
- **Official URL**: https://www.fordfoundation.org/work/our-grants/ford-graduate-fellowship-program  

> **Tip**: Many of these programs require a separate U S visa application. Apply early to avoid missing the scholarship deadlines.
