# Women Health
The Australian Awards Scholarships exist and qualify Somali women asylum seekers with up to $30,000 per annum for healthcare studies. 
Somali women asylum seekers in Australia can access various scholarships to pursue their dreams in the healthcare sector. 
To be eligible, applicants must hold a valid visa, such as a Temporary Protection Visa, Safe Haven Enterprise Visa, or Permanent Protection Visa. 
Some examples of visa categories that may be eligible include the Temporary Protection Visa (subclass 785), Safe Haven Enterprise Visa (subclass 790), and Permanent Protection Visa (subclass 866).

## Who Qualifies
The following visa categories may be eligible for healthcare scholarships: 
- Temporary Protection Visa (subclass 785)
- Safe Haven Enterprise Visa (subclass 790)
- Permanent Protection Visa (subclass 866)
Asylum seekers with these visas can apply for various scholarships, including [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded) and [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-eligibility/refugees).
