# Canada PR
The Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarship, which qualifies PhD students with a minimum GPA of 3.5/4.0, provides funding of up to $50,000 per year, and Turkish nationals looking to immigrate to Canada can explore various visa options to achieve permanent residency.

## Who Qualifies
Turkish nationals can apply for permanent residency in Canada through various visa categories, including the Federal Skilled Worker Program, Federal Skilled Trades Program, and Canadian Experience Class. The Express Entry system is a popular pathway for skilled workers, while family sponsorship and refugee programs also provide opportunities for eligible individuals.

## Scholarships for Turkish Nationals
### 1. Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarship 
The Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarship provides up to $50,000 per year for three years to PhD students with a minimum GPA of 3.5/4.0, and the deadline is November 1, 2026, [https://www.vanier.gc.ca](https://www.vanier.gc.ca).
### 2. Ontario Graduate Scholarship 
The Ontario Graduate Scholarship offers up to $15,000 per year for two years to master's and PhD students with a minimum GPA of 3.5/4.0, and the deadline is October 15, 2026, [https://osap.gov.on.ca](https://osap.gov.on.ca).
### 3. Trudeau Scholarship 
The Trudeau Scholarship provides up to $60,000 per year for three years to PhD students with a minimum GPA of 3.5/4.0, and the deadline is December 1, 2026, [https://www.trudeaufoundation.ca](https://www.trudeaufoundation.ca).
### 4. Banting Postdoctoral Fellowship 
The Banting Postdoctoral Fellowship offers up to $70,000 per year for two years to postdoctoral researchers with a minimum GPA of 3.5/4.0, and the deadline is September 21, 2026, [https://banting.fellowships-bourses.gc.ca](https://banting.fellowships-bourses.gc.ca).
### 5. SSHRC Doctoral Fellowship 
The SSHRC Doctoral Fellowship provides up to $20,000 per year for three years to PhD students with a minimum GPA of 3.5/4.0, and the deadline is October 17, 2026, [https://www.sshrc-crsh.gc.ca](https://www.sshrc-crsh.gc.ca).

## Application Steps
1. Determine eligibility for a visa category, such as the [Federal Skilled Worker Program](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/countries/Canada) or [Canadian Experience Class](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/countries/Canada).
2. Create an Express Entry profile, if applicable.
3. Gather required documents, including language test results and education credentials.
4. Submit an application through the Express Entry system or directly to Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC).
5. Pay the required application fees.
6. Wait for the application to be processed and a decision to be made.
7. If approved, apply for a permanent resident visa.
8. Complete a medical examination and obtain police certificates, if required.

## Frequently Asked Questions
1. What are the eligibility requirements for the Express Entry system?
Answer: candidates must meet the eligibility requirements for one of the three federal immigration programs: Federal Skilled Worker Program, Federal Skilled Trades Program, or Canadian Experience Class.
2. How long does it take to process an Express Entry application?
Answer: processing times vary depending on the complexity of the application, but most applications are processed within six months.
3. What is the minimum language proficiency requirement for Express Entry?
Answer: candidates must have a minimum language proficiency of Canadian Language Benchmark (CLB) 7 in all four skills: reading, writing, speaking, and listening.
4. Can I apply for permanent residency through multiple visa categories?
Answer: yes, but each application must be submitted separately and meet the eligibility requirements for the respective category.
5. How do I know if I am eligible for a [fully funded scholarship](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded) in Canada?
Answer: check the eligibility criteria for each scholarship, such as the [Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarship](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/countries/Canada).
6. What is the difference between a [PhD scholarship](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-level/phd) and a postdoctoral fellowship?
Answer: a PhD scholarship is for students pursuing a doctoral degree, while a postdoctoral fellowship is for researchers who have completed their PhD.
7. Can refugees apply for permanent residency in Canada?
Answer: yes, [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-eligibility/refugees) are available, and refugees can also apply for permanent residency through the Refugee and Humanitarian Resettlement Program.
8. How do I find [scholarship deadlines](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/deadlines) for Canadian scholarships?
Answer: check the official websites of the scholarship providers, such as the [Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarship](https://www.vanier.gc.ca) or [SSHRC Doctoral Fellowship](https://www.sshrc-crsh.gc.ca).
