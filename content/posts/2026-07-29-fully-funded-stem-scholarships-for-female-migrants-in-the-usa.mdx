# STEM Scholarships
The [Society of Women Engineers Scholarship](https://swe.org/scholarships/) exists and qualifies female migrant students pursuing STEM fields in the USA, offering funding amounts ranging from $1,000 to $10,000.

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for these STEM scholarships, migrant women must hold valid visa categories such as F-1, J-1, or have a Green Card, and be enrolled in a STEM program at an accredited institution in the USA.

## Available Scholarships
### National Science Foundation Graduate Research Fellowship Program
* Amount: $34,000
* Eligibility: Female migrant students pursuing a research-based master's or doctoral degree in STEM fields
* Deadline: October 2026
* Official URL: [https://www.nsfgrfp.org/](https://www.nsfgrfp.org/)
### Microsoft Scholarship
* Amount: $20,000
* Eligibility: Female migrant students pursuing a undergraduate or graduate degree in STEM fields
* Deadline: March 2026
* Official URL: [https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/diversity/programs/microsoft-scholarship](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/diversity/programs/microsoft-scholarship)
### Google Anita Borg Memorial Scholarship
* Amount: $10,000
* Eligibility: Female migrant students pursuing a undergraduate or graduate degree in computer science or related STEM fields
* Deadline: February 2026
* Official URL: [https://anitab.org/showcase/scholarship/](https://anitab.org/showcase/scholarship/)
### American Association of University Women International Fellowship
* Amount: $18,000
* Eligibility: Female migrant students pursuing a graduate degree in STEM fields
* Deadline: November 2026
* Official URL: [https://www.aauw.org/what-we-do/educational-funding-and-awards/international-fellowships/](https://www.aauw.org/what-we-do/educational-funding-and-awards/international-fellowships/)
### P.E.O. International Peace Scholarship
* Amount: $12,500
* Eligibility: Female migrant students pursuing a graduate degree in STEM fields
* Deadline: December 2026
* Official URL: [https://www.peointernational.org/ipe-scholarship](https://www.peointernational.org/ipe-scholarship)

## Application Steps
1. Review the eligibility criteria for each scholarship
