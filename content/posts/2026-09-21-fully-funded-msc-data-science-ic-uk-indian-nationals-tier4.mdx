The Imperial College London International Scholarship Programme awards £20,000 to Indian nationals on a Tier 4 visa for a fully funded MSc in Data Science.

## Who Qualifies

| Visa Category | Eligibility Criteria | Notes |
|----------------|----------------------|-------|
| Tier 4 (General) | • Indian citizen<br>• Accepted into the MSc Data Science programme at Imperial College London<br>• Meet UK visa requirements (English proficiency, maintenance funds, etc.) | The scholarship covers tuition fees, a £12,000 annual living stipend, and a one‑way return airfare. |
| Tier 4 (Student) | Same as above | |
| Post‑Graduate Tier 4 | Eligible if you have completed an undergraduate degree in a related field (Computer Science, Mathematics, Statistics) and hold an offer for the MSc. | |

Students who hold a valid Indian passport, a confirmed place in the MSc Data Science course, and meet the standard UK visa criteria are eligible. Applicants must submit evidence of academic excellence (GPA > 3.5 / 70 % or equivalent), a research proposal, and a strong personal statement.

## Five Real Scholarships

### 1. Imperial College London International Scholarship Programme
- **Amount:** £20,000 (covers tuition and £12,000 living stipend)  
- **Eligibility:** Indian nationals on Tier 4 visa accepted into any MSc programme, including Data Science.  
- **Deadline:** 1 September 2026 (varies by programme)  
- **Official URL:** https://www.imperial.ac.uk/study/masters/international-scholarships/

### 2. Imperial College London Global Excellence Scholarship
- **Amount:** £30,000 (covers tuition and £15,000 stipend)  
- **Eligibility:** Indian students with an outstanding academic record applying to graduate courses such as MSc Data Science.  
- **Deadline:** 31 August 2026  
- **Official URL:** https://www.imperial.ac.uk/scholarships/global-excellence-scholarship/

### 3. Chevening Scholarship
- **Amount:** Fully funded (tuition, living stipend, travel) – £28,000 per year  
- **Eligibility:** Indian citizens with a strong leadership profile, academic excellence, and a clear post‑study plan in India.  
- **Deadline:** 31 May 2026 (early‑bird) / 30 June 2026 (late)  
- **Official URL:** https://www.chevening.org/scholarships/

### 4. Commonwealth Scholarship & Fellowship Plan (CSFP)
- **Amount:** Fully funded (tuition, living stipend, travel) – £25,000 per year  
- **Eligibility:** Indian nationals with a first‑class undergraduate degree applying to a UK master's.  
- **Deadline:** 30 June 2026  
- **Official URL:** https://www.cscuk.org/

### 5. UKRI EPSRC Postgraduate Studentship
- **Amount:** £15,000–£25,000 (depends on project) covering tuition and living costs  
- **Eligibility:** Indian applicants with a strong research proposal in Data Science or Machine Learning.  
- **Deadline:** Continuous (apply as soon as your project is ready)  
- **Official URL:** https://www.ukri.org/grants/epsrc/

## Eight Application Steps

1. **Secure Your Offer** – Apply to Imperial College London’s MSc Data Science and receive an unconditional offer.  
2. **Choose Your Scholarship** – Review the list above and select the scholarship that best matches your profile and deadline.  
3. **Prepare Academic Credentials** – Obtain certified copies of your degree certificate, transcripts, and, if applicable, a research proposal.  
4. **Gather English Proficiency Proof** – IELTS/TOEFL/IELTS with at least 7.0 overall for Imperial; 6.5 for Chevening.  
5. **Compile Personal Statement & Leadership Profile** – Highlight your achievements, career goals, and why you chose Data Science.  
6. **Arrange Reference Letters** – Two academic referees (ideally from your undergraduate institution) and a professional referee (if available).  
7. **Submit Scholarship Application** – Complete the online form, upload required documents, and pay any application fee (e.g., Chevening £20).  
8. **Interview & Decision** – Attend the scholarship interview (if required) and await the result; accept the offer and secure funding before the Tier 4 visa application.

## Eight FAQs (Migrant‑Specific)

1. **Can I apply for a scholarship after receiving my university offer?**  
   Yes, most scholarships (Imperial, Chevening, CSFP) accept applications post‑offer, but deadlines may differ. Keep a timeline and submit early to secure funding.

2. **Do I need to be a UK resident to claim the Imperial Scholarship?**  
   No. Indian nationals on a Tier 4 visa are eligible; the scholarship does not require prior UK residency.

3. **How does the scholarship cover living expenses?**  
   The stipend is paid monthly via a UK bank account or an equivalent. If you open a UK account, the scholarship will transfer funds directly.

4. **Will my scholarship affect my Tier 4 visa status?**  
   No. Scholarships are considered part of your tuition fee payment and living expenses; they do not alter your visa category.

   Most scholarships require a signed acceptance; if you must withdraw, contact the scholarship provider immediately to understand the refund policy.
