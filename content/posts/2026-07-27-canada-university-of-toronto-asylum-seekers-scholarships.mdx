The University of Toronto's [President's Award of Excellence](https://www.utsc.utoronto.ca/registrar/scholarships-president-award-excellence) offers $10,000 to asylum seekers from Africa in Canada. 
The University of Toronto offers various scholarships to support students with refugee status, including asylum seekers from Africa. 
To be eligible for these scholarships, applicants must have a valid Canadian study permit or have been granted refugee status in Canada. 

## Who Qualifies
Asylum seekers from Africa who are currently residing in Canada may be eligible for University of Toronto scholarships. 
The following visa categories may be eligible: 
- Protected persons
- Convention refugees
- Humanitarian-protected persons abroad
- Privately sponsored refugees
- Government-assisted refugees
