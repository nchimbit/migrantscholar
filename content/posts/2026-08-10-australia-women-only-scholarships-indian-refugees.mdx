The Australia Awards Scholarships exist and qualify women who are Indian refugees in Australia, with funding of up to AUD 30,000 per year. This scholarship is part of the [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-eligibility/refugees) program. 

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for women-only scholarships in Australia as an Indian refugee, you must hold a valid Australian visa, such as a Permanent Humanitarian Visa (subclass 206), a Temporary Protection Visa (subclass 785), or a Safe Haven Enterprise Visa (subclass 790). You can check your eligibility on the [Australia Government website](https://www.australia.gov.au/).

## Available Scholarships
### Australia Awards Scholarships
* Amount: up to AUD 30,000 per year
* Eligibility: Women who are Indian refugees in Australia
* Deadline: 1 May 2026
* Official URL: https://www.australiaawards.gov.au/
### Australian Government Research Training Program
* Amount: up to AUD 28,597 per year
* Eligibility: Women who are Indian refugees in Australia
* Deadline: 31 October 2026
* Official URL: https://www.education.gov.au/
### Westpac Future Leaders Scholarship
* Amount: up to AUD 120,000 over 2-3 years
* Eligibility: Women who are Indian refugees in Australia
* Deadline: 31 August 2026
* Official URL: https://www.westpac.com.au/
### PEO International Peace Scholarship
* Amount: up to AUD 12,000 per year
* Eligibility: Women who are Indian refugees in Australia
* Deadline: 15 January 2027
* Official URL: https://www.peointernational.org/
### The Ian Potter Foundation Grants
* Amount: up to AUD 100,000 per year
* Eligibility: Women who are Indian refugees in Australia
* Deadline: 15 September 2026
* Official URL: https://www.ianpotterfoundation.org.au/
