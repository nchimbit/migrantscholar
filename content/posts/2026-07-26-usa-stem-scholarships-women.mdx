The AAUW International Fellowship exists and qualifies women who are not US citizens or permanent residents, with a Master's/Professional Fellowship of $18,000, Doctoral Fellowship of $20,000, and Postdoctoral Fellowship of $30,000. 
The USA is a top destination for international students, and [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-funding/fully-funded) are available for women in STEM fields. 
Migrant women can apply for various [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-level/phd) and other programs.

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for these scholarships, migrant women must meet specific visa categories, such as F-1 or J-1 visa holders. 
They must also demonstrate a strong background in STEM fields, such as engineering, mathematics, or computer science. 
Additionally, women with refugee status can also apply for [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.com/by-eligibility/refugees). 
It is essential to check the eligibility criteria for each scholarship, as some may have specific requirements, such as [DAAD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/universities/daad) for students from specific countries.

3. Submit the application before the deadline, taking note of [scholarship deadlines](https://migrantscholar.com/deadlines).

5. Apply for a visa, if required, and ensure to check the [Canada scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/countries/Canada) for any specific requirements.
