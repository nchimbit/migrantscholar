# Africa Scholar
The Heinrich Boll Foundation Scholarship exists for African asylum seekers, providing funding of up to €1,000 per month. 
As an African female asylum seeker in Germany, you may be eligible for various women-only healthcare scholarships to pursue your higher education. 
These scholarships aim to support your academic and professional development in the field of healthcare. 
To be eligible for these scholarships, you must hold a valid visa, such as a residence permit or a visa for humanitarian reasons.

## Who Qualifies
The following visa categories are eligible for women-only healthcare scholarships in Germany: 
- Subsidiary protection 
- Refugee status 
- Family reunification 
- Humanitarian visa 
You must also meet specific eligibility criteria, such as being a female asylum seeker from Africa, having a strong academic record, and demonstrating a commitment to the healthcare field.

## Available Scholarships
### DAAD Scholarship for Healthcare Professionals
The DAAD Scholarship for Healthcare Professionals offers up to €750 per month, and is eligible for African female asylum seekers with a Bachelor's degree in a healthcare-related field. 
The deadline for application is October 31st, and you can find more information on the [official DAAD website](https://www.daad.de/en/).
### Kofi Annan Fellowship
The Kofi Annan Fellowship provides funding of up to €1,500 per month, and is open to African female asylum seekers with a strong academic record and a commitment to healthcare. 
The deadline for application is January 15th, and you can find more information on the [official ESMT website](https://www.esmt.org/kofi-annan-fellowship).
### Erasmus Mundus Scholarship
The Erasmus Mundus Scholarship offers up to €1,000 per month, and is eligible for African female asylum seekers with a Bachelor's degree in a healthcare-related field. 
The deadline for application is December 15th, and you can find more information on the [official European Commission website](https://ec.europa.eu/programmes/erasmus-mundus/).
### Friedrich Ebert Foundation Scholarship
The Friedrich Ebert Foundation Scholarship provides funding of up to €1,200 per month, and is open to African female asylum seekers with a strong academic record and a commitment to social justice. 
The deadline for application is August 31st, and you can find more information on the [official Friedrich Ebert Foundation website](https://www.fes.de/en/scholarship).
### Rosa Luxemburg Foundation Scholarship
The Rosa Luxemburg Foundation Scholarship offers up to €1,300 per month, and is eligible for African female asylum seekers with a Bachelor's degree in a healthcare-related field. 
The deadline for application is April 30th, and you can find more information on the [official Rosa Luxemburg Foundation website](https://www.rosalux.de/en/scholarship).
