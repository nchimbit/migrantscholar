Iraqi nationals who hold an Australian Humanitarian Visa can apply for various scholarships to pursue higher education at the Australian National University. ## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for an Australian Humanitarian Visa, Iraqi nationals must meet specific criteria, including holding a valid visa, such as a Temporary Protection Visa (TPV) or a Safe Haven Enterprise Visa (SHEV). The main visa categories for Iraqi nationals include:
- Permanent Protection Visa (PPV)
- Temporary Protection Visa (TPV)
- Safe Haven Enterprise Visa (SHEV)
- Humanitarian Stay Visa
