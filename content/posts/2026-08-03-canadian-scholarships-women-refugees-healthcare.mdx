## Who Qualifies
Women refugees who hold a valid Canadian visa, such as a Permanent Resident Visa or a Protected Person Visa, can apply for these scholarships. Additionally, women who are Convention Refugees or have been granted asylum in Canada may also be eligible. ## Available Scholarships
### The Margaret McMillan Memorial Scholarship
The Margaret McMillan Memorial Scholarship offers up to $10,000 to women refugees pursuing healthcare careers. Eligibility includes being a Canadian citizen or permanent resident, and being enrolled in a healthcare program. The deadline is May 15th, and the official URL is https://www.cpa.ca/mcmillan. 

### The Canadian Federation of University Women (CFUW) Fellowship
The CFUW Fellowship offers up to $10,000 to women pursuing graduate studies in any field, including healthcare. Eligibility includes being a Canadian citizen or permanent resident, and being enrolled in a graduate program. The deadline is November 1st, and the official URL is https://cfuwsc.ca/fellowships-awards/. 

### The Ontario Graduate Scholarship (OGS) Program
The OGS Program offers up to $15,000 to graduate students, including women refugees, pursuing studies in any field, including healthcare. Eligibility includes being a Canadian citizen or permanent resident, and being enrolled in a graduate program at an Ontario university. The deadline is October 1st, and the official URL is https://osap.gov.on.ca/eng/notices/ogsfrench.htm. 

### The Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarship
The Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarship offers up to $50,000 per year for three years to doctoral students, including women refugees, pursuing studies in any field, including healthcare. Eligibility includes being a Canadian citizen or permanent resident, and being enrolled in a [PhD program](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-level/phd) at a Canadian university. The deadline is November 1st, and the official URL is https://vanier.gc.ca/en/home-accueil.html. 

### The Pierre Elliott Trudeau Foundation Scholarship
The Pierre Elliott Trudeau Foundation Scholarship offers up to $60,000 per year for three years to doctoral students, including women refugees, pursuing studies in any field, including healthcare. Eligibility includes being a Canadian citizen or permanent resident, and being enrolled in a [PhD program](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-level/phd) at a Canadian university.
