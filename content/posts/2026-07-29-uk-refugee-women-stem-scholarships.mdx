# STEM Scholarships
The Felix Scholarship qualifies refugee women for a fully funded £18,297 award to pursue a master's degree in a STEM field in the UK. 

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for these scholarships, applicants must be refugee women with a valid UK visa, including but not limited to, asylum seekers, humanitarian protection, or family reunion visa holders. They must also meet the specific eligibility criteria for each scholarship.

## Available Scholarships
### The Felix Scholarship
* Amount: £18,297
* Eligibility: Refugee women pursuing a master's degree in a STEM field
* Deadline: January 2027
* Official URL: https://www.felixscholarship.org/
### The British Council Scholarship for Women in STEM
* Amount: Up to £35,000
* Eligibility: Female international students, including refugees, pursuing a master's degree in a STEM field
* Deadline: February 2027
* Official URL: https://www.britishcouncil.org/
### The Commonwealth Scholarship
* Amount: Full tuition fees and living expenses
* Eligibility: Refugee women who are Commonwealth citizens pursuing a master's or PhD in a STEM field
* Deadline: December 2026
* Official URL: https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/
### The Imperial College London President's PhD Scholarships
* Amount: Full tuition fees and living expenses
* Eligibility: Refugee women pursuing a PhD in a STEM field
* Deadline: January 2027
* Official URL: https://www.imperial.ac.uk/
### The University of Edinburgh Global Scholarships
* Amount: Up to £15,000
* Eligibility: Female international students, including refugees, pursuing a master's degree in a STEM field
* Deadline: April 2027
* Official URL: https://www.ed.ac.uk/

## Application Steps
1. Choose your desired scholarship and check the eligibility criteria.
2. Review the required documents and start gathering them.
3. Fill out the online application form and upload the required documents.
4. Submit your application before the deadline.
5. Wait for the selection committee to review your application.
6. Participate in an interview if required.
7. Receive the scholarship award letter and accept the offer.
8. Prepare to start your academic program in the UK.

## Documents Checklist
* Valid UK visa
* Passport
* Academic transcripts
* Language proficiency test results
* Personal statement
* Letters of recommendation
* Proof of financial need

## Frequently Asked Questions
1. Can I apply for a scholarship if I have a pending asylum application?
Yes, you can apply, but you must have a valid UK visa at the time of application.
2. How do I prove my refugee status?
You can provide a copy of your UK visa, asylum seeker's card, or a letter from the UK Home Office.
3. Can I apply for multiple scholarships at the same time?
Yes, you can apply for multiple scholarships, but you must meet the eligibility criteria for each one.
4. What is the language requirement for UK universities?
Most UK universities require IELTS or TOEFL language proficiency test results.
5. Can I bring my family with me to the UK if I receive a scholarship?
It depends on the scholarship and the UK visa requirements.
6. How long does it take to process a scholarship application?
It can take several weeks to several months to process a scholarship application.
7. Can I apply for a scholarship if I have a disability?
Yes, you can apply, and some scholarships are specifically designed for students with disabilities.
8. What are the living expenses in the UK for international students?
The living expenses vary depending on the location and lifestyle, but a scholarship can help cover these costs.
