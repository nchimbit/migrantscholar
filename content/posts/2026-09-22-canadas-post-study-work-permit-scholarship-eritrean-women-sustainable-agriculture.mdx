## Canada’s Post‑Study Work Permit Scholarship for Eritrean Women in Sustainable Agriculture

The Canadian International Graduate Scholarship Program (CIGS) offers **CAD 15,000 per year** to students from Eritrea, including women, pursuing sustainable agriculture degrees in Canadian universities.  

### Who Qualifies?

| Visa Category | Eligibility | Key Points |
|---------------|-------------|------------|
| **Post‑Graduation Work Permit (PGWP)** | Eritrean women who have earned a bachelor's, master's, or doctoral degree in sustainable agriculture or related fields from a designated Canadian university. | The PGWP allows up to three years of work in Canada; the scholarship funds cover living expenses during this period. |
| **Student Visa (Study Permit)** | Eritrean women currently studying sustainable agriculture at a Canadian post‑secondary institution. | Applicants can receive the scholarship before graduation to secure funding for their PGWP. |
| **Temporary Resident Visa (TRV)** | Eritrean women who have been accepted into a Canadian program and hold a valid TRV. | Must transition to a study permit before the scholarship can be awarded. |
