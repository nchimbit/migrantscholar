# STEM USA
The Paul & Daisy Soros Fellowships for New Americans scholarship exists and qualifies Turkish refugees in STEM fields with a funding amount of up to $90,000 for two years. This prestigious scholarship is one of the many fully funded master's degree programs available in the USA for Turkish refugees in STEM fields. For those interested in [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded) like the Paul & Daisy Soros Fellowships, there are numerous options to explore.

## Who Qualifies
Turkish refugees who hold a valid visa, such as an F-1 or J-1 visa, are eligible to apply for these scholarships. Additionally, refugees with asylum status or those who have been granted permanent residency in the USA may also be eligible. It's essential to check the specific eligibility criteria for each scholarship, as some may require a certain GPA, test scores, or work experience. ## Scholarships
### Paul & Daisy Soros Fellowships for New Americans
* Amount: up to $90,000 for two years
* Eligibility: Turkish refugees who are pursuing a graduate degree in the USA
* Deadline: November 1st
* Official URL: https://www.pdsoros.org/

### Fulbright Foreign Student Program
* Amount: full tuition, living stipend, and airfare
* Eligibility: Turkish refugees who have completed their undergraduate degree and are pursuing a master's degree in the USA
* Deadline: varies by country
* Official URL: https://foreign.fulbrightonline.org/

### AAUW International Fellowships
* Amount: $18,000 to $30,000
* Eligibility: Turkish refugee women who are pursuing a graduate degree in the USA
* Deadline: November 15th
* Official URL: https://www.aauw.org/

### P.E.O. International Peace Scholarship
* Amount: up to $10,000
* Eligibility: Turkish refugee women who are pursuing a graduate degree in the USA
* Deadline: December 15th
* Official URL: https://www.peointernational.org/

### Microsoft Scholarship Program
* Amount: full tuition and a stipend
* Eligibility: Turkish refugees who are pursuing a graduate degree in a STEM field in the USA
* Deadline: October 1st
* Official URL: https://www.microsoft.com/

## Application Steps
1. Research and select a scholarship that matches your eligibility criteria
2. Review the application requirements and deadlines
3. Prepare your application materials, including transcripts, test scores, and letters of recommendation
4. Submit your application through the official website or online portal
5. Wait for the application review process to be completed
