## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for a Tier 4 visa, students must meet certain requirements, including being accepted into a course at a UK university, having sufficient funds to support themselves, and meeting the English language proficiency requirements. The visa categories for Pakistani students include Tier 4 (General) student visa and Tier 4 (Child) student visa. ## Application Steps
1. Choose a course and university in the UK.
2. Check the eligibility criteria for the Tier 4 visa.
3. Apply for the course and receive a confirmation letter from the university.
4. Prepare the required documents, including a valid passport, birth certificate, and language proficiency test results.
5. Apply for the Tier 4 visa online and pay the application fee.
6. Attend a biometric appointment and provide fingerprints.
7. Wait for the visa decision and receive the visa approval letter.
8. Apply for [Canada scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/countries/Canada) or [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.com/by-eligibility/refugees) to explore other study options.

## Frequently Asked Questions
1. What is the processing time for the Tier 4 visa?
The processing time for the Tier 4 visa can take up to 3 weeks.
2. Can I work part-time while studying in the UK?
Yes, students with a Tier 4 visa can work part-time for up to 20 hours per week.
3. Do I need to take a language proficiency test?
Yes, Pakistani students need to take a language proficiency test, such as IELTS or TOEFL, to meet the UK visa requirements.
4. Can I bring my family with me to the UK?
Yes, students with a Tier 4 visa can bring their family members with them to the UK, but they need to meet certain requirements.
5. How much does the Tier 4 visa application cost?
The Tier 4 visa application fee is £348.
6. Can I apply for a [scholarship deadlines](https://migrantscholar.com/deadlines) extension?
No, the scholarship deadlines are fixed, and students need to apply before the deadline.
7. What is the difference between a Tier 4 (General) student visa and a Tier 4 (Child) student visa?
The Tier 4 (General) student visa is for students who are 16 or older, while the Tier 4 (Child) student visa is for students who are under 16.
8. Can I apply for a Tier 4 visa if I have a criminal record?
It depends on the nature of the crime and the sentence received. Students with a criminal record need to check the UK visa requirements before applying.
