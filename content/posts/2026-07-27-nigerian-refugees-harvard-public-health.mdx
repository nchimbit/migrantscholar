* Nigerian refugees with a valid refugee status

The Fully Funded Master's Programs in Public Health at Harvard University covers:
* Full tuition fees for the Master's program
* Living expenses, including accommodation and meals
* Health insurance for the duration of the program
* Travel costs to and from the USA
* A stipend for books and other educational materials
