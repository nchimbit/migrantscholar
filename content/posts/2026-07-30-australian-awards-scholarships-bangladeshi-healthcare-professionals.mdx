# Aust Awards
The Australian Awards Scholarships are available to Bangladeshi healthcare professionals, with a funding amount of up to AU$30,000 per year, to pursue a master's degree in healthcare fields in Australia.

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for the Australian Awards Scholarships, Bangladeshi healthcare professionals must meet the following visa categories: Subclass 500, Subclass 570, Subclass 571, Subclass 572, and Subclass 573. They must also have a minimum of two years of work experience in the healthcare sector and meet the English language requirements.

* Amount: up to AU$30,000 per year
