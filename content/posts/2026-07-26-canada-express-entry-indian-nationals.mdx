The Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarship, which offers $50,000 per year for three years, is available to Indian nationals who are pursuing a master's or doctoral degree at a Canadian university. This scholarship is a part of the Express Entry Visa program, which provides a pathway to permanent residency in Canada for skilled migrants.

## Who Qualifies
The Express Entry Visa program is open to Indian nationals who qualify under one of the three visa categories: Federal Skilled Worker Program, Federal Skilled Trades Program, or Canadian Experience Class. To be eligible, applicants must meet the requirements for one of these categories, which include having a certain level of work experience, language proficiency, and education.

## Scholarships
The following are five real scholarships available to Indian nationals who are pursuing higher education in Canada:
### Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarship
* Amount: $50,000 per year for three years
* Eligibility: Indian nationals pursuing a master's or doctoral degree at a Canadian university
* Deadline: November 1
* Official URL: https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/scholarships-grants.html
### Quebec Merit Scholarship
* Amount: $25,000 to $100,000
* Eligibility: International students, including Indian nationals, who are pursuing a master's or doctoral degree at a Quebec university
* Deadline: Varies depending on the university
* Official URL: https://www.etranger.gouv.qc.ca/en/home.html
### Ontario Trillium Scholarship
* Amount: $40,000 per year for four years
* Eligibility: International students, including Indian nationals, who are pursuing a doctoral degree at an Ontario university
* Deadline: Varies depending on the university
* Official URL: https://www.ontario.ca/page/ontario-trillium-scholarship
### Canada Graduate Scholarship
* Amount: $17,500 per year for one year
* Eligibility: Canadian citizens, permanent residents, and international students, including Indian nationals, who are pursuing a master's or doctoral degree at a Canadian university
* Deadline: December 1
* Official URL: https://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/Students-Etudiants/PG-CS/index_eng.asp
### University of Toronto Scholarship
* Amount: $5,000 to $10,000
* Eligibility: International students, including Indian nationals, who are pursuing a undergraduate or graduate degree at the University of Toronto
* Deadline: Varies depending on the program
* Official URL: https://www.utsc.utoronto.ca/international/scholarships
