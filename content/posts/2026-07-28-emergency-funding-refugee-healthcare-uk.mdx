The British Red Cross Refugee Scholarship exists for refugee healthcare workers in the UK, providing up to £1,000 in funding. 
Refugee healthcare workers who are asylum seekers, have refugee status, or are under humanitarian protection in the UK are eligible to apply for emergency funding to support their education and career goals. 

## Who Qualifies
To qualify for emergency funding as a refugee healthcare worker in the UK, applicants typically need to hold a specific visa category, such as a refugee visa, asylum seeker visa, or a visa under the humanitarian protection category.
