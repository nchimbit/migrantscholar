## Who Qualifies
The following visa categories and eligibility criteria apply to Pakistani nationals:
* Work visa: Pakistani nationals who have been offered a job by a Turkish employer
* Freelance work visa: Pakistani nationals who wish to work as freelancers in Turkey
* Entrepreneur visa: Pakistani nationals who wish to start their own business in Turkey
* Other visa categories: Pakistani nationals who qualify for other visa categories, such as student or tourist visas

## What the Scholarship Covers
The Turkey work visa covers the right to work in Turkey for a specified period, typically up to one year. The visa does not cover funding for living expenses, accommodation, or other costs associated with living in Turkey. Please check the official Turkish government website for the latest information on visa fees and requirements.

## How to Apply
To apply for a Turkey work visa, Pakistani nationals must follow these steps:
1. Check the official Turkish government website for the latest information on visa requirements and application procedures.
2. Gather all required documents, including a valid passport, completed application form, and supporting documents such as a job offer letter or business plan.
3. Submit the application and supporting documents to the Turkish embassy or consulate in Pakistan.
4. Pay the required visa fee.
5. Wait for the application to be processed, which may take several weeks or months.
6. Attend an interview at the Turkish embassy or consulate, if required.
7. Receive the visa decision and collect the visa, if approved.
8. Register with the local authorities in Turkey within a specified period after arrival.

## Required Documents
The following documents are required to apply for a Turkey work visa:
* Valid passport with at least six months' validity
* Completed application form
* Job offer letter or contract from a Turkish employer
* Supporting documents, such as a degree certificate or diploma
* Proof of language proficiency, if required
* Proof of health insurance
* Police certificate, if required
* Other documents, as required by the Turkish embassy or consulate
