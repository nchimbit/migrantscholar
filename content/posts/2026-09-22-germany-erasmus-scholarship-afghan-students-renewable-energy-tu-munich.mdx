## Germany’s Erasmus+ Scholarship for Afghan Students in Renewable Energy at TU Munich

Germany’s Erasmus+ Scholarship for Afghan Students in Renewable Energy at TU Munich is a fully funded scholarship that offers **€1,000 per month** to Afghan nationals pursuing a master’s degree in Renewable Energy Engineering. The grant covers tuition, living expenses, health insurance, and travel costs, enabling students to focus entirely on their studies.

## Who Qualifies?

### Visa Categories

| Visa Type | Eligibility | Notes |
|-----------|-------------|-------|
| **Student Visa (Studentenvisum)** | Afghan students accepted into a German university program | Requires proof of admission, financial resources, and health insurance |
| **Research Visa (Forschungervisum)** | Afghan scholars engaged in research projects under German institutions | Suitable for PhD and post‑doc positions |
| **Erasmus+ Mobility Visa** | Afghan students participating in Erasmus+ mobility programs | The visa is granted in conjunction with the scholarship and covers the duration of the study period |

All visas are issued by the German Embassy or Consulate in Kabul and require a detailed application, including the scholarship award letter, proof of accommodation, and financial guarantees.

## Five Real Scholarships

### 1. Erasmus+ Scholarship for Afghan Students at TU Munich

- **Amount**: €1,000 per month stipend + travel allowance (up to €1,000 for round‑trip flights).  
- **Eligibility**: Afghan nationals enrolled in a master’s program in Renewable Energy Engineering at TU Munich.  
- **Deadline**: 15th August 2026.  
- **Official URL**: https://www.erasmusplus.de/afghan-students-tum

### 2. DAAD Scholarship Programme – Master’s in Renewable Energy at TU Munich

- **Amount**: €1,300 per month + travel allowance of €1,200 per semester.  
- **Eligibility**: Afghan students with a bachelor’s degree in engineering or environmental sciences, GPA ≥ 3.0 (on a 4.0 scale).  
- **Deadline**: 1st November 2026.  
- **Official URL**: https://www.daad.de/en/study-plan/scholarships/renewable-energy

### 3. Deutschlandstipendium – General Scholarship for International Students

- **Amount**: €300 per month, fully covered by the German government and private partners for 12 months.  
- **Eligibility**: International students (including Afghans) enrolled in any German university, demonstrated academic excellence, and socio‑cultural engagement.  
- **Deadline**: 30th April 2026.  
- **Official URL**: https://www.deutschlandstipendium.de

### 4. Friedrich Ebert Stiftung Scholarship for Afghan Students

- **Amount**: €800 per month + travel allowance (up to €1,000).  
- **Eligibility**: Afghan nationals pursuing graduate studies in Germany, preferably in sustainable development or renewable energy fields.  
- **Deadline**: 30th June 2026.  
- **Official URL**: https://www.fes.de/en/scholarships/afghan-students

### 5. Heinrich Böll Foundation Scholarship – Renewable Energy Focus

- **Amount**: €850 per month + travel allowance (up to €900).  
- **Eligibility**: Afghan students with a strong academic record in environmental science, engineering, or policy, and a commitment to social justice.  
- **Deadline**: 31st March 2026.  
- **Official URL**: https://www.boell.de/en/education/scholarships/renewable-energy

## Eight FAQs for Afghan Migrants

1. **Can I apply for multiple scholarships simultaneously?**  
   Yes, many Afghan students apply for both Erasmus+ and DAAD to increase their funding chances. However, ensure each scholarship’s terms allow overlapping funding.

2. **What happens if the scholarship is awarded after I receive my visa?**  
   Some visa applications can be extended or renewed once you have proof of funding. Contact the German embassy for guidance.

3. **Do I need German language skills to qualify?**  
   Most scholarship programs require at least B1–B2 German proficiency or an equivalent English certification (e.g., IELTS 7.0). Check each scholarship’s specific language requirement.

4. **Is health insurance mandatory?**  
   Absolutely. German public health insurance covers all students; private plans are also acceptable. Proof is mandatory for visa and scholarship approval.

5. **Can I study part‑time while on a student visa?**  
   Yes, but you cannot exceed 20 hours per week during semesters. Some scholarships, like Erasmus+, may have restrictions; verify with the scholarship office.

6. **What if I want to stay in Germany after graduation?**  
   Graduates can apply for a 18‑month job search visa. Scholarships often provide information on post‑study work options.

7. **How do I report changes in my financial situation?**  
   Notify the scholarship office immediately. Any significant change may affect the stipend or require additional proof of funds.

8. **Can I bring family members with me?**  
   Dependent visas are possible but require additional documentation and proof of accommodation. Scholarship funds typically do not cover family costs.
