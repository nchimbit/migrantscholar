# Post‑Study Work Visa Pathways for Syrian Refugees in Canada

The Trudeau Scholarship offers $30,000 CAD per year for two years to eligible Syrian refugee students pursuing a master’s degree in Canada.  

## Who Qualifies for These Pathways

| Visa Category | What It Enables | Who Can Apply |
|----------------|----------------|---------------|
| **Post‑Study Work Permit (PSWP)** | Allows graduates to work in Canada for up to 3 years (4 years if you earned a master’s). | Syrian refugees who complete a Canadian post‑graduate program that is at least 8 months long. |
| **Canada Experience Class (CEC)** | Fast‑track permanent residency for skilled workers with Canadian work experience. | Refugees with at least 12 months of full‑time work post‑graduation. |
| **Provincial Nominee Program (PNP)** | Enables nomination for permanent residency by a province. | Refugees who study in a province and secure a job offer in a skilled occupation. |
| **Global Talent Stream (GTS)** | 12‑month work permit for highly skilled foreign workers. | Refugees with advanced degrees and a job offer in a STEM field. |
| **Post‑Graduate Work Permit (PGWP) + Express Entry** | Combines work experience with points‑based immigration. | Syrian refugees who secure high‑skill employment post‑graduation. |

These pathways are interconnected: a scholarship‑funded master's or PhD program not only finances your education but also unlocks a post‑study work permit, which in turn can lead to permanent residency through CEC, PNP, or GTS.

## Five Real Scholarships for Syrian Refugees

### 1️⃣ Trudeau Scholarship
- **Amount:** $30,000 CAD per year for two years.  
- **Eligibility:** International students, including refugees, enrolled in a Canadian master’s program in any discipline.  
- **Deadline:** June 15 (2026).  
- **Official URL:** https://www.trudeau-scholarship.ca/  

### 2️⃣ Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarship
- **Amount:** $50,000 CAD per year for three years.  
- **Eligibility:** PhD candidates from around the world, including Syrian refugees, with exceptional research potential.  
- **Deadline:** December 15 (2026).  
- **Official URL:** https://vanier.gc.ca/en/home.html  

### 3️⃣ Canada Graduate Scholarship – Master’s (CGS‑M)
- **Amount:** $25,000 CAD per year for three years.  
- **Eligibility:** International students pursuing a master’s in Canada, including Syrian refugees, who demonstrate academic excellence.  
- **Deadline:** September 30 (2026).  
- **Official URL:** https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/scholarships.html
