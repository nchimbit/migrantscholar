The University of Toronto Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program exists and qualifies Somali refugee students in Canada for up to $80,000 in funding. 
The program aims to support [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.com/by-eligibility/refugees) pursuing [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-funding/fully-funded) in [Canada scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/countries/Canada). 
Refugee students can apply for various [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-level/phd) in STEM fields at the University of Toronto.

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for the University of Toronto Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program, applicants must be Somali refugee students with a valid Canadian visa, including visa categories such as Convention Refugees, Protected Persons, or Refugee Claimants. 
They must also demonstrate financial need and have a strong academic record in a STEM field.

## Available Scholarships
The following scholarships are available for Somali refugee students in Canada:
### University of Toronto Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program
* Amount: up to $80,000
* Eligibility: Somali refugee students in Canada with a valid visa
* Deadline: varies
* Official URL: https://www.utoronto.ca/mastercard-foundation-scholars-program
### Ontario Graduate Scholarship
* Amount: up to $15,000
* Eligibility: graduate students in Ontario, including refugee students
* Deadline: varies
* Official URL: https://www.ontario.ca/page/ontario-graduate-scholarship
### Canada Graduate Scholarships-Master's Program
* Amount: up to $17,500
* Eligibility: master's students in Canada, including refugee students
* Deadline: December 1
* Official URL: https://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/Students-Etudiants/CGSM-BESCM_eng.asp
### Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarships
* Amount: up to $50,000
* Eligibility: doctoral students in Canada, including refugee students
* Deadline: November 1
* Official URL: https://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/Students-Etudiants/VanierCGS-VanierBES/index_eng.asp
### Queen Elizabeth II Graduate Scholarships in Science and Technology
* Amount: up to $15,000
* Eligibility: graduate students in science and technology, including refugee students
* Deadline: varies
* Official URL: https://www.utoronto.ca/apply/scholarships/queen-elizabeth-ii-graduate-scholarships-science-and-technology
