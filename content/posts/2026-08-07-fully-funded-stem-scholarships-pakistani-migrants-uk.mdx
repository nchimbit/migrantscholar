The Commonwealth Scholarship and Fellowship Plan is available to Pakistani migrants, offering a funding amount of up to £1,347 per month for masters and PhD students. Pakistani migrants who are holders of a Tier 4 student visa, a family visa, or a work visa, and have been living in the UK for at least 3 years, may be eligible for fully funded STEM scholarships at UK universities. 

## Who Qualifies
To qualify for fully funded STEM scholarships at UK universities, Pakistani migrants must hold a valid visa, such as a Tier 2 general visa, a Tier 5 temporary worker visa, or a family visa. They must also meet the eligibility criteria set by the scholarship provider, which may include academic excellence, language proficiency, and a strong research proposal.

### Available Scholarships
The following fully funded STEM scholarships are available to Pakistani migrants at UK universities:
* ### Commonwealth Scholarship and Fellowship Plan
  + Amount: up to £1,347 per month
  + Eligibility: Pakistani migrants with a Tier 4 student visa
  + Deadline: varies
  + Official URL: https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/
* ### Chevening Scholarship
  + Amount: full tuition fees and a monthly stipend
  + Eligibility: Pakistani migrants with a bachelor's degree and at least 2 years of work experience
  + Deadline: November 2026
  + Official URL: https://www.chevening.org/
* ### Gates Cambridge Scholarship
  + Amount: full tuition fees and a monthly stipend
  + Eligibility: Pakistani migrants with a bachelor's degree and a strong research proposal
  + Deadline: December 2026
  + Official URL: https://www.gatescambridge.org/
* ### Clarendon Scholarship
  + Amount: full tuition fees and a monthly stipend
  + Eligibility: Pakistani migrants with a bachelor's degree and a strong research proposal
  + Deadline: January 2027
  + Official URL: https://www.ox.ac.uk/clarendon/

  + Amount: £5,000 to £10,000
  + Eligibility: Pakistani migrants with a bachelor's degree and a strong research proposal

## Application Steps
To apply for fully funded STEM scholarships at UK universities, Pakistani migrants must follow these steps:
1. Research the scholarship provider and the eligibility criteria
2. Check the official website for the application deadline
3. Prepare a strong research proposal and a personal statement

5. Submit the application form online
