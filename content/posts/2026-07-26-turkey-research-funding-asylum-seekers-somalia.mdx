The [Turkish Government Scholarship](https://www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr/) exists and qualifies Master's students from Somalia with a fully funded opportunity worth $750 per month.

## Who Qualifies
The Turkish Government Scholarship is open to asylum seekers from Somalia who hold a valid visa, including visa categories such as student visa, humanitarian visa, or refugee visa. To be eligible, applicants must meet the requirements set by the Turkish Government, including language proficiency in English or Turkish.

The following scholarships are available for asylum seekers from Somalia:
### Turkish Government Scholarship
* Amount: $750 per month
* Eligibility: Master's students from Somalia
* Deadline: February 20, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr/

* Amount: $1,000 per month

* Amount: $1,000 per month
* Eligibility: Master's students from Somalia

### Chevening Scholarship
