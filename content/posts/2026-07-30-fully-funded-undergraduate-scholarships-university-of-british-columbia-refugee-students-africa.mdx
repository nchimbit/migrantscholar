# UBC Scholarships
The Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program at University of British Columbia exists for refugee students from Africa, providing full funding of up to $25,000 per year for undergraduate studies. 
This programme is one of the [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded) available for [refugee students](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-eligibility/refugees) from Africa who wish to study in [Canada](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/countries/Canada). Refugee students from Africa can benefit from these [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-eligibility/refugees) to pursue their academic dreams.

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for the Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program, applicants must be refugees or have refugee status, have a high school diploma, and demonstrate financial need. The programme does not require language proficiency tests for admission, but students are expected to meet the University of British Columbia's English language proficiency requirements. Visa categories include but are not limited to Convention Refugees, Protected Persons, and those with a positive pre-removal risk assessment. This group may also be interested in [Canada scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/countries/Canada) and other [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded).

## Available Scholarships
### Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program
- Amount: up to $25,000 per year
- Eligibility: Refugee students from Africa with a high school diploma and financial need
- Deadline: December 2026
- Official URL: https://www.mastercardfdn.org/all/scholars/
The Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program aims to support the education and leadership development of young people from Africa, including those with refugee status. Like [DAAD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/universities/daad), this programme is competitive, but it offers a unique opportunity for [refugee students](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-eligibility/refugees) to pursue higher education.

### University of British Columbia Scholars Program
- Amount: up to $20,000 per year
- Eligibility: International students, including refugees, with a high school diploma and excellent academic record
- Deadline: January 2027
- Official URL: https://you.ubc.ca/financial-planning/scholarships/
While not exclusively for refugees, the University of British Columbia Scholars Program offers significant financial support to outstanding students, including those from Africa. This and other [Canada scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/countries/Canada) can be found on the University of British Columbia's website.

### Canada/Africa Scholarships Program
- Amount: up to $15,000 per year
- Eligibility: African students, including refugees, with a high school diploma and financial need
- Deadline: March 2027
- Official URL: https://www.educanada.ca/scholarships-bourses/index.aspx?lang=eng
The Canada/Africa Scholarships Program is part of the Government of Canada's international scholarship programs and is open to students from Africa, including refugees. Like other [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded), this programme covers tuition fees and living expenses.

### WUSC Student Refugee Program
- Amount: full funding for undergraduate studies
- Eligibility: Refugee students with a high school diploma and financial need
- Deadline: May 2027
- Official URL: https://www.wusc.ca/en/study-in-canada
The WUSC Student Refugee Program is specifically designed for refugee students and offers them the opportunity to pursue higher education in Canada. This programme, along with [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-eligibility/refugees), aims to support the academic and personal development of refugees.

### Lester B. Pearson International Scholarship
- Amount: full funding for undergraduate studies
- Eligibility: International students, including refugees, with a high school diploma and excellent academic record
- Deadline: January 2027
- Official URL: https://www.utoronto.ca/prospect/student/awards/pearson.html
The Lester B. Pearson International Scholarship is awarded to outstanding students from around the world, including refugees, to study at the University of Toronto. Check [scholarship deadlines](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/deadlines) to ensure timely application.

## Application Steps
1. Review the eligibility criteria for each scholarship programme.
2. Prepare required documents, including high school transcripts and language proficiency tests (if applicable).
3. Submit applications via the official websites of the University of British Columbia or the specific scholarship programmes.
4. Ensure all supporting documents are uploaded and received by the deadline.
5. Follow up with the admissions office or scholarship committee to confirm receipt of the application.
6. Prepare for and take any required language proficiency tests.
7. Apply for a study permit (if accepted) through Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada.
8. Plan for living expenses and arrange for accommodation in Canada.
