The University of Toronto offers fully funded scholarships for Syrian women in STEM fields, with a funding amount of up to $50,000 per year. These scholarships are available to Syrian refugee women who are pursuing undergraduate or graduate degrees in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics. Please check the official scholarship website for the latest information.

## Who Qualifies
* Syrian refugee women who are pursuing undergraduate or graduate degrees in STEM fields
* Applicants must have a valid refugee claim or be in the process of claiming refugee status in Canada
* Applicants must meet the admission requirements for the University of Toronto
* Applicants must demonstrate financial need and a strong academic record

## What the Scholarship Covers
The University of Toronto scholarships for Syrian women in STEM fields cover the following expenses:
* Tuition fees: up to $50,000 per year
* Living expenses: up to $10,000 per year
* Health insurance: up to $1,000 per year
* Other expenses: up to $2,000 per year
Please note that the exact funding breakdown may vary depending on the applicant's individual circumstances.

## How to Apply
1. Review the eligibility criteria and ensure you meet the requirements
2. Submit an application for admission to the University of Toronto
3. Complete a separate application for the scholarship
4. Upload all required documents, including transcripts, language proficiency test results, and proof of refugee status
5. Submit a personal statement outlining your academic and career goals
6. Provide two academic references
7. Submit a detailed budget outlining your living expenses and other financial needs
8. Submit your application by the deadline, as listed on the official scholarship website

## Required Documents
* Transcripts from previous academic institutions
* Language proficiency test results (IELTS or equivalent)
* Proof of refugee status or refugee claim
* Personal statement outlining academic and career goals
* Two academic references
* Detailed budget outlining living expenses and other financial needs
* Proof of admission to the University of Toronto
