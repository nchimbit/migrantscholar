The Friedrich Ebert Foundation Scholarship exists for Iranian refugees in Germany, providing up to €750 per month in emergency funding. Iranian refugees in Germany who hold a residence permit or a visa in categories such as asylum, refugee, or subsidiary protection may qualify for various emergency funding opportunities. 

## Who Qualifies
Iranian refugees in Germany who hold a residence permit or a visa in categories such as:
- Asylum seeker (§ 18a Abs. 1 AsylG)
- Refugee (§ 3 Abs. 1 AsylG)
- Subsidiary protection (§ 4 Abs. 1 AsylG)
- Temporary protection (§ 24 Abs. 1 AufenthG)
may be eligible for emergency funding. For more information on visa categories, visit the [official government website](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/for/visa).

## Scholarships
### Friedrich Ebert Foundation Scholarship
- Amount: up to €750 per month
- Eligibility: Iranian refugees in Germany with a residence permit or a visa in categories such as asylum, refugee, or subsidiary protection
- Deadline: varies
- Official URL: https://www.fes.de/en/scholarship
### DAAD Refugee Scholarship
- Amount: up to €750 per month
- Eligibility: Iranian refugees in Germany with a university degree and a residence permit or a visa in categories such as asylum, refugee, or subsidiary protection
- Deadline: varies
- Official URL: https://www.daad.de/en/scholarship/further-information/
### Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung Scholarship
- Amount: up to €1,200 per month
- Eligibility: Iranian refugees in Germany with a university degree and a residence permit or a visa in categories such as asylum, refugee, or subsidiary protection
- Deadline: varies
- Official URL: https://www.kas.de/en/scholarship
### Hesse Foundation Scholarship
- Amount: up to €1,000 per month
- Eligibility: Iranian refugees in Germany with a university degree and a residence permit or a visa in categories such as asylum, refugee, or subsidiary protection
- Deadline: varies
- Official URL: https://www.hessische-stiftung.eu/en/scholarship
### Heinrich Böll Foundation Scholarship
- Amount: up to €1,200 per month
- Eligibility: Iranian refugees in Germany with a university degree and a residence permit or a visa in categories such as asylum, refugee, or subsidiary protection
- Deadline: varies
- Official URL: https://www.boell.de/en/scholarship

## Application Steps
1. Check your eligibility for the scholarship.
2. Gather all required documents, including your residence permit or visa.
3. Fill out the application form on the official website of the scholarship provider.
4. Submit your application before the deadline.
5. Wait for the scholarship provider to review your application.
6. If selected, receive your emergency funding.
7. Use the funding to cover your living expenses.
8. Report back to the scholarship provider on how you used the funding.
