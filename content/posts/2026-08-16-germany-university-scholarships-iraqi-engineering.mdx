# Germany Scholarships
The DAAD Scholarship, which qualifies Iraqi nationals pursuing degrees in engineering, offers a monthly funding amount of 750 euros for master's students. 
Iraqi nationals with a strong interest in engineering can benefit from various German university scholarships to pursue their academic goals in Germany. 
To be eligible for these scholarships, Iraqi nationals typically need to hold a valid visa, such as a student visa or a job seeker visa, to enter and stay in Germany for the duration of their studies. 
WHO qualifies for these scholarships includes Iraqi students with a bachelor's degree in a relevant field, proficiency in English or German language, and a strong academic record. 
Visa categories for scholarship applicants include student visa, job seeker visa, and language course visa.

### DAAD Scholarship
* Amount: 750 euros per month
* Eligibility: Iraqi nationals with a bachelor's degree in a relevant field, proficiency in English or German language, and a strong academic record
* Deadline: varies depending on the program
* Official URL: https://www.daad.de/

### DeutschlandStipendium
* Amount: 300 euros per month
* Eligibility: Iraqi nationals with a strong academic record, proficiency in English or German language, and a clear motivation for studying in Germany
* Deadline: varies depending on the university
* Official URL: https://www.deutschland-stipendium.de/

### Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung Scholarship
* Amount: 750 euros per month
* Eligibility: Iraqi nationals with a bachelor's degree in a relevant field, proficiency in English or German language, and a strong academic record
* Deadline: July 15th
* Official URL: https://www.kas.de/

### Heinrich Böll Foundation Scholarship
* Amount: 750 euros per month
* Eligibility: Iraqi nationals with a bachelor's degree in a relevant field, proficiency in English or German language, and a strong academic record
* Deadline: March 1st
* Official URL: https://www.boell.de/

### Friedrich Ebert Foundation Scholarship
* Amount: 750 euros per month

## Application Steps
1. Check the official website of the scholarship provider for eligibility criteria and application requirements.
2. Prepare all required documents, including academic transcripts, language proficiency certificates, and a motivation letter.
3. Fill out the online application form and submit it before the deadline.
4. Pay the application fee, if required.
5. Wait for the scholarship committee to review your application and contact you for an interview, if necessary.
6. Receive the scholarship award letter and confirm your acceptance.
7. Apply for a student visa, if required.
8. Register at the German university and start your studies.
