The H1B Visa is a type of non-immigrant visa that allows skilled workers to work in the USA for a specific period. Skilled migrants who wish to work in the USA can apply for the H1B Visa, which is a popular visa category. The USA offers various job opportunities and visa sponsorship programs for skilled migrants, including the H1B Visa. 

## Who Qualifies
Skilled migrants who qualify for the H1B Visa include those with a bachelor's degree or higher in a specific field, such as engineering, computer science, or medicine. The H1B Visa is categorized into several types, including H1B, H1B1, and H4. The H1B1 visa is for citizens of Chile and Singapore, while the H4 visa is for spouses and dependents of H1B visa holders. ## Application Steps
To apply for the H1B Visa, skilled migrants can follow these steps:
1. Check the eligibility criteria for the H1B Visa
2. Choose a sponsor employer in the USA
3. File a Labor Condition Application (LCA) with the U.S. Department of Labor
4. Prepare and submit the H1B Visa application
5. Pay the required fees
6. Attend an interview at a U.S. embassy or consulate
7. Receive the H1B Visa approval and enter the USA
