# STEM Aid
The Fully Funded STEM Scholarships for Afghan Refugees in the USA provide up to $50,000 in funding per year to Afghan refugees who hold a valid asylum or refugee visa. This scholarship is designed to support Afghan refugees in pursuing higher education in STEM fields at universities in the USA. Please check the official scholarship website for the latest information on available funding and eligibility criteria.

## Who Qualifies
* Afghan refugees who hold a valid asylum or refugee visa (category AS-1 or AS-6)
* Applicants who have been admitted to or are currently enrolled in a STEM program at a university in the USA
* Students who demonstrate financial need and academic merit
* Applicants who are under the age of 30 (for undergraduate and masters programs) or 35 (for PhD programs)

## What the Scholarship Covers
The Fully Funded STEM Scholarships for Afghan Refugees cover the following expenses:
* Tuition fees up to $20,000 per year
* Living allowance of $15,000 per year
* Health insurance of $2,000 per year
* Travel allowance of $1,000 per year
* Research and equipment allowance of $2,000 per year (for PhD students)

## How to Apply
1. Check the official scholarship website for the latest information on eligibility criteria and application deadlines.
2. Review the list of participating universities and programs to ensure that your chosen program is eligible for the scholarship.
3. Prepare all required documents, including your asylum or refugee visa, academic transcripts, and English language proficiency test results.
4. Submit your application through the online portal, including all required documents and a personal statement.
5. Await the initial review and shortlisting process, which may take several weeks.
6. If shortlisted, prepare for an interview with the scholarship committee, which may be conducted in person or via video conference.
7. Receive notification of the scholarship decision, which will include information on the funding amount and duration of the award.
8. Accept the scholarship offer and complete any additional requirements, such as registering for classes and obtaining health insurance.

## Required Documents
* Asylum or refugee visa (category AS-1 or AS-6)
* Academic transcripts and diplomas
* English language proficiency test results (if required by the university)
* Personal statement (1-2 pages)
* Letters of recommendation (2-3)
* Proof of financial need (if required)
