The Türkiye Scholarship exists and qualifies international students, migrants, and refugees, with funding amounts ranging from 700 to 1,100 Turkish Lira per month. Migrant students in Turkey, especially those pursuing healthcare-related fields, can benefit from these scholarships. The Turkish Government Scholarships aim to provide financial support to international students, including migrants, to study in Turkey. 

## Who Qualifies
Migrant students with visa categories such as student visa, humanitarian visa, or family reunion visa may be eligible for Turkish Government Scholarships. The scholarship eligibility criteria include being a citizen of a country other than Turkey, meeting the academic requirements of the chosen program, and not being a registered student at any Turkish university. 

## Available Scholarships
### Türkiye Scholarship
 Türkiye Scholarship provides 1,100 Turkish Lira per month for undergraduate students, 1,400 Turkish Lira per month for master's students, and 1,800 Turkish Lira per month for PhD students, with eligibility for international students, deadline varying by country, and official URL [www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr](http://www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr).
### İbrahim Bodur Scholarship
 İbrahim Bodur Scholarship offers 750 Turkish Lira per month for undergraduate students, 1,050 Turkish Lira per month for master's students, with eligibility for Syrian students, deadline in June, and official URL [www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr](http://www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr).
### Turkey Graduate Scholarships
 Turkey Graduate Scholarships provide 2,000 Turkish Lira per month for master's students and 2,500 Turkish Lira per month for PhD students, with eligibility for international students, deadline in February, and official URL [www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr](http://www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr).
### Research Fellowship Program
 Research Fellowship Program offers 2,500 Turkish Lira per month for postdoctoral researchers, with eligibility for international researchers, deadline in September, and official URL [www.tubitak.gov.tr](http://www.tubitak.gov.tr).
### TÜBİTAK Scholarship
 TÜBİTAK Scholarship provides 2,000 Turkish Lira per month for master's students and 2,500 Turkish Lira per month for PhD students, with eligibility for international students, deadline in October, and official URL [www.tubitak.gov.tr](http://www.tubitak.gov.tr).
