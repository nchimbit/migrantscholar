## Fully Funded MPH for Nigerian Refugees at ANU

The **ANU International Scholarship** offers a fully funded Master of Public Health to Nigerian refugees, covering tuition, a living stipend, and health insurance.  

---

## Who Qualifies?

| Visa Category | Eligibility Criteria |
|---------------|----------------------|
| **Refugee or Protected Person Visa** (subclass 200, 201, 102, 103) | Must hold a valid Australian visa that recognises refugee status. |
| **Humanitarian Program Visas** (subclass 200, 201, 103) | Must be a refugee or person granted humanitarian protection in Australia. |
| **Asylum Seeker Visa** (subclass 010, 011, 012, 013, 014) | Must be recognised as an asylum seeker in the Australian immigration system. |
| **Refugee Resettlement** | Must be resettled through a reputable organisation (e.g., UNHCR). |

## 5 Real Scholarships for a Fully Funded Master’s in Public Health

### 1. ANU International Scholarship
- **Amount**: Full tuition, $2,500 living stipend per semester, and Commonwealth Medical Insurance Scheme (CMIS) coverage.  
- **Eligibility**: International students, including refugees, with a strong academic record in a relevant undergraduate field.  
- **Deadline**: 30 May 2026.  
- **Official URL**: <https://www.anu.edu.au/study/fees-and-financial-aid/scholarships/international-scholarships>

### 2. ANU Refugee Scholarship
- **Amount**: Full tuition, $3,000 living stipend per semester, CMIS, and travel allowance for international students.  
- **Eligibility**: Refugees and protected persons holding an Australian visa, with an undergraduate degree in health sciences or related discipline.  
- **Deadline**: 15 June 2026.  
- **Official URL**: <https://www.anu.edu.au/study/fees-and-financial-aid/scholarships/refugee-scholarship>

### 3. Australia Awards Scholarship
- **Amount**: Full tuition, $3,500 living stipend, travel allowance, and CMIS.  
- **Eligibility**: Students from low‑ and middle‑income countries, including Nigeria, pursuing a Master of Public Health or equivalent.  
- **Deadline**: 30 August 2026.  
- **Official URL**: <https://www.dfat.gov.au/people-issues/aid/australia-awards-schemes/pages/australia-awards-scholarships>

### 4. Global Health Scholarship (Australia)
- **Amount**: Full tuition, $3,000 living stipend, travel allowance, and CMIS.  
- **Eligibility**: Citizens of low‑ and middle‑income countries, including Nigeria, with a background in health or science and a commitment to global health.  
- **Deadline**: 31 July 2026.  
- **Official URL**: <https://www.dfat.gov.au/people-issues/health/global-health-scholars/pages/global-health-scholars>

### 5. Australian International Health Scholarship (AIMS)
- **Amount**: Full tuition, $2,800 living stipend, travel allowance, and CMIS.  
- **Eligibility**: International students with refugee status who have completed a Bachelor’s in health‑related fields and intend to pursue MPH.  
- **Deadline**: 1 September 2026.  
- **Official URL**: <https://www.dfat.gov.au/people-issues/health/health-scholarships/pages/aims-scholarship>

## 8 Step-by-Step Application Process

1. **Research and Confirm Eligibility**  
   Review each scholarship’s requirements and ensure you meet the visa and academic criteria.  
   > *Tip: Use the [scholarships for refugees](/by-eligibility/refugees) guide to filter options.*

2. **Secure Your Visa Status**  
   Validate your Australian visa and obtain a copy of the visa grant letter.  
   > *Note: If you’re in the process of resettlement, liaise with your resettlement agency to confirm your status.*

3. **Gather Academic Transcripts**  
   Obtain certified copies of all undergraduate coursework and the official grade report.  

4. **Obtain a Strong Reference Letter**  
   Request a reference from an academic supervisor or a health professional who can attest to your academic prowess and public health interests.  

5. **Prepare a Statement of Intent**  
   Write a 1–2 page statement outlining your motivation for an MPH, your career goals, and how the scholarship will help you serve refugee communities.  

6. **Complete the Online Application**  
   Use the scholarship portal (ANU’s or DFAT’s) to submit your application, uploading all required documents.  

7. **Submit Additional Materials (if required)**  
   Some scholarships may ask for a research proposal or English language proof.  

8. **Track Your Application**  
   Monitor the status via the scholarship portal and be prepared to attend an interview or provide supplementary information.

## 8 FAQs for Nigerian Refugee Students

1. **Can I apply for multiple scholarships at once?**  
   Yes, but ensure you comply with each scholarship’s unique application deadline and avoid conflicting requirements.

2. **What if I don’t have a high GPA?**  
   The ANU Refugee Scholarship values potential and community impact; provide a strong statement of intent to compensate.

3. **How do I prove my refugee status?**  
   Provide your UNHCR registration letter, Australian refugee status documentation, or a letter from your resettlement agency.

4. **Is the living stipend enough to cover living costs?**  
   Stipends are designed to cover basic living expenses; however, budgeting and part‑time work are recommended.

5. **Will I need to pay tuition if I get a scholarship?**  
   No, a fully funded scholarship covers all tuition fees.

6. **Can I work part‑time while studying?**  
   Yes, as a refugee visa holder you can work up to 40 hours per fortnight during semesters.

7. **What support does ANU provide for refugee students?**  
   ANU offers academic support, counseling, and community networking events tailored to refugee learners.

8. **Do I need to be fluent in English before applying?**  
   Most scholarships require proof of English proficiency (IELTS ≥ 6.5 or equivalent). Check individual scholarship requirements.
