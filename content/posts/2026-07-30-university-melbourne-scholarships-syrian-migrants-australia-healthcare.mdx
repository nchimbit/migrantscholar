## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for the University of Melbourne scholarships, Syrian migrants must hold a valid Australian visa, such as the Temporary Protection Visa (subclass 785), the Safe Haven Enterprise Visa (subclass 790), or the Permanent Protection Visa (subclass 866). Additionally, applicants must meet the English language proficiency requirements and have a strong academic record.
