The Canadian Women's Foundation offers emergency funding to women asylum seekers in Canada, providing up to $1,000 in funding to support basic needs. Eligible applicants include women who have applied for asylum in Canada and are experiencing financial hardship. Please check the official scholarship website for the latest information on eligibility and application deadlines.

## Who Qualifies
* Women who have applied for asylum in Canada under the following visa categories: 
  * Claimant under the Immigration and Refugee Protection Act
  * Refugee Claimant
  * Protected Person
* Women who are experiencing financial hardship and are in need of emergency funding
* Women who are not receiving sufficient support from other sources, such as government assistance programs

## What the Scholarship Covers
The Canadian Women's Foundation Emergency Funding provides up to $1,000 in funding to support basic needs, including:
* Food and groceries
* Rent and utilities
* Medical expenses
* Transportation costs
* Other essential expenses

## How to Apply
1. Review the eligibility criteria and ensure you meet the requirements
2. Gather all required documents, including proof of asylum claim and identification
3. Fill out the application form, which can be found on the Canadian Women's Foundation website
4. Submit the application form and supporting documents via email or mail
5. Wait for the application to be reviewed and processed
6. Receive notification of the application decision
7. If approved, receive the emergency funding via bank transfer or cheque
8. Follow up with the Canadian Women's Foundation to confirm receipt of funding and provide any additional information if required

## Required Documents
* Proof of asylum claim, such as a copy of the asylum application or a letter from the Immigration and Refugee Board
* Identification, such as a passport or driver's license
* Proof of financial hardship, such as a bank statement or letter from a social worker
* Other supporting documents, such as medical records or proof of dependents
