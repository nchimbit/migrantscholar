Rohingya asylum seekers in Australia can access higher education through emergency funding provided by various organizations. The Australian Awards Scholarship, for instance, offers full funding to eligible asylum seekers, including Rohingya individuals, with up to AUD 30,000 per year for undergraduate and postgraduate studies. However, specific scholarships targeting Rohingya asylum seekers are limited, and applicants are encouraged to explore various options.

## Who Qualifies
* Asylum seekers with a pending protection visa application, including Rohingya individuals
* Holders of a Temporary Protection Visa (TPV) or a Safe Haven Enterprise Visa (SHEV)
* Applicants with a Bridging Visa E (BVE) who have applied for a protection visa
* Meet the eligibility criteria for the specific scholarship, including academic requirements and English language proficiency

## What the Scholarship Covers
The emergency funding for Rohingya asylum seekers in Australia may cover various expenses related to higher education, including:
* Tuition fees for undergraduate or postgraduate studies
* Living expenses, such as accommodation and food
* Health insurance and medical expenses
* Academic materials and equipment
* Travel costs for research or academic purposes
Please note that the specific funding breakdown may vary depending on the scholarship and the applicant's circumstances.
