UK Visa Options
Indian migrants pursuing healthcare careers in the UK can apply for various visa types, including the Skilled Worker visa and the Health and Care Worker visa, with funding amounts varying depending on the employer and the specific job offer. Please check the official UK government website for the latest information on visa categories and eligibility criteria. The UK's National Health Service (NHS) also offers various career opportunities for international healthcare professionals.

## Who Qualifies
* Skilled Worker visa: Indian migrants with a job offer in a skilled occupation, including healthcare, with a salary of at least £25,600 per year
* Health and Care Worker visa: Indian migrants with a job offer in a healthcare occupation, including doctors, nurses, and other healthcare professionals, with a salary of at least £25,600 per year
* Tier 2 visa: Indian migrants with a job offer in a skilled occupation, including healthcare, with a salary of at least £30,000 per year
* Tier 5 visa: Indian migrants with a job offer in a temporary or seasonal occupation, including healthcare, with a salary of at least £15,800 per year

## What the Scholarship Covers
The funding amount for Indian migrants pursuing healthcare careers in the UK varies depending on the employer and the specific job offer. The UK's National Health Service (NHS) offers a salary range of £25,600 to over £100,000 per year, depending on the job title and level of experience. Additionally, some employers may offer other benefits, such as accommodation, meals, and transportation.

## How to Apply
1. Check the official UK government website for the latest information on visa categories and eligibility criteria.
2. Find a job offer in a healthcare occupation that meets the eligibility criteria for the Skilled Worker visa or the Health and Care Worker visa.
3. Apply for the visa through the UK Visa and Immigration website.
4. Pay the visa application fee, which varies depending on the type of visa and the speed of processing.
5. Provide all required documents, including a valid passport, a job offer letter, and proof of language proficiency.
6. Attend an interview at a UK visa application centre, if required.
7. Wait for the visa application to be processed, which can take several weeks or months.
8. Once the visa is approved, apply for a biometric residence permit (BRP) and register with the UK's National Health Service (NHS), if required.
