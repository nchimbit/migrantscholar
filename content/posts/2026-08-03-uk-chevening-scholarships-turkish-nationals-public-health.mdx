# UK Cheven
The Chevening Scholarship exists for Turkish nationals, offering fully funded opportunities to pursue a master's degree in the field of public health with a funding amount of up to £18,000. 
Turkish nationals who are interested in pursuing a career in public health can apply for this prestigious award. 
To be eligible, applicants must have a bachelor's degree, at least two years of work experience, and meet the Chevening English language requirement.

## Who Qualifies
The Chevening Scholarship is open to Turkish nationals who hold a valid passport, have a bachelor's degree, and are eligible for a UK visa under the categories of Tier 4 (General) student visa or Tier 5 (Temporary Worker) visa. 
Applicants must also have at least two years of work experience in the field of public health and meet the English language requirement.
