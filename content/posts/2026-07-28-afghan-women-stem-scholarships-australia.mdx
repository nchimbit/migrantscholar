## Scholarships
### Australia Awards Scholarships
The Australia Awards Scholarships provide up to AUD 30,000 per year for tuition fees and living expenses to Afghan women pursuing higher education in STEM fields. Eligible applicants must hold a valid Australian visa and meet the required academic and language proficiency standards. The deadline for applications is April 30, 2027. For more information, visit [https://www.australiaawards.gov.au/](https://www.australiaawards.gov.au/).

### International Student Scholarship
The International Student Scholarship offered by the University of Melbourne provides up to AUD 25,000 per year for tuition fees to Afghan women pursuing undergraduate or graduate studies in STEM fields. Eligible applicants must meet the required academic and language proficiency standards. The deadline for applications is December 31, 2026. For more information, visit [https://www.unimelb.edu.au/scholarships](https://www.unimelb.edu.au/scholarships).

### STEM Scholarship for Women
The STEM Scholarship for Women offered by the Australian National University provides up to AUD 20,000 per year for tuition fees and living expenses to Afghan women pursuing higher education in STEM fields. Eligible applicants must meet the required academic and language proficiency standards. The deadline for applications is March 31, 2027. For more information, visit [https://www.anu.edu.au/study/scholarships](https://www.anu.edu.au/study/scholarships).

### Women in STEM Scholarship
The Women in STEM Scholarship offered by the University of Sydney provides up to AUD 15,000 per year for tuition fees and living expenses to Afghan women pursuing undergraduate or graduate studies in STEM fields. Eligible applicants must meet the required academic and language proficiency standards. The deadline for applications is January 15, 2027. For more information, visit [https://www.sydney.edu.au/scholarships](https://www.sydney.edu.au/scholarships).

### Afghan Women's Scholarship
The Afghan Women's Scholarship offered by the [DAAD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/universities/daad) program provides up to AUD 10,000 per year for tuition fees and living expenses to Afghan women pursuing higher education in STEM fields. Eligible applicants must meet the required academic and language proficiency standards. The deadline for applications is February 28, 2027. For more information, visit [https://www.daad.de/scholarships](https://www.daad.de/scholarships).
