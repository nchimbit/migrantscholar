# York Univ. STEM Scholarships for Syrian Refugees

The **York University International Student Scholarship** offers full tuition, books, and living expenses for Syrian refugee undergraduate STEM students, with a total award of up to **$20,000 per year**.  

## Who Qualifies?  
To be eligible for the scholarships highlighted below, you must meet one of the following visa categories:

1. **Refugee Status** – Individuals who have been granted refugee protection in Canada (under the Government of Canada’s *Refugee Protection* program).  
2. **Protected Persons** – Those who have applied for refugee status and are waiting for a decision.  
3. **Asylum Seekers** – Individuals who have applied for asylum in Canada and are in the process of their claim.  
4. **Humanitarian and Compassionate (H&C) Applicants** – Applicants whose status is pending under the *Humanitarian and Compassionate* provisions.  

All applicants must also be accepted as **undergraduate students** at York University in a STEM discipline (e.g., Computer Science, Engineering, Biology, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Environmental Science, etc.).
