# STEM Scholarships
The [Society of Women Engineers Scholarship](https://swe.org/scholarships/) exists for Indian women pursuing undergraduate or graduate degrees in STEM fields in the USA, with funding amounts ranging from $1,000 to $10,000.

## Who Qualifies
Indian women who qualify for this scholarship must be enrolled in an accredited STEM program in the USA, and hold a valid F-1 visa or other eligible visa categories such as H1B, L1, or O1. They must also demonstrate financial need and academic excellence.

## Available Scholarships
### American Association of University Women (AAUW) Scholarship
* Amount: $5,000 to $18,000
* Eligibility: Indian women pursuing graduate degrees in STEM fields in the USA
* Deadline: November 15
* Official URL: [www.aauw.org](https://www.aauw.org)

### Google Anita Borg Memorial Scholarship
* Amount: $10,000
* Eligibility: Indian women pursuing undergraduate or graduate degrees in computer science or related fields in the USA
* Deadline: February 2
* Official URL: [www.google.com/anitaborg](https://www.google.com/anitaborg)

### Microsoft Research PhD Fellowship
* Amount: $175,000 over two years
* Eligibility: Indian women pursuing PhD degrees in computer science or related fields in the USA
* Deadline: October 11
* Official URL: [www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/academic-program/phd-fellowship](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/academic-program/phd-fellowship)

### National Science Foundation (NSF) Graduate Research Fellowship Program (GRFP)
* Amount: $34,000 per year for three years
* Eligibility: Indian women pursuing graduate degrees in STEM fields in the USA
* Deadline: October 20
* Official URL: [www.nsfgrfp.org](https://www.nsfgrfp.org)

### Women in Technology International (WITI) Scholarship
* Amount: $2,500 to $5,000
* Eligibility: Indian women pursuing undergraduate or graduate degrees in STEM fields in the USA
* Deadline: May 31
* Official URL: [www.witi.com](https://www.witi.com)

## Application Steps
1. Research and choose a scholarship that matches your eligibility criteria and interests.
2. Review the official website and application guidelines for the chosen scholarship.
3. Prepare a strong resume and personal statement highlighting your academic achievements and career goals.
4. Secure letters of recommendation from academic or professional mentors.
5. Complete and submit the online application form before the deadline.
6. Upload required documents, such as transcripts and test scores.
7. Pay the application fee, if applicable.
8. Track the status of your application and follow up with the scholarship committee, if necessary.

## Documents Checklist
