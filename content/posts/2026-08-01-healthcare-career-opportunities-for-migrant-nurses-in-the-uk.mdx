## Scholarships
### Florence Nightingale Foundation Scholarship
The Florence Nightingale Foundation Scholarship provides £10,000 funding for postgraduate studies to registered nurses, midwives, and health visitors with a minimum of 2 years of experience. The deadline is usually in January, and eligible candidates can apply through the [Florence Nightingale Foundation website](https://www.florence-nightingale-foundation.org.uk/).
### RCN Foundation Scholarship
The RCN Foundation Scholarship offers £5,000 funding for undergraduate and postgraduate studies to registered nurses and nursing students. The deadline is usually in April, and eligible candidates can apply through the [RCN Foundation website](https://www.rcnfoundation.org.uk/).
### Burdett Trust for Nursing Scholarship
The Burdett Trust for Nursing Scholarship provides up to £10,000 funding for research and education projects to registered nurses and midwives. The deadline is usually in July, and eligible candidates can apply through the [Burdett Trust for Nursing website](https://www.burdetttrust.org/).
### Health Education England Scholarship
The Health Education England Scholarship offers funding for postgraduate studies to healthcare professionals, including nurses, in England. The deadline is usually in March, and eligible candidates can apply through the [Health Education England website](https://www.hee.nhs.uk/).
### NHS Scholarship
The NHS Scholarship provides funding for undergraduate and postgraduate studies to NHS employees, including nurses. The deadline is usually in October, and eligible candidates can apply through the [NHS website](https://www.nhs.uk/).

## Frequently Asked Questions
1. What is the Tier 2 visa, and how does it apply to migrant nurses?
The Tier 2 visa is a points-based system for skilled workers, including nurses, who have a job offer in the UK.
2. Can I apply for a scholarship and visa if I am already in the UK on a different visa category?
Yes, but you may need to meet specific requirements and apply for a visa extension or switch.
3. How long does it take to process a Tier 2 visa application?
The processing time for a Tier 2 visa application can vary, but it usually takes around 3-8 weeks.
4. Can I bring my family with me to the UK on a Tier 2 visa?
Yes, you may be able to bring your family members with you to the UK, but they will need to meet specific requirements and apply for a dependent visa.
5. What is the difference between a Tier 2 General and Tier 2 Intra-Company Transfer visa?
The main difference is that a Tier 2 General visa is for skilled workers with a job offer in the UK, while a Tier 2 Intra-Company Transfer visa is for employees who are being transferred to a UK branch of their company.
6. How do I register with the Nursing and Midwifery Council to practice as a nurse in the UK?
You can register with the NMC by meeting the required standards and qualifications, and applying through their website.
7. Can I apply for a scholarship if I am a refugee or asylum seeker?
Yes, some scholarships, such as [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-eligibility/refugees), are specifically designed for refugees and asylum seekers.
8. What is the language proficiency requirement for migrant nurses in the UK?
The language proficiency requirement is usually IELTS or TOEFL, with a minimum score of 7.0 or 100, respectively.
