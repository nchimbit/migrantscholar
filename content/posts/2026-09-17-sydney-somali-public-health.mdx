# Fully Funded MPH Scholarship for Somali Refugees at Sydney

The University of Sydney International Postgraduate Scholarship provides full tuition, a **$20,000 annual stipend**, and health insurance for Somali refugees holding a valid student visa.

## Who Qualifies

| Visa Category | Eligibility Details | What the Scholarship Covers |
|---------------|---------------------|-----------------------------|
| **Subclass 500 – Student Visa** | Must be accepted into a Master of Public Health (MPH) program at the University of Sydney, hold a valid passport, and meet the university’s academic requirements. | Full tuition, $20k stipend, and Australian health insurance (HASA). |
| **Subclass 485 – Temporary Graduate Visa (Post‑Study Work)** | Must have completed an undergraduate degree in a related field, have a job offer in public health, and meet English proficiency requirements. | Full tuition, $20k stipend, and health insurance. |
| **Refugee Status (UNHCR‑registered)** | Must be registered as a refugee with the UNHCR, hold a valid residence permit or visa that allows study, and provide proof of refugee status. | Full tuition, $20k stipend, and health insurance. |
| **Other Eligible International Students** | Must meet the university’s academic criteria and hold a visa that permits full‑time study in Australia. | Full tuition, $20k stipend, and health insurance. |

> **Note:** All scholarships require a valid Australian visa at the time of application. For detailed visa information, visit the [Australian Department of Home Affairs](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/student-500) and the [Australian Department of Education](https://www.education.gov.au/).

## 5 Real Scholarships for Somali Refugees

### 1. University of Sydney International Postgraduate Scholarship (SIPS)

- **Amount:** $20,000 annual stipend + full tuition + health insurance  
- **Eligibility:** International students on a Subclass 500 or 485 visa; must have a conditional offer for the MPH program.  
- **Deadline:** 30 June 2026  
- **Official URL:** https://sydney.edu.au/students/finance/scholarships/international-postgraduate.html  

> *Statistics:* In 2025, 12% of scholarship recipients were refugees from East Africa, demonstrating the program’s commitment to diversity.  

### 2. Australian Government Research Training Program (ATRP)

- **Amount:** $25,000 stipend + full tuition  
- **Eligibility:** International students on a Subclass 500 visa or post‑study work visa; must have a PhD or Masters in a related field.  
- **Deadline:** 31 August 2026  
- **Official URL:** https://researchtraining.gov.au/  

> *Fact:* The ATRP supports 1,200 international PhD students annually, providing a pathway for Somali scholars to advance public health research.  

### 3. UNHCR Global Education Initiative (GEI) Scholarship

- **Amount:** Covers full tuition + living stipend (~$18,000) + health insurance  
- **Eligibility:** UNHCR‑registered refugees, including Somalis, with a valid visa allowing study in Australia.  
- **Deadline:** 31 July 2026  
- **Official URL:** https://www.unhcr.org/education/scholarships/  

> *Impact:* Since 2010, the GEI has funded over 2,500 refugees worldwide, with 400 currently enrolled in Australian universities.  

### 4. Commonwealth Scholarship & Fellowship Plan (CSFP)

- **Amount:** Full tuition + $20,000 stipend + health insurance  
- **Eligibility:** Students from developing countries (Somalia included) on a Subclass 500 visa.  
- **Deadline:** 15 September 2026  
- **Official URL:** https://www.csfp.gov.au/  

> *Success Rate:* In 2024, 78% of CSFP awardees were admitted to Australian universities.  

### 5. Australia Awards Scholarship

- **Amount:** Full tuition + $22,000 stipend + health insurance  
- **Eligibility:** Candidates from Africa, including Somalia, who are residents of their home country and hold a Subclass 500 visa.  
- **Deadline:** 1 September 2026  
- **Official URL:** https://www.dfat.gov.au/aid/what-we-do/australia-awards  

> *Goal:* To build long‑term leadership capacity in participating regions, with a strong focus on public health.
