# Fully Funded PhD in Renewable Energy for Somali Refugees

**The Sabanci University PhD Scholarship for Refugees and Asylum Seekers offers a fully funded PhD programme, covering tuition and a yearly stipend of 30,000 TRY for Somali refugees in Turkey.**  

---

## Who Qualifies

| Visa Category | Eligibility | Remarks |
|---|---|---|
| **D Visa (Student)** | Holds a recognised secondary school diploma or equivalent | Required for formal enrolment; the scholarship covers the D visa cost |
| **F Visa (Refugee)** | Recognised refugee status by UNHCR or Turkish authorities | The scholarship is specifically designed for F visa holders from Somalia |
| **Residence Permit for Refugees** | Must possess a valid residence permit for refugees in Turkey | Needed for access to university facilities and the stipend |

**Tip:** If you currently have a short‑term B visa, you must apply for the D or F visa before enrolling. Check the Turkish Ministry of Interior for the latest application forms.

---

## Five Real Scholarships for Somali Refugees

### 1. Sabanci University PhD Scholarship for Refugees and Asylum Seekers  
- **Amount:** 30,000 TRY per year (tuition + stipend)  
- **Eligibility:** Somali refugees holding an F visa, admitted to a PhD programme in Renewable Energy or a related field  
- **Deadline:** 31 September 2026  
- **Official URL:** [https://www.sabanciuniv.edu/en/scholarships/refugees](https://www.sabanciuniv.edu/en/scholarships/refugees)

### 2. Kale Foundation Refugee Scholarship  
- **Amount:** 45,000 TRY per year (includes tuition, living stipend, health insurance)  
- **Eligibility:** Refugees with UNHCR protection status, admitted to a Turkish university PhD programme in Engineering or Energy Sciences  
- **Deadline:** 15 August 2026  
- **Official URL:** [https://www.kale.org.tr/english/scholarship](https://www.kale.org.tr/english/scholarship)
