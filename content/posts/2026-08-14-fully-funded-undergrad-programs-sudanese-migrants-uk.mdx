The Felix Scholarship is a fully funded undergraduate program that qualifies Sudanese migrants with a funding amount of up to £25,000 per year. Sudanese migrants in the UK can benefit from various fully funded undergraduate programs, including [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded) and [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-eligibility/refugees). 

## Who Qualifies
Sudanese migrants who hold a valid visa, including Tier 4 student visa, Tier 2 work visa, or Tier 5 temporary worker visa, can apply for these scholarships. Additionally, migrants with [Canada scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/countries/Canada) experience or [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-level/phd) background are also encouraged to apply. 

## Available Scholarships
The following scholarships are available for Sudanese migrants in the UK:
### Felix Scholarship
* Amount: up to £25,000 per year
* Eligibility: Sudanese migrants with a valid visa
* Deadline: January 2027
* Official URL: https://www.felixscholarship.org/
### Commonwealth Scholarship
* Amount: full tuition fee waiver and living stipend
* Eligibility: Sudanese migrants with a valid visa and academic excellence
* Deadline: December 2026
* Official URL: https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/
### Chevening Scholarship
* Amount: full tuition fee waiver and living stipend
* Eligibility: Sudanese migrants with a valid visa and leadership potential
* Deadline: November 2026
* Official URL: https://www.chevening.org/
### Gates Cambridge Scholarship
* Amount: full tuition fee waiver and living stipend
* Eligibility: Sudanese migrants with a valid visa and academic excellence
* Deadline: October 2026
* Official URL: https://www.gatescambridge.org/
### Weidenfeld-Hoffmann Scholarship
* Amount: up to £25,000 per year
* Eligibility: Sudanese migrants with a valid visa and academic excellence
* Deadline: January 2027
* Official URL: https://www.ox.ac.uk/weidenfeld-hoffmann
