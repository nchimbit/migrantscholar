The [Fulbright Foreign Student Program](https://fulbright.americancouncils.org/) exists and qualifies Afghan women to receive up to $100,000 in funding for STEM fields in the USA. The Fulbright Foreign Student Program is a fully funded scholarship that covers tuition, living expenses, and travel costs for international students, including Afghan women, to pursue higher education in the United States.

## Who Qualifies
Afghan women who hold a valid F-1 or J-1 visa, or are eligible for a visa, can apply for these scholarships. This includes women who are refugees, asylum seekers, or have been granted special immigrant status. Visa categories that may be eligible include F-1, J-1, F-2, J-2, and others. Eligible applicants must also meet the requirements for each scholarship, such as academic background, language proficiency, and field of study.

## Available Scholarships
### Fulbright Foreign Student Program
* Amount: up to $100,000
* Eligibility: Afghan women with a bachelor's degree and a minimum of two years of work experience
* Deadline: varies by country
* Official URL: https://fulbright.americancouncils.org/
### AAUW International Fellowships
* Amount: $18,000-$30,000
* Eligibility: Afghan women who are pursuing a master's or PhD in a STEM field
* Deadline: November 15
* Official URL: https://www.aauw.org/
### P.E.O. International Peace Scholarship
* Amount: up to $12,500
* Eligibility: Afghan women who are pursuing a graduate degree in a STEM field
* Deadline: December 15
* Official URL: https://www.peointernational.org/
### Margaret McNamara Memorial Fund
* Amount: up to $15,000
* Eligibility: Afghan women who are pursuing a graduate degree in a STEM field
* Deadline: January 15
* Official URL: https://www.mmmf.org/
### MIT-Zaragona Women in STEM Scholarship
* Amount: full tuition
* Eligibility: Afghan women who are pursuing a master's or PhD in a STEM field at MIT
* Deadline: January 15
* Official URL: https://www.mit.edu/

## Application Steps
1. Research the scholarship and review the eligibility criteria
2. Review the application requirements and gather necessary documents
3. Submit the application through the official website or email

5. Wait for the application to be reviewed and a decision to be made
6. Receive and review the scholarship award letter
7. Accept the award and complete any additional requirements
8. Begin the visa application process and prepare for travel to the USA

## Frequently Asked Questions
1. What types of STEM fields are eligible for these scholarships?
 Answer: Most STEM fields are eligible, including engineering, computer science, biology, chemistry, and physics.
2. Can I apply for multiple scholarships at the same time?
 Answer: Yes, but be sure to review the eligibility criteria and application requirements for each scholarship.
3. Do I need to have a certain level of English proficiency to apply?
 Answer: Yes, most scholarships require a minimum level of English proficiency, such as a TOEFL score of 80 or IELTS score of 6.5.
4. Can I bring my family with me to the USA on a student visa?
 Answer: It depends on the type of visa and the scholarship requirements, but generally, family members are not eligible to accompany the student to the USA.
5. How long does the application process take?
 Answer: The application process can take several months to a year or more, depending on the scholarship and the applicant's circumstances.
6. Can I apply for a scholarship if I am already in the USA on a different type of visa?
 Answer: It depends on the scholarship and the visa requirements, but generally, applicants who are already in the USA on a different type of visa may be eligible to apply.
7. What types of expenses are covered by the scholarship?
 Answer: Most scholarships cover tuition, living expenses, and travel costs, but the specific expenses covered may vary depending on the scholarship.
8. Can I renew my scholarship for multiple years?
 Answer: It depends on the scholarship and the applicant's progress, but some scholarships may be renewable for multiple years.
