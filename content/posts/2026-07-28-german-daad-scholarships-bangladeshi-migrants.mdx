The DAAD Scholarship exists and qualifies Bangladeshi migrants with a fully funded scholarship amount of up to €750 per month for master's and PhD students. 
As a Bangladeshi migrant, you can apply for various fully funded scholarships in Germany, including [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-funding/fully-funded) and [DAAD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/universities/daad). 
To be eligible, you need to have a strong academic background, particularly in [STEM](https://migrantscholar.com/by-field/stem) fields, and meet the specific requirements of each scholarship program.

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for these scholarships, Bangladeshi migrants must have a valid visa and be in one of the following visa categories: student visa, research visa, or work visa. 
Additionally, they must have a bachelor's or master's degree in a relevant field and meet the language requirements, which is typically German language proficiency or English language proficiency, depending on the program. 
Some scholarships also require a certain level of work experience or research experience.

### Scholarship 1: DAAD Scholarship
* Amount: up to €750 per month
* Eligibility: Bangladeshi migrants with a bachelor's or master's degree in a relevant field
* Deadline: varies depending on the program
* Official URL: https://www.daad.de/en/

### Scholarship 2: Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung Scholarship
* Amount: up to €1,000 per month
* Eligibility: Bangladeshi migrants with a bachelor's or master's degree in a relevant field and a strong commitment to social and political engagement
* Deadline: July 15th
* Official URL: https://www.kas.de/en/

### Scholarship 3: Friedrich-Ebert-Stiftung Scholarship
* Amount: up to €1,200 per month
* Eligibility: Bangladeshi migrants with a bachelor's or master's degree in a relevant field and a strong commitment to social democracy
* Deadline: November 30th
* Official URL: https://www.fes.de/en/

### Scholarship 4: Heinrich-Böll-Stiftung Scholarship
* Amount: up to €1,500 per month
* Eligibility: Bangladeshi migrants with a bachelor's or master's degree in a relevant field and a strong commitment to green policies
* Deadline: March 1st
* Official URL: https://www.boell.de/en

### Scholarship 5: Deutschlandstipendium
* Amount: up to €300 per month
* Eligibility: Bangladeshi migrants with a bachelor's or master's degree in a relevant field and a strong academic background
* Deadline: varies depending on the university
* Official URL: https://www.deutschlandstipendium.de/en/

## Application Steps
1. Check the eligibility criteria for each scholarship program and make sure you meet the requirements.
2. Choose the scholarship program that best fits your academic background and career goals.
3. Prepare all the necessary documents, including your passport, visa, and academic transcripts.
4. Fill out the online application form and submit it before the deadline.
5. Upload all the required documents, including your motivation letter and language proficiency certificate.
6. Pay the application fee, if required.
7. Submit your application and wait for the response from the scholarship committee.
8. If you are selected, you will receive an email with further instructions on how to proceed.
