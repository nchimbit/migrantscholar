**Emergency Funding for UK Migrant Students via Erasmus+**  

The Chevening UK Scholarship provides up to £18,000 per year to students from over 160 countries, covering tuition, living expenses, and travel costs, making it an ideal emergency funding source for migrants aiming to secure a student visa in the UK.

## Who Qualifies  
Migrants seeking study in the UK
