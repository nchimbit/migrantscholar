The Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP) Scholarship exists and qualifies African migrants to receive up to AUD 28,597 per annum, fully funding their PhD programs in STEM fields at Australian universities.

## Who Qualifies
African migrants with a valid Australian visa, including but not limited to, the Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485), the Skilled Independent visa (subclass 189), the Skilled Nominated visa (subclass 190), and the Refugee visa (subclass 200 or 201), may be eligible to apply for fully funded PhD programs in Australia. 

## Available Scholarships
### Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP) Scholarship
The Australian Government Research Training Program (RTP) Scholarship provides up to AUD 28,597 per annum, is open to international students, including African migrants, and the deadline varies depending on the university. For more information, visit [https://www.education.gov.au/research-training-program](https://www.education.gov.au/research-training-program).

### University of Melbourne Graduate Research Scholarships
The University of Melbourne Graduate Research Scholarships provide a living allowance of up to AUD 31,200 per annum, is open to international students, including African migrants, and the deadline is October 31. For more information, visit [https://gradresearch.unimelb.edu.au/funding](https://gradresearch.unimelb.edu.au/funding).

### Australian National University (ANU) PhD Scholarships
The Australian National University (ANU) PhD Scholarships provide a stipend of up to AUD 28,597 per annum, is open to international students, including African migrants, and the deadline varies depending on the college. For more information, visit [https://www.anu.edu.au/study/scholarships](https://www.anu.edu.au/study/scholarships).

### University of Sydney International Scholarship
The University of Sydney International Scholarship provides up to AUD 35,000 per annum, is open to international students, including African migrants, and the deadline varies depending on the course. For more information, visit [https://sydney.edu.au/scholarships](https://sydney.edu.au/scholarships).

### Monash University Graduate Scholarship
The Monash University Graduate Scholarship provides a living allowance of up to AUD 29,500 per annum, is open to international students, including African migrants, and the deadline varies depending on the faculty. For more information, visit [https://www.monash.edu/graduate-research/funding](https://www.monash.edu/graduate-research/funding).
