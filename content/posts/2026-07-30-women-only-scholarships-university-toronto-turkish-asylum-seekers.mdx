# Women Scholarships
The University of Toronto's [Loran Scholars Foundation](https://loranscholar.ca/) offers a scholarship to female asylum seekers from Turkey, providing up to $100,000 in funding over four years. 
The scholarship is part of the [Canada scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/countries/Canada) and [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-funding/fully-funded) available to international students.

## Who Qualifies
Female asylum seekers from Turkey who have been granted refugee status in Canada under the [Protected Person](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/refugees/canada-role.html) or [Convention Refugee](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/refugees/canada-role.html) categories are eligible to apply. Applicants must also meet the admission requirements for the University of Toronto and demonstrate financial need.

## Available Scholarships
### Ontario Graduate Scholarship
* Amount: $15,000 per year
* Eligibility: Open to graduate students, including [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-level/phd) candidates
* Deadline: Varies by department
* Official URL: https://www.ontario.ca/page/ontario-graduate-scholarship

### Loran Scholars Foundation
* Amount: Up to $100,000 over four years
* Eligibility: Open to undergraduate students, including [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.com/by-eligibility/refugees)
* Deadline: October 27, 2026
* Official URL: https://loranscholar.ca/

### Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarship
* Amount: $50,000 per year
* Eligibility: Open to graduate students, including [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-level/phd) candidates
* Deadline: November 1, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.canada.ca/en/research-funding-agencies/programs/vanier-canada-graduate-scholarships.html

### University of Toronto Scholarship
* Amount: Up to $10,000 per year
* Eligibility: Open to undergraduate and graduate students, including [Canada scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/countries/Canada) recipients
* Deadline: Varies by department
* Official URL: https://future.utoronto.ca/scholarships/

### Pierre Elliott Trudeau Foundation Scholarship
* Amount: Up to $60,000 per year
* Eligibility: Open to graduate students, including [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-level/phd) candidates
* Deadline: December 21, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.trudeaufoundation.ca/en/programs/scholarship
