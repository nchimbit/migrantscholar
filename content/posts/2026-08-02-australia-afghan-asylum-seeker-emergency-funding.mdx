# Afghan Aid
The Australia Awards Scholarships exist for Afghan asylum seekers, providing up to AUD 30,000 per year for women to pursue higher education in Australia. 

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for emergency funding, Afghan asylum seekers in Australia must hold a valid visa, such as a Temporary Protection Visa (TPV) or a Safe Haven Enterprise Visa (SHEV), and meet specific eligibility criteria set by each scholarship provider. 

## Available Scholarships
### Australian Awards Scholarship
* Amount: up to AUD 30,000 per year
* Eligibility: Afghan women asylum seekers
* Deadline: 30 April 2026
* Official URL: https://www.australiaawards.gov.au/
### Zonta International Amelia Earhart Fellowship
* Amount: USD 10,000
* Eligibility: Women pursuing PhD in aerospace-related fields
* Deadline: 15 November 2026
* Official URL: https://www.zonta.org/
### Australia Council for the Arts Scholarship
* Amount: up to AUD 20,000
* Eligibility: Women artists and creatives
* Deadline: 31 August 2026
* Official URL: https://www.australiacouncil.gov.au/
### Westpac Future Leaders Scholarship
* Amount: up to AUD 120,000 over 2-3 years
* Eligibility: Women leaders pursuing postgraduate studies
* Deadline: 30 September 2026
* Official URL: https://www.westpac.com.au/
### Australian National University Scholarship
* Amount: up to AUD 28,597 per year
* Eligibility: Women pursuing undergraduate or postgraduate studies
* Deadline: 31 March 2026
* Official URL: https://www.anu.edu.au/
