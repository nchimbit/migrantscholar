The German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD) Scholarships offers fully funded scholarships to refugee students in STEM fields, with a funding amount of up to €750 per month, to pursue their higher education in Germany.

## Who Qualifies
The DAAD Scholarships are open to refugee students who hold a visa category of asylum, subsidiary protection, or a residence permit in Germany. To be eligible, applicants must have a strong academic background in a STEM field and meet the language requirements of the desired university. Applicants with a visa category of family reunification or other humanitarian reasons may also be considered.

## Available Scholarships
The following are five real scholarships available to refugee students in STEM fields:
### DAAD Scholarship Program
* Amount: up to €750 per month
* Eligibility: Refugee students in STEM fields
* Deadline: varies
* Official URL: https://www.daad.de/en/
### Deutschlandstipendium
* Amount: €300 per month
* Eligibility: Students from all disciplines, including STEM fields
* Deadline: varies
* Official URL: https://www.deutschlandstipendium.de/en/
### Friedrich Ebert Foundation Scholarship
* Amount: up to €744 per month
* Eligibility: Students from all disciplines, including STEM fields
* Deadline: varies
* Official URL: https://www.fes.de/en/
### Heinrich Böll Foundation Scholarship
* Amount: up to €1,500 per month
* Eligibility: Students from all disciplines, including STEM fields
* Deadline: varies
* Official URL: https://www.boell.de/en
### Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung Scholarship
* Amount: up to €1,200 per month
* Eligibility: Students from all disciplines, including STEM fields
* Deadline: varies
* Official URL: https://www.kas.de/en/

## Application Steps
To apply for the DAAD Scholarships, follow these steps:
1. Check the eligibility criteria and ensure you meet the requirements.
2. Choose your desired course of study and university.
3. Gather all required documents, including transcripts, diplomas, and language certificates.
4. Submit your application through the DAAD online portal.
5. Pay the application fee, if required.
6. Wait for the selection process to be completed.
7. Receive an acceptance letter and apply for a visa, if required.
8. Arrive in Germany and begin your studies.

## Documents Checklist
To apply for the DAAD Scholarships, you will need the following documents:
* Passport copy

* Academic transcripts and diplomas
* Language certificates (e.g. TOEFL, IELTS, or German language certificate)
* Proof of financial support, if required
* Health insurance proof
* Motivation letter
* Recommendation letters, if required
