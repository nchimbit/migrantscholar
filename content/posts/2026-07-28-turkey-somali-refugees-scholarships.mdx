The scholarship aims to support the education of refugees in Turkey, promoting their integration into Turkish society. 
To be eligible, applicants must be refugees residing in Turkey, holding a valid visa, and meeting specific requirements such as language proficiency and academic achievement. 
For more information on visa categories, visit the [Turkish Government's official website](https://www.turkiye.gov.tr/en).

Somali refugees in Turkey who hold a valid visa, such as a residence permit or a student visa, are eligible to apply for the Turkish Government Scholarship.

- Refugee residence permit
