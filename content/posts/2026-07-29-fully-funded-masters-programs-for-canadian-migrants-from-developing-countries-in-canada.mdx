# Funded Masters
The Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarship exists and qualifies Canadian migrants from developing countries, with a funding amount of $50,000 per year for three years, to pursue a Master's degree in Canada.

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for fully funded Master's programs in Canada as a migrant from a developing country, candidates must hold a valid Canadian visa in categories such as study permits, work permits, or permanent resident visas. This includes but is not limited to, refugee claimants, protected persons, and migrant workers. Visa categories that qualify for these scholarships include but are not limited to: study permits, work permits, and permanent resident visas. ## Available Scholarships
### Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarship
The Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarship provides $50,000 per year for three years to Canadian migrants from developing countries, with eligibility limited to those who have achieved a first-class average, as determined by their institution, in each of the last two years of full-time study. Deadline: November 1, 2026. [Official URL](https://www.vanier.gc.ca/en/home-accueil.html)
### Master's Scholarship by the University of Toronto
The Master's Scholarship by the University of Toronto offers $10,000 to $50,000 per year, eligible to international students including migrants from developing countries, with a deadline of January 15, 2027. [Official URL](https://www.utoronto.ca/)
### Ontario Graduate Scholarship
The Ontario Graduate Scholarship provides $15,000 per year for two years, open to international students including migrants from developing countries studying in Ontario, Canada. Deadline: October 31, 2026. [Official URL](https://osap.gov.on.ca/-/ontario-graduate-scholarship)
### Quebec Merit Scholarship for Foreign Students
The Quebec Merit Scholarship for Foreign Students offers $25,000 to $50,000 for the duration of the study program, open to international students including migrants from developing countries, with a deadline of September 1, 2026. [Official URL](https://www.mrif.gouv.qc.ca/en/home)
### Pierre Elliott Trudeau Foundation Scholarship
The Pierre Elliott Trudeau Foundation Scholarship provides $60,000 for three years, open to international students including migrants from developing countries, with a deadline of December 21, 2026. [Official URL](https://www.trudeaufoundation.ca/en)
