## UK Emergency Housing Grants for Ukrainian Refugees 2026

The Imperial College Ukraine Refugee Scholarship, worth up to £15 000 per year, is available to Ukrainian refugees holding a Tier 5 visa or UK residence status.  

---

### Who Qualifies for Emergency Housing Grants?

| Visa / Status | Eligibility Criteria | Notes |
|---------------|----------------------|-------|
| **Tier 5 (Temporary Worker)** | Must have a valid Tier 5 visa issued before 30 September 2026 and be a registered Ukrainian refugee. | Includes the *Temporary Worker – Government Authorised Exchange* (TWGAE) route. |
| **UK Settlement Status (Refugee or Humanitarian)** | Must hold a valid refugee or humanitarian protection status in the UK. | Applies to those settled under the 2023–24 Ukrainian Resettlement Scheme. |
| **Other UK Visas (e.g., Student, Skilled Worker)** | Must demonstrate that they have been granted refugee status or are in the UK under a humanitarian protection programme. | Temporary accommodation is offered on a case‑by‑case basis. |
