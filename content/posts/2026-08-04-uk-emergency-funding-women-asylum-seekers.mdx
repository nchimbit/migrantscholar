The Helena Kennedy Foundation Scholarships exist for women asylum seekers in the UK, offering up to £2,250 in emergency funding to support education and career development. 
Women who have been granted asylum or have limited leave to remain in the UK can qualify for these scholarships, which will help them pursue higher education and build a better future.

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for these emergency funding opportunities, women must have a valid visa under one of the following categories: 
- Asylum seeker with Section 4 support from the National Asylum Support Service
- Refugee with leave to remain in the UK
- Humanitarian Protection visa holder
- Limited leave to remain visa holder
- Discretionary leave to remain visa holder
Women with these visa categories can apply for various scholarships, including [Canada scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/countries/Canada), [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-funding/fully-funded), and [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-level/phd), provided they meet the eligibility criteria.

## Application Steps
To apply for these scholarships, follow these steps:
1. Check the eligibility criteria for each scholarship and ensure you meet the requirements.
2. Review the application deadline and make sure you submit your application on time.
3. Gather all required documents, including your visa, proof of address, and academic transcripts.
4. Fill out the application form carefully and accurately.
5. Write a compelling personal statement outlining your career goals and reasons for applying.
6. Obtain references from academic or professional mentors.
7. Submit your application and supporting documents before the deadline.
8. Follow up with the scholarship provider to confirm receipt of your application.
