The Women's Refugee Commission's scholarship exists and qualifies women refugees pursuing healthcare careers in the USA with up to $2,000 in emergency funding. 
The scholarship aims to support women refugees who are facing financial difficulties in pursuing their education and careers in the healthcare sector. 
To be eligible for this scholarship, applicants must be women refugees who are currently residing in the USA and are pursuing a career in the healthcare sector.

## Who Qualifies
This scholarship is open to women refugees who are currently residing in the USA and hold a valid visa, including but not limited to, asylum status, refugee status, or special immigrant visa categories such as SIV, SQ, or SI. 
Applicants must also be enrolled in a healthcare-related program at an accredited institution in the USA, such as [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded) for [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-level/phd) or [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-eligibility/refugees).

## Available Scholarships
### American Association of University Women (AAUW) Emergency Funding
The AAUW provides emergency funding to women who are pursuing a degree in the USA and are facing financial difficulties. 
The funding amount is up to $5,000. 
Eligibility includes women who are pursuing a degree in the USA and are facing financial difficulties. 
The deadline is ongoing. 
For more information, visit [www.aauw.org](http://www.aauw.org).

### The National Alliance to Nurture the Aged and the Youth (NANAY) Scholarship
The NANAY scholarship provides funding to women who are pursuing a career in the healthcare sector. 
The funding amount is up to $1,000. 
Eligibility includes women who are pursuing a career in the healthcare sector. 
The deadline is June 30, 2026. 
For more information, visit [www.nanay.org](http://www.nanay.org).

### The Women's Refugee Commission Scholarship
The Women's Refugee Commission scholarship provides funding to women refugees who are pursuing a career in the healthcare sector. 
The funding amount is up to $2,000. 
Eligibility includes women refugees who are pursuing a career in the healthcare sector. 
The deadline is December 31, 2026. 
For more information, visit [www.womensrefugeecommission.org](http://www.womensrefugeecommission.org).

### The Asylum Seeker Advocacy Project (ASAP) Scholarship
The ASAP scholarship provides funding to asylum seekers who are pursuing a career in the healthcare sector. 
The funding amount is up to $1,500. 
Eligibility includes asylum seekers who are pursuing a career in the healthcare sector. 
The deadline is September 30, 2026. 
For more information, visit [www.asylumadvocacy.org](http://www.asylumadvocacy.org).

### The Society of Refugee Healthcare Providers (SRHP) Scholarship
The SRHP scholarship provides funding to refugees who are pursuing a career in the healthcare sector. 
The funding amount is up to $2,500. 
Eligibility includes refugees who are pursuing a career in the healthcare sector. 
The deadline is March 31, 2026. 
For more information, visit [www.srhp.org](http://www.srhp.org).
