The Afghan Scholarships by the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA) exist and qualify Afghan asylum seekers for funding of up to $10,000 per year. 
Afghan asylum seekers in the USA can apply for various scholarships to support their education and living expenses. 
To be eligible for these scholarships, applicants must hold a valid asylum seeker visa, such as an F-1 or J-1 visa, or have a pending asylum application with a valid visa category like F-1, J-1, or B-1/B-2.

## Who Qualifies
Asylum seekers with the following visa categories may be eligible for emergency funding: F-1, J-1, or B-1/B-2. 
Additionally, applicants must meet specific requirements, such as being enrolled in a US university or having a strong academic record, to be considered for these scholarships.

## Scholarships
### The Paul and Daisy Soros Fellowships for New Americans
The Paul and Daisy Soros Fellowships for New Americans offers up to $90,000 for graduate studies, eligibility is restricted to New Americans, deadline is in November, and the official URL is https://www.pdsoros.org.
### The Asylum Seeker Scholarship
The Asylum Seeker Scholarship by the [University of California, Berkeley](https://migrantscholar.com/universities/university-of-california-berkeley) provides up to $5,000 for undergraduate studies, eligibility is restricted to asylum seekers, deadline is in February, and the official URL is https://financialaid.berkeley.edu.
### The [Fulbright Scholarship](https://migrantscholar.com/universities/fulbright) 
The Fulbright Scholarship offers up to $100,000 for graduate studies, eligibility is restricted to international students, deadline is in October, and the official URL is https://us.fulbrightonline.org.
### The [International Student Scholarship](https://migrantscholar.com/scholarships/international-student-scholarship)
The International Student Scholarship by the University of Wisconsin-Madison provides up to $10,000 for undergraduate studies, eligibility is restricted to international students, deadline is in March, and the official URL is https://www.wisc.edu.
### The [Refugee Scholarship](https://migrantscholar.com/by-eligibility/refugees) 
The Refugee Scholarship by the [University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA)](https://migrantscholar.com/universities/university-of-california-los-angeles) offers up to $5,000 for undergraduate studies, eligibility is restricted to refugees, deadline is in April, and the official URL is https://www.ucla.edu.
