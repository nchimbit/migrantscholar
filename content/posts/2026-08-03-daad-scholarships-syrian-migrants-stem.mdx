The German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD) Scholarships offers fully funded opportunities to Syrian migrants in STEM fields, with a funding amount of up to €750 per month, to pursue their academic goals in Germany. 
As a [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded) recipient, Syrian migrants can enjoy a life-changing experience in Germany, with access to world-class education and research opportunities. 
This scholarship is specifically designed for [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-eligibility/refugees) and [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-level/phd) in STEM fields.

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for the DAAD Scholarships, Syrian migrants must hold a valid visa, such as a student visa or a residence permit, and meet specific eligibility criteria. 
The following visa categories are eligible: 
- Subsidiary protection 
- Refugee status 
- Temporary protection 
- Other humanitarian grounds 
Applicants must also meet the [scholarship deadlines](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/deadlines) and requirements set by the DAAD.

## Available Scholarships
The following are some of the available scholarships for Syrian migrants in STEM fields:
### DAAD Scholarship for Master's Studies
Amount: €750 per month 
Eligibility: Bachelor's degree in a STEM field 
Deadline: October 31st 
Official URL: https://www.daad.de/en/study-and-research-in-germany/scholarships/ 
### Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung Scholarship
Amount: €1,200 per month 
Eligibility: Bachelor's or Master's degree in a STEM field 
Deadline: July 15th 
Official URL: https://www.kas.de/en/scholarships/ 
### Friedrich-Ebert-Stiftung Scholarship
Amount: €1,200 per month 
Eligibility: Bachelor's or Master's degree in a STEM field 
Deadline: November 30th 
Official URL: https://www.fes.de/en/scholarships/ 
### Heinrich-Böll-Stiftung Scholarship
Amount: €1,200 per month 
Eligibility: Bachelor's or Master's degree in a STEM field 
Deadline: September 1st 
Official URL: https://www.boell.de/en/foundation/scholarships 
### Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft (DFG) Scholarship
Amount: €2,000 per month 
Eligibility: PhD in a STEM field 
Deadline: January 15th 
Official URL: https://www.dfg.de/en/research_funding/ 

## Application Steps
Here are the steps to apply for the DAAD Scholarships:
1. Check the eligibility criteria and deadline for the scholarship 
2. Prepare the required documents, including a CV, motivation letter, and language proficiency certificate 
3. Submit the application online through the DAAD portal 
4. Wait for the selection committee to review the application 
5. Receive the scholarship award letter and confirm acceptance 
6. Apply for a student visa or residence permit 
7. Register at the university and begin studies 
8. Maintain a good academic record and comply with the scholarship terms and conditions
