The German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD) offers fully funded STEM scholarships to Afghan refugees in Germany, providing a funding amount of €750 per month for undergraduate and graduate students. These scholarships are designed to support Afghan refugees pursuing degrees in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM) fields. The DAAD scholarship program aims to promote academic excellence and cultural exchange between Germany and Afghanistan.

## Who Qualifies
* Afghan refugees with a valid refugee status in Germany
* Applicants with a strong academic background in STEM fields
* Students who have been admitted to a German university for undergraduate or graduate studies
* Candidates who demonstrate a high level of language proficiency in English (IELTS or equivalent)
* Applicants who are not older than 35 years of age

## What the Scholarship Covers
The DAAD STEM scholarship provides a monthly stipend of €750 for undergraduate and graduate students, which covers living expenses, tuition fees, and other study-related costs. The scholarship also includes:
* A one-time payment of €250 for travel and accommodation expenses
* A monthly payment of €100 for health insurance
* A language course in Germany before the start of the academic program
* A study allowance of €150 per month for books, materials, and other study-related expenses

## How to Apply
1. Check the official DAAD website for the latest information on the scholarship program and application deadlines.

4. Upload all required documents to the online application portal.
5. Pay the application fee (if applicable) and submit the payment receipt.
6. Wait for the DAAD to review your application and contact you for an interview (if shortlisted).
