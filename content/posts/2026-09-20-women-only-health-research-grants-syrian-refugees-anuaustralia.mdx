# Women‑Only Health Research Grants for Syrian Refugees at ANU  

The ANU International PhD Scholarship offers up to AUD 38,000 per year for international women pursuing health research, including Syrian refugees holding a humanitarian visa.  

## Who Qualifies  

| Visa Category | Eligibility Notes |
|---------------|-------------------|
| **Humanitarian Visa (Subclass 200)** | Accepted for ANU scholarships that require international student status. |
| **Refugee Visa (Subclass 201, 203, 300, 301, 302, 503)** | Eligible if the visa allows study and you are a woman. |
| **Temporary Graduate Visa (Subclass 485)** | Can apply if you completed an Australian tertiary qualification within the last 4 years. |
| **Student Visa (Subclass 500)** | Accepts international students, including refugees, if they meet the scholarship criteria. |
| **Other Humanitarian and Refugee‑Related Visas** | Check specific scholarship guidelines; many ANU awards are open to all international students. |

*All scholarships require that you be a woman, be enrolled in a health‑related PhD, Master’s, or research program, and hold a valid visa that permits study in Australia.*

> **Tip:** Many scholarships overlap; you can apply to more than one, but you must not claim the same funding twice for the same study period.  

## Eight Step Application Process  

1. **Confirm Visa Eligibility** – Check your visa subclass and ensure it allows full‑time study.  
2. **Choose Your Program** – Secure an offer from ANU’s School of Medicine or Health Sciences for a PhD/Master’s in health research.  
3. **Review Scholarship Guidelines** – Each scholarship has unique application forms, documents, and deadlines.  
4. **Prepare Your Research Proposal** – Highlight health‑related questions relevant to Syrian refugee communities.  
5. **Gather Supporting Documents** – See the checklist below for required certificates, transcripts, and proof of visa status.  
6. **Complete Online Application Forms** – Use the scholarship portal (e.g., ANU Scholarship portal or Australian Government portal).  
7. **Submit and Pay Fees** – Some scholarships require a nominal processing fee; ensure you keep receipts.  
8. **Await Decision & Confirm Acceptance** – Once offered, accept by the deadline and provide any additional paperwork.

| Document | Purpose | Notes |

| **Passport‑sized Photo** | Identification | For scholarship certificates. |

*Ensure every document is in English or accompanied by a certified translation.*  

## FAQs for Syrian Refugee Applicants  

1. **Can I apply if I only have a temporary visa?**  
   Yes, many scholarships accept holders of Subclass 485, 500, or 200 visas, provided the visa permits full‑time study.  

2. **Do I need to speak English fluently?**  
   If your prior tertiary education was in English, you’re exempt from an English proficiency test. Otherwise, an IELTS score of 6.5 or equivalent is typically required.  

3. **Will the scholarship cover health‑related living costs?**  
   Most scholarships cover tuition and a living stipend (e.g., AUD 38,000 per year). Check each scholarship’s exact stipend details.  

4. **Do I need a sponsor to study in Australia?**  
   Not for scholarship applicants; the scholarship covers tuition and living costs, removing the need for a private sponsor.
