The University of Melbourne Human Rights Scholarship exists and qualifies Sudanese asylum seekers in Australia, providing up to $100,000 in funding. 
The University of Melbourne offers various scholarships to support Sudanese asylum seekers in pursuing their higher education goals in Australia. 
To be eligible for these scholarships, applicants must meet specific visa category requirements, including being a holder of a Bridging Visa E, Temporary Protection Visa, or Safe Haven Enterprise Visa.

## Who Qualifies
To qualify for the University of Melbourne scholarships, Sudanese asylum seekers must hold a valid visa, including Bridging Visa E (BVE), Temporary Protection Visa (TPV), or Safe Haven Enterprise Visa (SHEV). 
They must also meet the eligibility criteria for each scholarship, which may include academic merit, language proficiency, and financial need.

$30,000, eligibility: international students, deadline: October 2026, [official URL](https://scholarships.unimelb.edu.au/awards/melbourne-graduate-scholarship)

$5,000, eligibility: undergraduate students, deadline: March 2027, [official URL](https://scholarships.unimelb.edu.au/awards/access-scholarship)

$10,000, eligibility: undergraduate students, deadline: January 2027, [official URL](https://scholarships.unimelb.edu.au/awards/chancellors-scholarship)
