# Aid
The Albert Einstein German Academic Refugee Initiative (DAFI) scholarship exists and qualifies Syrian refugees in Turkey with an amount of up to €500 per month for undergraduate studies. This initiative is part of the [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-eligibility/refugees) program, specifically designed to support displaced individuals in pursuing higher education. As a [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded) program, DAFI aims to cover all necessary expenses for refugee students.

## Who Qualifies
To qualify for emergency funding and university scholarships in Turkey as a Syrian refugee, applicants typically fall under specific visa categories such as the humanitarian residence permit or student residence permit. The Turkish government, in collaboration with international organizations, offers various [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-level/phd) and undergraduate programs to support refugees. These efforts are part of broader initiatives like [Canada scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/countries/Canada) and [DAAD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/universities/daad), which provide educational opportunities worldwide.

## Available Scholarships
The following scholarships are available for Syrian refugees in Turkey:
### DAFI Scholarship
* Amount: Up to €500 per month
* Eligibility: Syrian refugees with a humanitarian residence permit or student residence permit
* Deadline: Varies
* Official URL: https://www.unhcr.org/dafi-scholarship-programme
### Turkish Government Scholarship
* Amount: Full tuition fee coverage, monthly stipend, and health insurance
* Eligibility: International students, including Syrian refugees, with a strong academic background
* Deadline: February to March each year
* Official URL: https://www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr
### Erasmus Mundus Scholarship
* Amount: Full tuition fee coverage, monthly stipend, and travel costs
* Eligibility: International students, including Syrian refugees, applying for master's programs in Europe
* Deadline: Varies by program, typically between November and January
* Official URL: https://erasmusmundus.uniba.it
### Islamic Development Bank Scholarship
* Amount: Full tuition fee coverage, monthly stipend, and health insurance
* Eligibility: Students from member countries, including Syria, with a strong academic background
* Deadline: February to April each year
* Official URL: https://www.isdb.org/scholarships
### United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees (UNHCR) Scholarship
* Amount: Up to $5,000 per year
* Eligibility: Refugee students, including Syrians, with a strong academic background
* Deadline: Varies
* Official URL: https://www.unhcr.org/education

## Application Steps
1. Research available scholarships and their specific requirements.
2. Check the eligibility criteria and ensure you meet all the conditions.
3. Prepare all necessary documents, including academic transcripts and language proficiency certificates.
4. Submit your application before the deadline, either online or through the relevant embassy.
5. Wait for the review process, which may involve interviews or additional assessments.
6. Receive notification of the scholarship decision, which can take several months.
7. Upon selection, finalize your admission to the chosen university and arrange for travel and accommodation.
8. Maintain good academic standing and comply with the scholarship's conditions to ensure continued funding.
