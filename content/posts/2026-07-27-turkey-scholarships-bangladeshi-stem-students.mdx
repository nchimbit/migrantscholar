Turkey Scholarships
Bangladeshi students pursuing degrees in STEM fields are eligible for the Turkey Government Scholarships, which provide fully funded awards covering tuition fees, living expenses, and other benefits. The scholarship amount varies depending on the study level and program. Please check the official scholarship website for the latest information on funding amounts.

## Who Qualifies
* Bangladeshi students with a strong academic background in STEM fields
* Students applying for undergraduate, masters, or PhD programs in Turkey
* Students who meet the language proficiency requirements (IELTS or Turkish language)
* Students who are not currently holding a Turkish government scholarship
* Students who are eligible for a student visa in Turkey

## What the Scholarship Covers
The Turkey Government Scholarships provide fully funded awards that cover:
* Tuition fees for the entire duration of the program
* Monthly living stipend (approximately 700-1,100 Turkish Lira)
* Accommodation in a public university dormitory
* Health insurance
* One-year Turkish language course
* Travel expenses (one-way ticket to Turkey)

## How to Apply
1. Check the official website for the application deadline and required documents
2. Prepare all required documents, including transcripts, diplomas, and language proficiency certificates
3. Fill out the online application form on the Turkey Scholarships website
4. Upload all required documents to the online application system
5. Submit the application and wait for the evaluation process
6. Take the Turkish language proficiency exam (if required)
7. Take the IELTS exam (if required)
8. Wait for the final evaluation and scholarship award decision

## Required Documents
* Passport
* National ID card
* Birth certificate
* Diploma or certificate of graduation
* Transcripts
* Language proficiency certificate (IELTS or Turkish language)
* Two recommendation letters
* Personal statement or motivation letter

## Frequently Asked Questions
Q: What is the language of instruction in Turkish universities?
A: The language of instruction in Turkish universities is usually Turkish, but some programs may be taught in English.
Q: Can I apply for the Turkey Government Scholarships if I am not a Bangladeshi citizen?
A: No, the Turkey Government Scholarships are only available for Bangladeshi students.
Q: How long does it take to evaluate my application?
A: The evaluation process can take several months, so it is recommended to apply well in advance.
Q: Can I apply for a master's program if I don't have a bachelor's degree?
A: No, you must have a bachelor's degree to be eligible for a master's program.
Q: How much is the monthly living stipend for PhD students?
A: The monthly living stipend for PhD students is approximately 1,100 Turkish Lira.
Q: Can I work part-time while studying in Turkey?
A: Yes, international students are allowed to work part-time in Turkey, but they must obtain a work permit.
