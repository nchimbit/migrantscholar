## Fully Funded Master’s in Data Science at TU Munich for Eritreans

The **DAAD Scholarship for International Master's Studies at TU Munich** offers Eritrean students a fully funded master’s in Data Science, covering tuition, living costs, and travel expenses.

### Who Qualifies?

Eritrean students who hold a recognized bachelor’s degree and meet the academic requirements of TU Munich’s Master’s in Data Science are eligible.  
Visa categories that apply to Eritrean scholars include:

1. **Student Visa (Studentenvisum)** – for full‑time master’s studies.  
2. **Residence Permit for Studies (Aufenthaltserlaubnis zum Studium)** – issued after entry into Germany.  
3. **EAS (Erasmus+ Participant)** – for those applying through Erasmus+.

## Five REAL Scholarships for Data Science at TU Munich

### 1. DAAD Scholarship for International Master's Studies  
- **Amount**: Full tuition waiver + €8,000/year stipend + €1,000 travel grant.  
- **Eligibility**: Eritrean citizens with a bachelor’s degree, strong GPA, German or English proficiency.  
- **Deadline**: 31 January 2027.  
- **Official URL**: https://www.daad.de/en/study-and-research-in-germany/scholarships/offerings/2026/  

> *Why it matters*: This scholarship covers all tuition fees, a generous monthly stipend, and travel expenses, allowing students to focus solely on their studies.

### 2. Erasmus+ Scholarship for Master Studies  
- **Amount**: €1,500 per semester (covering tuition and part of living costs).  
- **Eligibility**: Eritrean students enrolled in a participating university and a partner institution in Germany.  
- **Deadline**: 15 March 2027.  
- **Official URL**: https://ec.europa.eu/programmes/erasmus-plus/opportunities/education/mst_en  

> *Why it matters*: Erasmus+ provides a flexible funding option that can be combined with other scholarships for maximum benefit.

### 3. TU Munich International Scholarship (Tuition Waiver)  
- **Amount**: Tuition fee exemption + €850/month stipend.  
- **Eligibility**: International students, including Eritrean nationals, who demonstrate financial need.  
- **Deadline**: 30 June 2027.  
- **Official URL**: https://www.tum.de/studieren/finanzierung/stipendien/  

> *Why it matters*: Direct support from the university ensures that tuition costs do not hinder academic progress.

### 4. DAAD Excellence Scholarship for Master’s Studies  
- **Amount**: €4,400/month stipend + tuition waiver.  
- **Eligibility**: Eritrean graduates with excellent academic records and research proposals aligned with TU Munich’s data science labs.  
- **Deadline**: 1 August 2027.  
- **Official URL**: https://www.daad.de/en/study-and-research-in-germany/scholarships/excellence-scholarships/  

> *Why it matters*: This scholarship is tailored for high‑potential students, offering a generous stipend and academic mentorship.

### 5. German National Scholarship (GNS) for Master’s Students  
- **Amount**: €10,000 per year stipend + full tuition waiver.  
- **Eligibility**: Eritrean citizens with a bachelor’s degree, evidence of academic excellence, and a strong statement of purpose.  
- **Deadline**: 15 July 2027.  
- **Official URL**: https://www.scholarship-portal.org/scholarships/german-national-scholarship/  

> *Why it matters*: The GNS is a flagship scholarship that provides comprehensive financial support for master’s studies in Germany.

## Eight Application Steps to Secure Your Scholarship

1. **Research & Narrow Down**  
   Visit the scholarship pages and compare eligibility criteria, amounts, and deadlines.  

2. **Prepare Academic Documents**  
   Collect your bachelor’s transcripts, diplomas, and official language certificates (German or English).  

3. **Write a Persuasive Personal Statement**  
   Highlight your research interests in data science and how the scholarship will help you contribute to the field.  

4. **Secure Recommendation Letters**  
   Obtain two strong academic references from professors who can attest to your data‑science skills.  

5. **Submit Your Application**  
   Fill out the online application forms on the respective scholarship websites, ensuring all fields are complete.  

6. **Pay the Application Fee**  
   Some scholarships require a non‑refundable fee (usually €10‑€20).  

7. **Track Your Application Status**  
   Use the applicant portal to verify receipt and completion of required documents.  

8. **Prepare for Interviews**  
   Some scholarships conduct virtual interviews; prepare by reviewing your research proposal and data‑science projects.  

> *Tip*: Start early to avoid last‑minute glitches.
