# PhD USA
The Fulbright Foreign Student Program exists and qualifies Iranian nationals for fully funded PhD programs in the USA with a funding amount of up to $100,000 per year. Iranian students can pursue their PhD in various fields, including engineering, humanities, and social sciences. The program aims to promote cultural exchange and academic excellence between the USA and Iran. To be eligible, applicants must have a strong academic record, proficiency in English, and a clear research proposal.

## Who Qualifies
Iranian nationals who hold a bachelor's or master's degree from an accredited institution are eligible to apply for fully funded PhD programs in the USA. They must also meet the visa requirements, including F-1 or J-1 visa categories. Additionally, applicants must demonstrate financial need and have a strong potential for academic success. Iranian students who are already in the USA on a different visa category, such as F-2 or J-2, may also be eligible to apply for a PhD program.
