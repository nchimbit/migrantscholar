The Albert Einstein German Academic Refugee Initiative (DAFI) scholarship exists for Syrian refugees, providing up to €750 per month in funding. 
This programme is part of the [DAAD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/universities/daad) offered to support refugees in pursuing higher education in Germany.

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for these STEM scholarships, Syrian refugees must hold a valid visa, such as a residence permit or a visa for humanitarian reasons. The following visa categories are typically accepted: 
- Subsidiary protection
- Refugee status
- Temporary protection

## Available Scholarships
The following are fully funded STEM scholarships available to Syrian refugees in Germany:
### 1. DAFI Scholarship
- Amount: up to €750 per month
- Eligibility: Syrian refugees with a higher education entrance qualification
- Deadline: varies
- Official URL: https://www.daad.de/en/study-and-research-in-germany/scholarships/dafi/
### 2. Deutschlandstipendium
- Amount: €300 per month
- Eligibility: Syrian refugees enrolled in a German university
- Deadline: varies
- Official URL: https://www.deutschlandstipendium.de/en/
### 3. Friedrich Ebert Foundation Scholarship
- Amount: up to €750 per month
- Eligibility: Syrian refugees with a strong academic record and social commitment
- Deadline: varies
- Official URL: https://www.fes.de/en/scholarship
### 4. Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung Scholarship
- Amount: up to €1,200 per month
- Eligibility: Syrian refugees with a strong academic record and social commitment
- Deadline: varies
- Official URL: https://www.kas.de/en/scholarship
### 5. Heinrich Böll Foundation Scholarship
- Amount: up to €750 per month
- Eligibility: Syrian refugees with a strong academic record and social commitment
- Deadline: varies
- Official URL: https://www.boell.de/en/scholarship

## Frequently Asked Questions
1. What is the duration of the DAFI scholarship?
The DAFI scholarship is typically awarded for one year, with the possibility of extension.
2. Can I apply for multiple scholarships at the same time?
Yes, Syrian refugees can apply for multiple scholarships, but must disclose this information in their applications.
3. Do I need to speak German to apply for these scholarships?
While German language skills are not always required, proficiency in German or English is often necessary for academic success.
4. Can I apply for these scholarships if I am not a Syrian refugee?
No, these scholarships are specifically designed for Syrian refugees, but [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-eligibility/refugees) from other countries may be available.
5. How do I apply for a residence permit in Germany?
Syrian refugees can apply for a residence permit through the German embassy or consulate in their home country, or through the local immigration office in Germany.
6. What is the difference between a [fully funded scholarship](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded) and a partial scholarship?
A fully funded scholarship covers all living and tuition expenses, while a partial scholarship only covers a portion of these costs.
7. Can I apply for a [PhD scholarship](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-level/phd) if I do not have a master's degree?
In some cases, yes, but a master's degree is often required for PhD programmes.
8. How do I find [Canada scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/countries/Canada) for Syrian refugees?
Syrian refugees can search for Canada scholarships on the [MigrantScholar](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/blog) website or through the official government website.
