# Aussie Women
The Australian Government's **Research Training Program International Scholarship** exists and qualifies migrant women in Australia who hold a valid student visa subclass 500, with funding of up to $124,000 over three years, to pursue higher education in healthcare fields. Many migrant women in Australia who are holders of visa categories such as subclass 457, 482, or 485 are also eligible to apply for various women-only healthcare scholarships. 

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for these scholarships, migrant women must hold a valid visa, such as a student visa subclass 500, and meet specific eligibility requirements. The main visa categories that qualify for these scholarships include:
- Subclass 457 - Temporary Business (Long Stay) visa
- Subclass 482 - Temporary Skilled Migration (TSM) visa
- Subclass 485 - Temporary Graduate visa
- Subclass 500 - Student visa

## Scholarships
### Research Training Program International Scholarship
The Australian Government's Research Training Program International Scholarship provides up to $124,000 over three years to migrant women pursuing higher education in healthcare fields. Eligibility includes holding a valid student visa subclass 500, meeting specific GPA requirements, and being accepted into a research program. Deadline varies, [https://www.education.gov.au/research-training-program](https://www.education.gov.au/research-training-program).

### Australian Awards Scholarship
The Australian Awards Scholarship provides up to $30,000 per year to migrant women from developing countries to pursue higher education in healthcare fields. Eligibility includes holding a valid student visa subclass 500, meeting specific GPA requirements, and being from a participating country. Deadline is April 30, [https://www.australiaawards.gov.au/](https://www.australiaawards.gov.au/).

### NSW Health Nursing and Midwifery Scholarship
The NSW Health Nursing and Midwifery Scholarship provides up to $30,000 to migrant women pursuing a career in nursing or midwifery in New South Wales. Eligibility includes holding a valid student visa subclass 500, meeting specific GPA requirements, and being accepted into a nursing or midwifery program. Deadline is August 31, [https://www.health.nsw.gov.au/nursing/scholarships/Pages/nursing-scholarships.aspx](https://www.health.nsw.gov.au/nursing/scholarships/Pages/nursing-scholarships.aspx).

### Jean Rogerson Scholarship
The Jean Rogerson Scholarship provides up to $5,000 to migrant women pursuing a career in healthcare in Western Australia. Eligibility includes holding a valid student visa subclass 500, meeting specific GPA requirements, and being accepted into a healthcare program. Deadline is October 31, [https://www.rdhonours.com.au/jean-rogerson-scholarship/](https://www.rdhonours.com.au/jean-rogerson-scholarship/).

### Australian College of Nursing Scholarship
The Australian College of Nursing Scholarship provides up to $10,000 to migrant women pursuing a career in nursing in Australia. Eligibility includes holding a valid student visa subclass 500, meeting specific GPA requirements, and being accepted into a nursing program. Deadline is February 28, [https://www.acn.edu.au/scholarships](https://www.acn.edu.au/scholarships).
