The Fulbright Program offers fully funded STEM scholarships for Afghan women in the USA, providing up to $100,000 in funding for undergraduate, master's, and PhD studies. Afghan women who are migrants, refugees, or asylum seekers with a strong academic background in STEM fields are eligible to apply. The scholarship covers full tuition, living expenses, and travel costs for the duration of the study program.

## Who Qualifies
* Afghan women who are migrants, refugees, or asylum seekers
* Hold a strong academic background in STEM fields (science, technology, engineering, and mathematics)
* Meet the eligibility criteria for the Fulbright Program, including language proficiency and academic achievement
* Hold a valid passport or be able to obtain one
* Be admissible to the United States under the terms of the Fulbright Program

## What the Scholarship Covers
The Fulbright Program provides fully funded STEM scholarships for Afghan women, covering:
* Full tuition fees for the duration of the study program
* Living expenses, including accommodation, food, and transportation
* Travel costs to and from the United States
* Health insurance
* Book and equipment allowance
* Research and conference funding (for graduate students)

## How to Apply
1. Review the eligibility criteria and application requirements for the Fulbright Program.
2. Prepare all required documents, including transcripts, diplomas, and language proficiency test results.
3. Submit an online application through the Fulbright website.
4. Take the required language proficiency tests (TOEFL or IELTS).
5. Prepare for and take the GRE or GMAT test (for graduate students).
6. Submit a personal statement and study plan.
7. Obtain two letters of recommendation from academic or professional referees.
8. Submit the application package before the deadline.

## Required Documents
* Passport
* Transcripts and diplomas
* Language proficiency test results (TOEFL or IELTS)
* GRE or GMAT test results (for graduate students)
* Personal statement and study plan
* Two letters of recommendation
* Proof of English language proficiency
