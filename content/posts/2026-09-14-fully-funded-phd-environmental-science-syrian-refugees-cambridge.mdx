The **Cambridge Trust Scholarship for Refugees** offers a fully funded PhD in Environmental Science to Syrian refugees, providing up to £30,000 per year for tuition and living expenses.  

## Who Qualifies

| Visa Category | Eligibility Criteria | Key Points |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 4 (Student) Visa** | Must be accepted to a PhD program at a UK university and have a Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS). | Full scholarship covers visa costs. |
| **Tier 2 (General) Visa** | Suitable if you have a job offer related to your PhD research or a sponsorship from a university. | Scholarships may include a salary stipend. |
| **EU Settlement Scheme (EU/EEA nationals)** | If you were living in the UK before 31 December 2020. | Not applicable to Syrians, but important for other refugees. |
| **Refugee Status or Humanitarian Protection** | Must hold a valid refugee status or humanitarian protection in the UK. | Full tuition and living stipend are included. |
| **International Student Status (non-UK)** | You must be a citizen of a non-UK country and be accepted into a UK PhD program. | Scholarships often cover the entire duration. |

### Key Eligibility Checks

* Proof of Syrian refugee status (e.g., UNHCR certificate, UK Home Office documentation).  
* Acceptance letter from the Department of Environmental Science at Cambridge.  
* Demonstrated proficiency in English (IELTS ≥ 7.5 or equivalent).  
* Academic transcripts and a research proposal that aligns with Cambridge’s environmental research priorities.

## Five Real Scholarships for Syrian Refugees

### 1. Cambridge Trust Scholarship for Refugees  
- **Amount**: Up to £30,000 per year (tuition + living stipend).  
- **Eligibility**: Syrian refugees with a confirmed PhD offer at the University of Cambridge.  
- **Deadline**: 31 May each year.  
- **Official URL**: https://www.cambridgetrust.org/scholarships/  

### 2. Cambridge Global Scholarship  
- **Amount**: £25,000 per year (covers tuition and a generous living stipend).  
- **Eligibility**: International students from developing or transitional economies, including refugees.  
- **Deadline**: 15 August annually.  
- **Official URL**: https://www.cambridge.org/uk/global-scholarships  

### 3. UNHCR Scholarship Programme  
- **Amount**: Full tuition plus a £18,000 per year living stipend for PhD studies in the UK.  
- **Eligibility**: Syrian refugees holding UNHCR-issued certificates.  
- **Deadline**: 30 June for 2026 intake.  
- **Official URL**: https://www.unhcr.org/scholarship-programme/  

### 4. British Council International Awards  
- **Amount**: Full tuition, living stipend, and travel allowance for PhD in Environmental Science.  
- **Eligibility**: Syrian refugees with an admission offer from a UK university.  
- **Deadline**: 1 July each year.  
- **Official URL**: https://www.britishcouncil.org/scholarships  

### 5. Commonwealth Scholarship & Fellowship Plan (CSFP)  
- **Amount**: Full funding for PhD studies (tuition, stipend, travel).  
- **Eligibility**: Citizens of Commonwealth countries who have refugee status in the UK.  
- **Deadline**: 15 October for 2027 intake.  
- **Official URL**: https://cscuk.dfid.gov.uk/scholarships

## Eight Steps to Apply

1. **Secure an Offer** – Apply to the PhD programme in Environmental Science at Cambridge and secure an official acceptance letter.  
2. **Gather Refugee Documentation** – Obtain your UNHCR certificate, UK visa status letter, and proof of residence.  
3. **Prepare Academic Records** – Transcripts, CV, research proposal, and two academic references.  
4. **Compose an Impact Statement** – Explain how your research will benefit Syrian communities and the global environment.  
5. **Complete Scholarship Applications** – Use the official forms for each scholarship; pay attention to specific questions on displacement and resilience.
