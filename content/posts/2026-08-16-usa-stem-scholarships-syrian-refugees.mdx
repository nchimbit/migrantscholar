# STEM Aid
The Fulbright Scholarship for Syrian Students in the United States, which qualifies Syrian refugees, provides full funding up to $100,000 for graduate study in the USA.

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for these scholarships, you must hold one of the following visa categories: F-1, J-1, or asylum status. Syrian refugees in the USA can apply for these STEM scholarships to pursue higher education.

The following are some fully funded STEM scholarships available to Syrian refugees in the USA:
1. **The Paul and Daisy Soros Fellowships for New Americans**, $100,000, Eligible for New Americans, Deadline: November 1, [https://www.pdsoros.org/](https://www.pdsoros.org/)
2. **The National Science Foundation Graduate Research Fellowship**, $138,000, Eligible for STEM graduate students, Deadline: October 20, [https://www.nsfgrfp.org/](https://www.nsfgrfp.org/)
3. **The Microsoft Scholarship**, $20,000, Eligible for undergraduate students, Deadline: February 26, [https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/diversity/college/scholarships.aspx](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/diversity/college/scholarships.aspx)
4. **The Google Scholarship**, $10,000, Eligible for undergraduate and graduate students, Deadline: December 5, [https://buildyourfuture.withgoogle.com/](https://buildyourfuture.withgoogle.com/)
5. **The IBM Scholarship**, $10,000, Eligible for undergraduate and graduate students, Deadline: February 28, [https://www.ibm.com/employment/](https://www.ibm.com/employment/)
