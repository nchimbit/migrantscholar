## Who Qualifies
* Skilled Independent Visa: Pakistani healthcare professionals with a bachelor's or master's degree in a relevant field, and at least two years of work experience
* H-1B Visa: Pakistani healthcare professionals with a bachelor's or master's degree in a relevant field, and at least one year of work experience
* Other visa categories: please check the official visa websites for the latest information on eligibility criteria

## What the Scholarship Covers
The Skilled Independent Visa and H-1B Visa cover the cost of living and working in Australia and the USA, respectively. However, please note that these are not scholarships, but rather visa categories that allow Pakistani healthcare professionals to work in these countries. For scholarships, please check the official websites of universities and government organizations.
