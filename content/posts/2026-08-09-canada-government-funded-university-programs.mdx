## Who Qualifies
Refugee students who hold a valid Canadian permanent resident visa or a protected person status under Canada's Immigration and Refugee Protection Act may be eligible for these programs. This includes individuals who have been granted refugee status under the following visa categories:
- Convention Refugees
- Country of Asylum Class
- Protected Persons

To be eligible, refugee students must also meet specific requirements such as language proficiency, academic standing, and financial need.
