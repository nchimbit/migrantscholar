The DAAD Helmut-Schmidt-Programme is a fully funded scholarship for Afghan women to pursue Master's studies in STEM fields in Germany, with a funding amount of €934 per month. The scholarship is targeted at female students from Afghanistan who are interested in studying in Germany. Please check the official scholarship website for the latest information on the funding amount and eligibility criteria.

## Who Qualifies
* Afghan women with a Bachelor's degree in a STEM field
* Applicants who have been granted refugee status or asylum in Germany
* Applicants with a strong academic record and a proven interest in studying in Germany
* Applicants who can demonstrate a strong motivation for studying in Germany and a clear plan for their future career
* Please note that the eligibility criteria may change, and it is the applicant's responsibility to check the official scholarship website for the latest information.

## What the Scholarship Covers
The DAAD Helmut-Schmidt-Programme provides a fully funded scholarship that covers:
* Monthly stipend of €934
* Travel allowance for arrival and departure
* Health, accident, and liability insurance
* Study and research allowance
* Language course before the start of the study program
* Please note that the funding amount and coverage may change, and it is the applicant's responsibility to check the official scholarship website for the latest information.

## How to Apply
1. Check the eligibility criteria on the official scholarship website
2. Choose a study program from the list of eligible programs
3. Contact the university to inquire about the admission requirements and process
4. Apply for admission to the university
5. Apply for the DAAD Helmut-Schmidt-Programme scholarship
6. Submit the required documents, including a CV, motivation letter, and transcripts
7. Take the IELTS exam and submit the results
8. Wait for the selection results, which will be announced on the official scholarship website
