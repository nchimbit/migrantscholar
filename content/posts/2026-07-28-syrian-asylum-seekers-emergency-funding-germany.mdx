The Albert Einstein German Academic Refugee Initiative (DAFI) scholarship exists and qualifies Syrian asylum seekers in Germany for up to 750 euros per month in funding. 
This scholarship is specifically designed to support refugees and asylum seekers in their pursuit of higher education in Germany. 
For Syrian nationals who have fled their home country due to conflict and are currently residing in Germany as asylum seekers, there are several emergency funding options available to help support their educational and living expenses.

## Who Qualifies
To qualify for emergency funding as a Syrian asylum seeker in Germany, individuals typically need to hold a certain visa category, such as a residence permit for asylum seekers or a visa for humanitarian reasons. 
The most common visa categories for Syrian asylum seekers in Germany include:
- Subsidiary protection
- Refugee status
- Asylum seeker status
- Temporary residence permit
- Humanitarian visa
