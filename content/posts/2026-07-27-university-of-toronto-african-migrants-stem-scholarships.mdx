The University of Toronto offers fully funded scholarships to African migrants pursuing studies in STEM fields, with a funding amount of up to $50,000 per year. Eligible applicants include African migrants with a valid Canadian study permit or permanent residence, and the scholarship is open to students pursuing undergraduate or graduate degrees. Please check the official scholarship website for the latest information on eligibility and funding.

## Who Qualifies
* African migrants with a valid Canadian study permit or permanent residence
* Migrants who have been granted refugee status in Canada
* Asylum seekers with a valid work permit or study permit
* International students from African countries who are pursuing studies in STEM fields
* Applicants must meet the English language proficiency requirements, which include a minimum IELTS score of 6.5 or equivalent

## What the Scholarship Covers
The University of Toronto scholarship covers tuition fees, living expenses, and other related costs, including:
* Tuition fees: up to $50,000 per year
* Living expenses: up to $10,000 per year
* Health insurance: up to $1,000 per year
* Books and supplies: up to $1,000 per year
* Travel expenses: up to $2,000 per year

## How to Apply
1. Check the official scholarship website for eligibility and application requirements
2. Prepare the required documents, including transcripts, letters of recommendation, and language proficiency test scores
3. Submit an online application through the University of Toronto's admissions portal
4. Pay the application fee, which is currently $125
5. Upload supporting documents, including transcripts, letters of recommendation, and language proficiency test scores
6. Submit a personal statement outlining academic and career goals
7. Apply for a study permit or permanent residence, if required
8. Receive and review the admission decision and scholarship offer
