## Who Qualifies
* Skilled Independent Visa (Subclass 189): Bangladeshi STEM professionals in the USA with a valid skills assessment and sufficient points
* Skilled Nominated Visa (Subclass 190): Bangladeshi STEM professionals in the USA with a valid skills assessment, sufficient points, and a nomination from an Australian state or territory
* Temporary Skill Shortage Visa (Subclass 482): Bangladeshi STEM professionals in the USA with a valid skills assessment and a sponsor from an Australian employer
* Other visa categories may also be available, please check the official visa website for the latest information

## How to Apply
1. Check the eligibility criteria for the desired visa category
2. Gather all required documents, including a valid passport, birth certificate, and qualifications
3. Complete a skills assessment with the relevant assessing authority
4. Submit an expression of interest (EOI) through the Australian Government's SkillSelect system
5. Receive an invitation to apply for the visa
6. Submit the visa application, including all required documents and payment of the application fee
7. Wait for the visa application to be processed
8. Receive the visa decision and any further instructions
