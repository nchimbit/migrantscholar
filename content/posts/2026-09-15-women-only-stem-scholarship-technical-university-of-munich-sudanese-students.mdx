The Technical University of Munich (TUM) offers the Women in STEM Scholarship for Sudanese female students, awarding 10 000 € per year to cover tuition, living costs, and study materials.  

## Who qualifies
- **Nationality** – Female students holding Sudanese passports.  
- **Field of study** – STEM disciplines (Engineering, Natural Sciences, Informatics, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, or related fields).  
- **Academic level** – Undergraduate (Bachelor’s), Master’s, or
