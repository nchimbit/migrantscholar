# UT Scholarships
The University of Toronto's [Lester B. Pearson International Scholarship Program](https://www.utoronto.ca/academics/programs-and-courses/scholarships/lester-b-pearson-international-scholarship-program) exists for international students, including Somali refugees in Canada, with funding of $50,000 per year. 
As a Somali refugee, you may qualify for various scholarships at the University of Toronto. 
To be eligible, you must be a refugee with a valid Canadian visa, such as a [Protected Person] (https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/refugees/canada exemple-protected-persons.html) or [Convention Refugee] (https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/refugees/canada-convention-refugee.html) visa.

## Who Qualifies
Somali refugees in Canada can apply for various scholarships at the University of Toronto. 
To qualify, you must have one of the following visa categories: 
- Protected Person 
- Convention Refugee 
- [Refugee Claimant] (https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/refugees/claim-protection.html)

## Scholarships
The following scholarships are available for Somali refugees in Canada:
### Lester B. Pearson International Scholarship Program
$50,000 per year, for international students, including Somali refugees, deadline: January 17th, [official URL](https://www.utoronto.ca/academics/programs-and-courses/scholarships/lester-b-pearson-international-scholarship-program)
### President's Scholars of Excellence Program
$10,000 per year, for international students, including Somali refugees, deadline: January 17th, [official URL](https://www.utoronto.ca/academics/programs-and-courses/scholarships/presidents-scholars-excellence-program)
### University of Toronto Scholars Program
$7,500 per year, for international students, including Somali refugees, deadline: January 17th, [official URL](https://www.utoronto.ca/academics/programs-and-courses/scholarships/university-toronto-scholars-program)
### Canada Graduate Scholarships - Master's Program
$17,500 per year, for graduate students, including Somali refugees, deadline: December 1st, [official URL](https://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/Students-Etudiants/PG-CS/CGSM-BESCM_eng.asp)
### Ontario Graduate Scholarship
$15,000 per session, for graduate students, including Somali refugees, deadline: February 1st, [official URL](https://osap.gov.on.ca/ENG/special.htm)
