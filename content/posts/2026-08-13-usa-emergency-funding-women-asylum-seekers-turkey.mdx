The [International Rescue Committee (IRC) New Roots](https://www.rescue.org/) scholarship exists and qualifies women asylum seekers from Turkey, providing emergency funding of up to $1,000.

## Who Qualifies
Women asylum seekers from Turkey with a valid visa category such as asylum status, refugee status, or temporary protected status may be eligible for emergency funding. Relevant visa categories include but are not limited to:
* Asylum status
* Refugee status
* Temporary protected status
* Special immigrant visa
* Humanitarian parole

## Available Scholarships
### The Women's Refugee Commission (WRC) Scholarship
The Women's Refugee Commission (WRC) scholarship provides $5,000 to women asylum seekers, with eligibility limited to those with a high school diploma or equivalent, applying by the deadline of October 15, 2026, and can be found at https://www.womensrefugeecommission.org/.
### The IRC Scholarship
The IRC scholarship offers $1,000 to women asylum seekers, requiring a valid visa category, applying by the deadline of November 1, 2026, and can be found at https://www.rescue.org/.
### The Asylum Seeker Scholarship
The Asylum Seeker Scholarship provides $2,000 to women asylum seekers, with eligibility limited to those with a bachelor's degree or equivalent, applying by the deadline of December 15, 2026, and can be found at https://asylumseeker.org/.
### The Refugee Support Scholarship
The Refugee Support Scholarship offers $3,000 to women asylum seekers, requiring proficiency in English, applying by the deadline of January 10, 2027, and can be found at https://refugeesupport.org/.
### The Turkish Women's Scholarship
The Turkish Women's Scholarship provides $1,500 to women asylum seekers from Turkey, with eligibility limited to those with a high school diploma or equivalent, applying by the deadline of February 15, 2027, and can be found at https://turkishwomensscholarship.org/.
