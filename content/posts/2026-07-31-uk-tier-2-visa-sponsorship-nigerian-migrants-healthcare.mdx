The Chevening Scholarship exists for Nigerian migrants in the healthcare sector, providing funding of up to £18,000 for a master's degree. 
To be eligible, applicants must have a bachelor's degree, two years of work experience, and meet the [Tier 2 visa sponsorship](https://www.gov.uk/tier-2-general) requirements. 

## Who Qualifies
Applicants with a [Tier 2 visa](https://www.gov.uk/tier-2-general) or a [Tier 5 visa](https://www.gov.uk/tier-5) may be eligible for certain scholarships, while others may require a [Tier 4 visa](https://www.gov.uk/tier-4-general-visa) for study purposes. 
Nigerian migrants can explore various visa categories, such as the [Tier 2 (General) visa](https://www.gov.uk/tier-2-general) for skilled workers or the [Tier 2 (Intra-Company Transfer) visa](https://www.gov.uk/tier-2-intra-company-transfer) for employees transferring within a company.

The Chevening Scholarship provides £18,000 for a master's degree at a UK university, and is open to Nigerian migrants with two years of work experience and a bachelor's degree, application deadline is November 1, [apply now](https://www.chevening.org/).

The Commonwealth Scholarship offers full funding for a master's or PhD degree at a UK university, and is eligible for Nigerian migrants with a bachelor's degree, application deadline is December 1, [apply now](https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/).

The Felix Scholarship offers £15,000 for a master's degree at a UK university, and is eligible for Nigerian migrants with a bachelor's degree and two years of work experience, application deadline is January 20, [apply now](https://www.felixscholarship.org/).
