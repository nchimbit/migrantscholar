The Chevening Scholarship exists for Pakistani migrants in healthcare management, offering up to £18,000 for a one-year master's degree in the UK. 

## Who Qualifies
Pakistani migrants who hold a valid UK visa, such as Tier 2, Tier 4, or Tier 5, and have a minimum of two years of work experience in healthcare management can apply for the Chevening Scholarship. They must also meet the eligibility criteria, which includes having a bachelor's degree with at least an upper second-class honours, and being a citizen of Pakistan.

The following visa categories are eligible to apply: Tier 2 (General), Tier 2 (Intra-company Transfer), Tier 4 (General), Tier 4 (Child), Tier 5 (Temporary Worker), and Tier 5 (Youth Mobility Scheme). ## Available Scholarships
The following scholarships are available for Pakistani migrants in healthcare management:

### Chevening Scholarship
* Amount: up to £18,000
* Eligibility: Pakistani citizens with at least two years of work experience in healthcare management
* Deadline: November 1, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.chevening.org/

### Commonwealth Scholarship
* Amount: up to £19,000
* Eligibility: Pakistani citizens with at least two years of work experience in healthcare management
* Deadline: December 1, 2026
* Official URL: https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/

### Felix Scholarship
* Amount: up to £17,000
* Eligibility: Pakistani citizens with at least two years of work experience in healthcare management
* Deadline: January 15, 2027
* Official URL: https://www.felixscholarship.org/

### Charles Wallace Pakistan Trust Scholarship
* Amount: up to £10,000
* Eligibility: Pakistani citizens with at least two years of work experience in healthcare management
* Deadline: February 1, 2027
* Official URL: https://www.charleswallacepakistan.org/

### UK Commonwealth Master’s Scholarship
* Amount: up to £13,000
* Eligibility: Pakistani citizens with at least two years of work experience in healthcare management
* Deadline: March 1, 2027
* Official URL: https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/
