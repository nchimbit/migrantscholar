# German Aid
The **Albert Einstein German Academic Refugee Initiative (DAFI)** scholarship exists and qualifies asylum seekers, including Iraqi asylum seekers in Germany, for funding of up to €750 per month. 

## Who Qualifies
Iraqi asylum seekers with a valid visa, including but not limited to, visa categories such as humanitarian visa, asylum seeker visa, or subsidiary protection, may be eligible for emergency funding in Germany. The visa categories that qualify for emergency funding include:
- Humanitarian visa
- Asylum seeker visa
- Subsidiary protection
- Temporary protection
- Residence permit for humanitarian reasons
