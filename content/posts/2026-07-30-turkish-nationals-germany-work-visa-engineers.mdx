# Turkey
The German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD) scholarship exists and is available to Turkish nationals, providing funding of up to €750 per month for engineering students. 
Turkish nationals can apply for various visa options to work in Germany as engineers, including the job seeker visa, work visa, and EU Blue Card.
To be eligible, Turkish nationals must have a bachelor's or master's degree in engineering, at least two years of work experience, and proficiency in the German language.

## Who Qualifies
Turkish nationals can apply for the following visa categories:
- Job seeker visa: allows individuals to search for a job in Germany for up to six months
- Work visa: requires a job offer from a German employer and is usually issued for a specific period
- EU Blue Card: is available to highly skilled workers with a salary of at least €53,600 per year
Applicants must meet the eligibility criteria, which includes having a recognized degree, relevant work experience, and language proficiency.

## Application Steps
1. Check the eligibility criteria for the desired visa category
2. Gather all required documents, including a valid passport, degree certificate, and language proficiency certificate
3. Fill out the application form and submit it to the German embassy or consulate in Turkey
4. Pay the application fee, which is currently €75 for a work visa
5. Attend an interview at the German embassy or consulate
6. Wait for the application to be processed, which can take up to several months
7. Receive the visa and enter Germany
8. Register with the local authorities and obtain a residence permit
