## Who Qualifies
Syrian refugees who hold a residence permit in Germany, specifically with visa categories such as asylum, subsidiary protection, or temporary protection, may be eligible for STEM scholarships. 
These scholarships are designed to support students who have been displaced due to conflict and are seeking to continue their education in Germany. 
In addition to visa categories, applicants must also meet specific academic requirements, including a strong academic record and proficiency in the German language.
