# STEM Scholarships
The Friedrich Ebert Foundation Scholarship exists for Syrian refugees in Germany, providing up to €744 per month for master's and PhD students. Syrian refugees who have been granted a residence permit in the category of § 23 or § 25 (2) of the German Residence Act, or have been recognized as refugees according to the Geneva Convention, may be eligible for this fully funded STEM scholarship in Germany.

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for fully funded STEM scholarships for Syrian refugees in Germany, applicants typically need to hold a visa in the following categories: 
- § 23: for those with a temporary residence permit
- § 25 (2): for those with a residence permit for humanitarian reasons
- Geneva Convention: for those recognized as refugees
These categories may vary depending on the specific scholarship program, so it is essential to review the eligibility criteria carefully for each opportunity.

## Fully Funded Scholarships
### Friedrich Ebert Foundation Scholarship
The Friedrich Ebert Foundation Scholarship provides up to €744 per month for master's and PhD students, with eligibility for Syrian refugees with a residence permit in Germany. Deadline: varies, typically October or April, [www.fes.de](http://www.fes.de).
### DAAD Scholarship for Refugees
The DAAD Scholarship for Refugees offers €750 per month for master's and PhD students, with eligibility for Syrian refugees with a university degree. Deadline: varies, typically October or April, [www.daad.de](http://www.daad.de).
### KAAD Scholarship
The KAAD Scholarship provides up to €1,200 per month for master's and PhD students, with eligibility for Syrian refugees with a strong academic record. Deadline: March 30th or September 30th, [www.kaad.de](http://www.kaad.de).
### Heinrich Böll Foundation Scholarship
The Heinrich Böll Foundation Scholarship offers up to €1,200 per month for master's and PhD students, with eligibility for Syrian refugees with a focus on sustainability and social justice. Deadline: March 1st or September 1st, [www.boell.de](http://www.boell.de).
### Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung Scholarship
The Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung Scholarship provides up to €1,200 per month for master's and PhD students, with eligibility for Syrian refugees with a strong academic record and a focus on democratic values. Deadline: January 15th or July 15th, [www.kas.de](http://www.kas.de).

## Application Steps
1. Review the eligibility criteria for each scholarship program to ensure you meet the requirements.
2. Prepare all necessary documents, including transcripts, diplomas, and language proficiency certificates.
3. Submit your application online or by mail, depending on the requirements of the scholarship.
4. Ensure you meet the language proficiency requirements, typically German or English.
5. Take any required language proficiency tests, such as DSH or IELTS.
6. Prepare a strong motivation letter, highlighting your academic achievements and career goals.
7. Secure two academic references from professors or supervisors.
8. Track your application status and follow up with the scholarship provider if necessary.
