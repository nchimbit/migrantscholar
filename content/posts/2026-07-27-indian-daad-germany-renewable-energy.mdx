DAAD Scholarships
Indian migrants in Germany can pursue careers in renewable energy through the DAAD Scholarships, which offer a funding amount of up to €750 per month for master's students and up to €1,200 per month for doctoral students. The scholarships are fully funded and available for Indian nationals who meet the eligibility criteria.

| DAAD Scholarships | up to €1,200 per month | Please check the official scholarship website for the latest information | Indian nationals | Master's, PhD | Yes | [www.daad.de](http://www.daad.de) |

## Who Qualifies
* Indian nationals with a bachelor's or master's degree in a relevant field such as engineering, natural sciences, or economics
* Students who have been accepted into a master's or PhD program in a German university
* Asylum seekers and refugees who have been recognized as such by the German authorities and have a residence permit
* Migrants who have a valid residence permit in Germany and meet the eligibility criteria
* Students who have a strong academic record and can demonstrate a commitment to renewable energy

## What the Scholarship Covers
The DAAD Scholarships cover the following expenses:
* Monthly stipend of up to €750 for master's students and up to €1,200 for doctoral students
* Travel allowance for students who are not already in Germany
* Health insurance
* Accommodation allowance
* Research allowance for doctoral students
* Language course allowance for students who need to improve their German language skills

## How to Apply
1. Check the eligibility criteria and ensure you meet the requirements
2. Choose a master's or PhD program in a German university that is recognized by the DAAD
3. Apply for admission to the university and receive a letter of acceptance
4. Fill out the online application form on the DAAD website
5. Upload the required documents to the online application portal
6. Submit the application form and wait for the review process to be completed
7. If selected, receive a letter of award and instructions on how to receive the scholarship
8. Start your studies in Germany and receive the monthly stipend and other benefits

## Required Documents
* Letter of motivation
* CV
* Certificate of academic degree
* Transcript of records
* Proof of language proficiency (IELTS or TOEFL)
* Letter of acceptance from the German university
* Proof of residence permit (for migrants and asylum seekers)
