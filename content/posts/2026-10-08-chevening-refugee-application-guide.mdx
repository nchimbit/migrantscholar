## Eligibility and Important Requirements

Chevening Scholarships are awarded to outstanding leaders from around the world who have the potential to become future leaders in their home countries. While the core eligibility criteria apply to all applicants, refugees and asylum seekers must also satisfy the following:

1. **Refugee status** – You must be a recognized refugee or have been granted refugee status in your country of residence.  
2. **Academic background** – A good undergraduate degree (or equivalent) that qualifies you for a Master’s programme in the UK.  
3. **English proficiency** – Most courses require an IELTS or equivalent score; some may accept other tests.  
4. **Leadership potential** – Chevening looks for individuals who can influence change in their communities.  
5. **Return commitment** – Applicants must intend to return to their home country (or a country where they have refugee status) after graduation to contribute to development.

Because the exact wording of the eligibility criteria can be updated, check the official Chevening website for the most recent information.

## What Funding or Support May Be Available

Chevening provides **full funding** for:

- **Tuition fees** at the chosen UK university.  
- **Living allowance** (monthly stipend) to cover accommodation, food, and other living expenses.  
- **Travel costs** (return airfare to the UK and home country).  
- **Insurance** and other incidental costs.

The scholarship also offers networking opportunities, professional development workshops, and access to a global alumni network. Exact figures for living allowance and other allowances differ by university and region; consult the official website for the latest figures.

## How to Prepare an Application

Below is a practical, step‑by‑step framework that separates general preparation from the official application process.

### 1. Start Early – Map the Timeline  
- **Research courses**: Identify UK universities offering the Master’s programme you want.  
- **Check deadlines**: Chevening deadlines are usually in late March or early April, but they can vary by country.  
- **Plan your schedule**: Allow at least 6–8 months from the start of your research to the application submission.

### 2. Verify Eligibility  
- **Read the official criteria**: Visit https://www.chevening.org/ and review the “Eligibility” section.  
- **Confirm refugee status**: Have documentation (e.g., refugee certificate, UNHCR letter) ready.  
- **Check academic requirements**: Ensure your undergraduate degree meets the UK university’s entry standards.

### 3. Choose Your Course and University  
- **Align with Chevening’s focus areas**: Chevening often prioritises subjects that promote development, such as public policy, international relations, and STEM fields.  
- **Check university partnership**: Some universities have a partnership with Chevening, which can streamline the application.

### 4. Gather Supporting Documents  
- **Academic transcripts and certificates**  
- **Proof of refugee status**  
- **English language test results**  
- **Curriculum Vitae (CV)**  
- **Personal statement** (see next section)  
- **Reference letters** (two, ideally one academic and one professional)

### 5. Write a Strong Personal Statement  
- **Tell your story**: Highlight how your refugee experience has shaped your leadership potential.  
- **Connect to Chevening values**: Leadership, networking, and commitment to development.  
- **Show impact**: Explain how you plan to use the scholarship to benefit your community or country.

### 6. Secure References  
- **Choose referees who know you well**: Academic supervisors, employers, or community leaders.  
- **Provide them with guidance**: Share the Chevening reference form and explain what the scholarship looks for.  
- **Confirm submission**: Ensure referees submit their letters before the deadline.

### 7. Complete the Online Application  
- **Create a Chevening account**: Use the official portal.  
- **Fill in personal details**: Be accurate and consistent with your documents.  
- **Upload documents**: Follow the file format and size guidelines.  
- **Submit before the deadline**: Late applications are rarely considered.

### 8. Prepare for the Interview (if invited)  
- **Practice common questions**: Leadership, career goals, and how you’ll use the scholarship.  
- **Stay authentic**: Chevening values genuine stories and clear intentions.  
- **Dress appropriately**: Professional attire is recommended.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Why It Matters | How to Fix It |
|---------|----------------|---------------|
| **Missing the deadline** | Applications are not considered after the cut‑off. | Set calendar reminders 2–3 weeks before the deadline. |
| **Incomplete reference letters** | Chevening requires two completed references. | Confirm referees have submitted their letters and follow up politely. |
| **Weak personal statement** | Chevening looks for clear leadership potential. | Use a structured format: background, achievements, future impact. |
| **Not tailoring the application** | Chevening values relevance to UK and home country. | Align your goals with Chevening’s focus areas and UK policy priorities. |
| **Ignoring the “Chevening values” section** | Chevening assesses alignment with its values. | Explicitly mention how you embody leadership, networking, and commitment to development. |
| **Submitting unverified documents** | Documents must be official and up‑to‑date. | Request certified copies and double‑check dates. |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Can I apply for Chevening if I am still in the asylum process?  
Chevening typically requires that you have a confirmed refugee status or a valid asylum decision. If you are still awaiting a decision, you should wait until you receive official documentation before applying. Check the official website for any updates on this policy.

### 2. What if I have no English language test score?  
Most UK Master’s programmes require an English proficiency test. If you have not taken a test, you may need to do so before applying. Some universities offer English language courses that can be completed before the scholarship application. Verify the specific requirements on the university’s website.

### 3. How many reference letters are required?  
Chevening requires two reference letters: one academic and one professional or community-based. Ensure each referee understands the Chevening criteria and submits their letter through the official portal.

### 4. Is the scholarship open every year?  
Chevening Scholarships are awarded annually, but the application cycle and deadlines can change. Always check the official Chevening website for the current cycle’s dates.

### 5. Can I apply for a Chevening Scholarship if I already have a UK student visa?  
If you already hold a UK student visa, you may still apply for Chevening, but you must meet all eligibility criteria and submit a new application. The scholarship will cover tuition and living costs, potentially replacing your existing funding.

### 6. What happens after I submit my application?  
After submission, your application will be reviewed by the Chevening selection panel. If shortlisted, you may be invited for an interview. Successful candidates receive a scholarship offer, which you must accept and then proceed with the UK visa application.
