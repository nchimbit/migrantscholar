# STEM Australia
The Australian Awards Scholarship, which is funded up to AUD 30,912 per year, is available to Afghan women who are migrants in Australia pursuing studies in STEM fields. 
The scholarship aims to promote gender equality and empower women from developing countries, including Afghanistan, to undertake higher education and gain skills that can be applied to their home country.

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for the Australian Awards Scholarship and other STEM scholarships in Australia, Afghan women must hold a valid visa, such as a Permanent Humanitarian Visa, a Protection Visa, or a Temporary Graduate Visa. They must also meet specific eligibility criteria set by the scholarship providers, which may include academic achievements, English language proficiency, and a demonstrated commitment to their field of study.
