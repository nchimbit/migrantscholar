## Who Qualifies
* Women from Pakistan who have a bachelor's or master's degree can apply for the DAAD Scholarship
* Women from Pakistan who have a minimum of 2 years of work experience can apply for the Job Seeker Visa
* Women from Pakistan who have been accepted into a German university can apply for the Study Visa
* Women from Pakistan who have a strong command of the German language can apply for the Study Visa without IELTS

## What the Scholarship Covers
The DAAD Scholarship covers a monthly stipend of €750 for master's students and €1,000 for PhD students, as well as health insurance and travel costs. The Job Seeker Visa and Study Visa do not come with a stipend, but recipients can work part-time to support themselves.

## How to Apply
1. Check the official website for the latest information on eligibility and application deadlines
2. Prepare all required documents, including a valid passport, academic transcripts, and language proficiency certificates
3. Fill out the online application form and submit it along with the required documents
4. Pay the application fee, if applicable
5. Wait for the application to be processed and a decision to be made
6. If approved, apply for a visa at the German embassy or consulate in Pakistan
7. Attend a visa interview and provide biometric data, if required
8. Receive the visa and travel to Germany to start the program
