Afghan refugees in Canada can apply for the University of Toronto's Scholarship for Afghan Refugees, which provides full funding for undergraduate and graduate studies in STEM fields, covering up to $50,000 per year. Eligible applicants must hold a valid refugee protection claimant document or a permanent resident card in Canada. The scholarship is available for study at the University of Toronto, with the goal of supporting Afghan refugees in pursuing higher education in Canada.

## Who Qualifies
* Refugee protection claimants with a valid claimant document
* Permanent residents of Canada who were previously refugees
* Individuals who have been granted refugee status in Canada
* Applicants must be Afghan nationals
* Applicants must be planning to pursue undergraduate or graduate studies in a STEM field

## What the Scholarship Covers
The University of Toronto's Scholarship for Afghan Refugees provides full funding for undergraduate and graduate studies in STEM fields, covering:
* Tuition fees up to $50,000 per year
* Living expenses, including housing, food, and transportation
* Health insurance
* Books and other academic materials

## How to Apply
1. Review the eligibility criteria to ensure you are eligible for the scholarship
2. Choose your program of study and ensure it is a STEM field
3. Submit an application to the University of Toronto for admission
4. Once admitted, submit a separate application for the scholarship
5. Provide all required documents, including transcripts, language test results, and refugee documentation
6. Complete a personal statement outlining your academic goals and why you are a strong candidate for the scholarship
7. Provide two academic references
8. Submit your application by the deadline

## Required Documents
* Valid refugee protection claimant document or permanent resident card
* Passport
* Transcripts from previous academic institutions
* Language test results (IELTS or equivalent)
* Personal statement
* Two academic references
* Proof of admission to the University of Toronto
