## Emergency Funding for Eritrean Medical Asylum Seekers

The **Fulbright Foreign Student Program** offers $8,000 per year to international medical students in the USA, including Eritrean asylum seekers on **F‑1** or **J‑1** visas.  

---

### Who Qualifies

| Visa Category | Eligibility Criteria | Key Points |
|---------------|----------------------|------------|
| **F‑1** (Student Visa) | • Valid F‑1 status<br>• Enrollment in a U.S. medical school or graduate program<br>• Demonstrated financial need | Many asylum seekers maintain F‑1 status while completing a medical degree. |
| **J‑1** (Exchange Visitor Visa) | • Accepted into a U.S. exchange program (e.g., research or teaching)<br>• Visa sponsorship by a U.S. institution | J‑1 is common for clinical research internships. |
| **H‑4** (Dependent of H‑1B) | • Spouse of a H‑1B holder<br>• Pursuing medical studies while in the U.S. | Limited scholarship options but still eligible for some U.S. federal programs. |
| **DHS‑Sponsored Asylum Seekers** | • Granted asylum in the U.S.<br>• Holding an I‑94 and valid immigration status | These individuals may qualify for emergency aid from U.S. universities and nonprofit organizations. |
