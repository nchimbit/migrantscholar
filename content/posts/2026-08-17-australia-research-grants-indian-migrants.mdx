# Migrant Research
The Australia Awards Scholarships exist and qualify Indian migrants with a funding amount of up to AUD 30,000 per year for research grants in Australian universities. Indian migrants who are currently residing in Australia on a valid visa, such as a subclass 500 Student visa, subclass 485 Temporary Graduate visa, or subclass 189 Skilled Independent visa, may be eligible to apply for fully funded research grants to pursue their higher education in Australian universities.

To be eligible for fully funded research grants in Australian universities, Indian migrants must meet specific visa categories and eligibility criteria. Some of the common visa categories that may be eligible include subclass 500 Student visa, subclass 485 Temporary Graduate visa, subclass 189 Skilled Independent visa, subclass 190 Skilled Nominated visa, and subclass 489 Skilled Regional (Provisional) visa. It is essential to check the official government websites for the most up-to-date information on visa categories and eligibility criteria.
