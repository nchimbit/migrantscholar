# Canada Scholarships
The Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarships exist for international students, offering $50,000 per year for three years to pursue a graduate program at the University of Toronto. 
International students can apply for [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-funding/fully-funded) to study in Canada. The University of Toronto offers various graduate programs, including [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-level/phd) and [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.com/by-eligibility/refugees).

## Who Qualifies
International students with a valid study permit or those who are eligible to apply for a study permit under the following visa categories can apply: Student Visa, Work Permit, or Permanent Resident Visa. They must also meet the eligibility criteria for the specific graduate program they are applying to. [Canada scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/countries/Canada) are competitive, and applicants must demonstrate academic excellence and language proficiency.

## Available Scholarships
The following are some fully funded graduate programs available to international students at the University of Toronto:
### Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarships
* Amount: $50,000 per year for three years
* Eligibility: International students pursuing a graduate program at the University of Toronto
* Deadline: November 1, 2026
* Official URL: https://vanier.gc.ca/en/home-accueil.html
### Ontario Graduate Scholarship
* Amount: $15,000 per year for two years
* Eligibility: International students pursuing a graduate program at the University of Toronto
* Deadline: October 1, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.ontario.ca/page/ontario-graduate-scholarship
### University of Toronto Graduate Scholarship
* Amount: $10,000 per year for two years
* Eligibility: International students pursuing a graduate program at the University of Toronto
* Deadline: February 1, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.sgs.utoronto.ca/funding-awards/university-toronto-graduate-scholarship/
### Pierre Elliott Trudeau Foundation Scholarship
* Amount: $60,000 per year for three years
* Eligibility: International students pursuing a graduate program at the University of Toronto
* Deadline: December 1, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.trudeaufoundation.ca/en/programs/scholarship
### Canada Graduate Scholarships - Master's Program
* Amount: $17,500 per year for one year
* Eligibility: International students pursuing a master's program at the University of Toronto
* Deadline: December 1, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/Students-Etudiants/PG-CS/CANadaGradScholarships-BESCM_eng.asp

## Application Steps
1. Check the eligibility criteria for the specific graduate program and scholarship.
2. Review the [scholarship deadlines](https://migrantscholar.com/deadlines) and application requirements.
3. Submit an online application to the University of Toronto.
4. Upload required documents, including transcripts and language proficiency test scores.
5. Apply for a study permit, if required.
6. Provide proof of language proficiency, such as IELTS or TOEFL.
7. Submit letters of recommendation from academic or professional referees.
8. Wait for the application to be reviewed and a decision to be made.
