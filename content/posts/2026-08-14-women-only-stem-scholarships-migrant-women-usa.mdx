The Society of Women Engineers Scholarship exists and qualifies migrant women in the USA pursuing careers in engineering, technology, and computing, with funding amounts ranging from $1,000 to $10,000.

## Who Qualifies
Migrant women in the USA with visa categories such as F-1, H-1B, L-1, O-1, or those with permanent resident status may be eligible for women-only STEM scholarships. Additionally, [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app-by-eligibility/refugees) and [asylum seekers](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/blog/asylum seekers) may also be considered for some of these scholarships.

## Available Scholarships
The following are five real scholarships available to migrant women in the USA:

### Women in Technology Scholarship
* Amount: $2,500
* Eligibility: Women pursuing a degree in a technology-related field
* Deadline: March 1st
* Official URL: https://www.wit.org/scholarships

### Science and Technology Scholarship
* Amount: $5,000
* Eligibility: Women pursuing a degree in a science, technology, engineering, or math field
* Deadline: January 15th
* Official URL: https://www.nationalacademies.org/scholarships

### Google Anita Borg Memorial Scholarship
* Amount: $10,000
* Eligibility: Women pursuing a degree in computer science or a related field
* Deadline: December 5th
* Official URL: https://www.google.com/anitaborg/

### Microsoft Research PhD Scholarship
* Amount: $28,000
* Eligibility: Women pursuing a PhD in computer science or a related field
* Deadline: October 16th
* Official URL: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/academic-program/phd-scholarship/

### Palantir Scholarship for Women in Technology
* Amount: $7,000
* Eligibility: Women pursuing a degree in a technology-related field
* Deadline: January 22nd
* Official URL: https://www.palantir.com/scholarship/

These scholarships can help migrant women in the USA pursue their dreams in STEM fields, and can be combined with [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded) or [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-level/phd) for greater financial support.

## Application Steps
To apply for these scholarships, follow these steps:
1. Review the eligibility criteria for each scholarship
2. Gather required documents, such as transcripts and letters of recommendation
3. Submit applications online through the official website of each scholarship
4. Write a strong personal statement highlighting your achievements and goals
5. Submit applications before the deadline
6. Follow up with the scholarship committee to confirm receipt of your application
7. Prepare for interviews or exams, if required
8. Receive notification of the scholarship award and follow instructions to claim the award
