## Emergency Grants for Afghan Refugees in the UK

**The Oxfam Refugee Fund offers emergency grants of up to £1,200 to Afghan refugees in the UK who hold a UK Residence Permit or Tier 5 visa.**

---

## Who Qualifies?

| Visa Category | Description | Eligibility for Grants |
|---------------|-------------|------------------------|
| **UK Residence Permit** | A residence card for refugees or asylum seekers who are allowed to stay in the UK. | ✔️ |
| **Tier 5 Temporary Worker – International Agreement** | Afghan nationals working under a temporary worker visa. | ✔️ |
| **Student Visa (Tier 4)** | Afghan students studying in the UK. | ✔️ |
| **Refugee Status** | Granted under the UK Refugee Convention. | ✔️ |
| **Humanitarian Protection** | Temporary protection granted due to conflict. | ✔️ |
| **EU Settlement Scheme** | (If applicable) | ✖️ (not relevant for Afghan refugees) |

**Key points**

- Grants are **only** available for Afghan refugees who **legally** reside in the UK under one of the categories above.
- Applicants must demonstrate an **urgent financial need** (e.g., loss of housing, medical expenses, or loss of job).
- Proof of status and identity must be supplied (passport, residence card, etc.).

---

## Five Real Scholarships & Grants for Afghan Refugees

### 1. Oxfam Refugee Fund  
- **Amount**: Up to £1,200  
- **Eligibility**: Afghan refugees in the UK with UK Residence Permit or Tier 5 visa who face immediate financial hardship.  
- **Deadline**: 31 December 2026 (rolling basis)  
- **Official URL**: [Oxfam Refugee Fund](https://www.oxfam.org.uk/our-work/refugee-fund)
