## Who Qualifies
* Asylum seekers holding a Bridging Visa E (BVE) or a Temporary Protection Visa (TPV)
* Asylum seekers who have applied for a Protection Visa (subclass 866) or a Safe Haven Enterprise Visa (subclass 790)
* Individuals who have been granted a Permanent Protection Visa (subclass 866) or a Resolution of Status Visa (subclass 851)
* Applicants must meet the specific eligibility criteria for each scholarship program

## What the Scholarship Covers
The emergency funding provided through university support programs can cover various expenses, including:
* Living costs, such as accommodation, food, and transportation
* Tuition fees, including course materials and equipment
* Health insurance and medical expenses
* Childcare costs, if applicable
* Other expenses related to studying in Australia

## How to Apply
1. Research the available scholarship programs and their eligibility criteria

## Required Documents
