The Commonwealth Shared Scholarship Scheme exists and qualifies Nigerian migrants with a minimum of a second-class upper division, for a fully funded master's degree in healthcare in the UK, worth up to £19,890. This scholarship is part of the [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded) available to international students, including Nigerian migrants. 

## Who Qualifies
Nigerian migrants who hold a valid visa, such as a Tier 2 or Tier 4 visa, or have settled status in the UK, can apply for these scholarships. Additionally, those with refugee status or humanitarian protection can also be eligible for some of the scholarships. The UK government's [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-eligibility/refugees) can provide more information on the available options. 

### Available Scholarships
The following scholarships are available for Nigerian migrants in the UK:
1. **Chevening Scholarship**, up to £18,000, eligible for Nigerian migrants with a minimum of two years of work experience, deadline varies, [https://www.chevening.org/](https://www.chevening.org/)
2. **Commonwealth Shared Scholarship Scheme**, up to £19,890, eligible for Nigerian migrants with a minimum of a second-class upper division, deadline typically in December, [https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/](https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/)
3. **Erasmus Mundus Scholarship**, up to £47,000, eligible for Nigerian migrants with a minimum of a bachelor's degree, deadline typically in January, [https://www.erasmusmundus.be/](https://www.erasmusmundus.be/)
4. **Wellcome Trust Scholarship**, up to £30,000, eligible for Nigerian migrants with a minimum of a bachelor's degree in a healthcare-related field, deadline typically in July, [https://wellcome.org/](https://wellcome.org/)
5. **NHS Scholarship**, up to £5,000, eligible for Nigerian migrants working in the NHS, deadline typically in April, [https://www.nhs.uk/](https://www.nhs.uk/)
