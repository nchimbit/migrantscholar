## Scholarships
### Albert Einstein German Academic Refugee Initiative (DAFI) Scholarship
The Albert Einstein German Academic Refugee Initiative (DAFI) Scholarship provides funding up to $1,000 per month for refugee students pursuing higher education. Eligibility includes being a refugee with a valid refugee document, having a strong academic record, and being enrolled in a German university. Deadline is variable, and the official URL is https://www.daad.de/en/.
### Erasmus Mundus Scholarship
The Erasmus Mundus Scholarship provides funding up to $1,400 per month for master's students studying in Europe. Eligibility includes having a bachelor's degree, being from an eligible country, and being accepted into a participating Erasmus Mundus program. Deadline is variable, and the official URL is https://ec.europa.eu/programmes/erasmus-mundus/.
### European Union's Horizon 2020 Scholarship
The European Union's Horizon 2020 Scholarship provides funding up to $2,600 per month for researchers pursuing a PhD. Eligibility includes having a master's degree, being from an eligible country, and being accepted into a participating Horizon 2020 program. Deadline is variable, and the official URL is https://ec.europa.eu/programmes/horizon2020/.
### Joint Japan World Bank Graduate Scholarship Program
The Joint Japan World Bank Graduate Scholarship Program provides funding up to $30,000 per year for students pursuing a master's degree. Eligibility includes having a bachelor's degree, being from an eligible country, and being accepted into a participating program. Deadline is April 21, and the official URL is https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/scholarships.
### Turkey Scholarship
The Turkey Scholarship provides funding up to $800 per month for international students studying in Turkey. Eligibility includes having a strong academic record, being from an eligible country, and being accepted into a participating Turkish university. Deadline is February 20, and the official URL is https://www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr/.
