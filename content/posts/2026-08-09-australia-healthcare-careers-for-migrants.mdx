## Who Qualifies
Migrants on various visa categories, including Subclass 500, Subclass 485, Subclass 820, or Subclass 801, may be eligible to apply for these scholarships, allowing them to pursue a career in the Australian healthcare sector. This can lead to permanent residency, especially for those in high-demand fields like nursing, dentistry, and medicine, often found under [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-funding/fully-funded) or [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-level/phd).

## Available Scholarships
### Australian Government Research Training Program
The Australian Government Research Training Program provides up to $28,597 per annum. It is eligible for international students, including migrants, with a deadline varying by university. The official URL is https://www.education.gov.au/research-training-program.
### Monash University International Student Scholarships
Monash University International Student Scholarships offer up to $30,000 per annum. Eligibility is for international undergraduate and graduate students, with a deadline of October 31, 2026. The official URL is https://www.monash.edu/study/fees-scholarships/scholarships.
### University of Melbourne Graduate Research Scholarships
University of Melbourne Graduate Research Scholarships fund up to $31,200 per annum. Eligibility is for domestic and international students pursuing a graduate research degree, with deadlines of October 31, 2026. The official URL is https://scholarships.unimelb.edu.au/awards/graduate-research-scholarships.
### Australian Awards Scholarships
Australian Awards Scholarships cover full tuition fees, living expenses, and more, with eligibility for international students from participating countries. The deadline varies by country, and the official URL is https://www.australiaawards.gov.au/.
### Commonwealth Scholarships
Commonwealth Scholarships offer varying amounts. Eligibility is for citizens of Commonwealth countries, with deadlines by December 2026. The official URL is https://www.commonwealthscholarships.com/.
