# STEM UK Scholarships
The Chevening Scholarship is a fully funded scholarship that exists for Afghan migrants in the UK, with a funding amount of up to £18,000, and qualifies individuals who are looking to pursue a master's degree in a STEM field. 

## Who Qualifies
Afghan migrants who hold a valid visa in the UK, including those with Refugee or Humanitarian Protection status, Limited Leave to Remain, or Indefinite Leave to Remain, are eligible to apply for STEM scholarships. Additionally, migrant students with a Tier 4 student visa or a Tier 2 work visa may also be eligible. It is essential for applicants to check the specific eligibility criteria for each scholarship, as some may have additional requirements or restrictions.

## Available Scholarships
### Chevening Scholarship
The Chevening Scholarship offers up to £18,000 in funding, and eligible candidates must have a bachelor's degree and at least two years of work experience in a relevant field. The deadline for applications is usually in November, and applicants can visit the [official Chevening website](https://www.chevening.org/) for more information.

### Commonwealth Scholarship
The Commonwealth Scholarship provides full tuition fees, a monthly stipend, and other benefits, with eligibility restricted to citizens of Commonwealth countries, including Afghanistan.
