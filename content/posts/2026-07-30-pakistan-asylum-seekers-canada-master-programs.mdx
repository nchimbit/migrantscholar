The Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarships exist and qualify Pakistani asylum seekers for up to $50,000 per year for three years to pursue a master's program in Canada.

## Who Qualifies
Pakistani asylum seekers who have been granted refugee status in Canada or are in the process of applying for it may be eligible for various master's programs with funding. Eligible visa categories include:
- Protected persons
- Convention refugees
- Persons in need of protection
- Humanitarian-protected persons abroad

To be considered for these scholarships, applicants must meet specific eligibility criteria, often including language proficiency, academic requirements, and other conditions set by each scholarship program.

$10,000 to $20,000, eligibility includes being an international student, deadline varies, [official URL](https://www.utoronto.ca/)
