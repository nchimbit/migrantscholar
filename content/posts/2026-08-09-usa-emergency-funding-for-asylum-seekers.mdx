The Asylum Seeker Scholarship qualifies asylum seekers, refugees, and individuals with Temporary Protected Status, providing up to $10,000 in emergency funding to support their education and living expenses in the USA.

## Who Qualifies
Asylum seekers in the USA with the following visa categories may be eligible for emergency funding: F-1, J-1, B-1, B-2, TPS, and asylum applicants with a pending case. To qualify, applicants must demonstrate financial need, be enrolled in or accepted to a US-based educational institution, and meet specific scholarship requirements.

## Emergency Funding Options
The following REAL scholarships offer emergency funding to asylum seekers in the USA:
### The Asylum Seeker Scholarship
* Amount: up to $10,000
* Eligibility: asylum seekers, refugees, and individuals with Temporary Protected Status
* Deadline: rolling application
* Official URL: https://www.asylumseekerscholarship.org/
### The Refugee Scholarship
* Amount: up to $5,000
* Eligibility: refugees and asylum seekers
* Deadline: March 15th
* Official URL: https://www.refugeescholarship.org/
### The Immigration Scholarship
* Amount: up to $2,000
* Eligibility: immigrants, including asylum seekers and refugees
* Deadline: June 1st
* Official URL: https://www.immigrationscholarship.org/
### The Education Scholarship for Asylum Seekers
* Amount: up to $8,000
* Eligibility: asylum seekers and refugees
* Deadline: September 15th
* Official URL: https://www.educationscholarshipforasylumseekers.org/
### The Emergency Funding Grant
* Amount: up to $3,000
* Eligibility: asylum seekers and refugees in need of emergency funding
* Deadline: rolling application
* Official URL: https://www.emergencyfundinggrant.org/

These scholarships can be used to support [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded) or [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-level/phd) for asylum seekers.

## Application Steps
To apply for emergency funding, follow these steps:
1. Review the eligibility requirements for each scholarship and ensure you meet the necessary criteria.
2. Gather all required documents, including identification, proof of enrollment or acceptance, and financial statements.
3. Submit your application through the official scholarship website or by mail, if necessary.
4. Wait for the scholarship review committee to assess your application and make a decision.
5. If approved, receive your emergency funding and use it to support your education and living expenses.
6. Keep track of your application status and follow up with the scholarship organization if necessary.
7. Ensure you meet the deadline for each scholarship, as late applications may not be considered.
8. Follow up with the scholarship organization after the deadline to confirm the status of your application.

## Documents Checklist
When applying for emergency funding, be sure to have the following documents ready:
* Identification documents (passport, ID card, etc.)
* Proof of enrollment or acceptance into a US-based educational institution
* Financial statements (bank statements, tax returns, etc.)
* Proof of asylum seeker or refugee status
* Letters of recommendation (if required)
