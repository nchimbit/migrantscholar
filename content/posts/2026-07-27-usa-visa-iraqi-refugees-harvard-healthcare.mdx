## Who Qualifies
* Iraqi refugees with a valid visa category, including Special Immigrant Visas (SIV) or Refugee/Asylum status
* Applicants must have a strong academic record and a commitment to pursuing a career in healthcare
* Priority is given to applicants who have been displaced due to conflict or persecution and are seeking to rebuild their lives in the United States

## What the Scholarship Covers
The Harvard University Scholarship provides full funding for tuition and living expenses for the duration of the program. This includes:
* Tuition fees for the Master's or PhD program in healthcare
* Living expenses, including accommodation and food
* Health insurance and other benefits
* Travel costs to and from the United States

## How to Apply
1. Check the official scholarship website for the latest information on eligibility and application requirements
