Fulbright Scholarships
The Fulbright Scholarships are available to Iraqi refugees who are seeking to pursue higher education in the USA. These fully funded scholarships cover all expenses, including tuition fees, living expenses, and travel costs, with a funding amount of up to $100,000 per year. Iraqi refugees who hold a valid refugee visa are eligible to apply for these scholarships.

## Who Qualifies
Iraqi refugees who meet the following eligibility criteria can apply for the Fulbright Scholarships:
* Hold a valid refugee visa
* Are citizens of Iraq
* Have a strong academic record
* Meet the English language proficiency requirements
* Are applying for a Master's or PhD program in the USA
* Have not previously received a Fulbright Scholarship

## What the Scholarship Covers
The Fulbright Scholarships are fully funded and cover all expenses related to studying in the USA, including:
* Tuition fees: up to $50,000 per year
* Living expenses: up to $20,000 per year
* Travel costs: up to $5,000 per year
* Health insurance: up to $5,000 per year
* Book and equipment allowance: up to $1,000 per year

## How to Apply
To apply for the Fulbright Scholarships, follow these steps:
1. Check the eligibility criteria to ensure you qualify
2. Review the application requirements and prepare the necessary documents
3. Submit your application through the online portal
4. Take the required English language proficiency tests, such as IELTS
5. Prepare for and take the GRE or GMAT tests, if required
6. Submit your transcripts and academic records
7. Obtain letters of recommendation from academic or professional references
8. Attend an interview with the Fulbright selection committee, if shortlisted

## Required Documents
To apply for the Fulbright Scholarships, you will need to provide the following documents:
* Passport
* Refugee visa
* Academic transcripts and diplomas
* English language proficiency test results
* GRE or GMAT test results, if required
* Letters of recommendation
* Personal statement
* Study plan
