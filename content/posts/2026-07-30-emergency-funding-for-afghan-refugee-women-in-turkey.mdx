## Available Scholarships
### UNHCR DAFI Scholarship
The UNHCR DAFI scholarship provides up to $2,000 per year to refugee students, including Afghan refugee women in Turkey. Eligibility criteria include being a registered refugee with UNHCR, having a valid residence permit, and being enrolled in a higher education institution. The deadline for application is usually in July. For more information, visit the [UNHCR website](https://www.unhcr.org/).
### Islamic Development Bank Scholarship
The Islamic Development Bank scholarship provides full funding for undergraduate, master's, and PhD studies. Eligible candidates must be from an IDB member country, have a valid residence permit, and meet the required language proficiency. The deadline for application is usually in February. For more information, visit the [IDB website](https://www.isdb.org/).

The Fulbright scholarship provides full funding for master's and PhD studies in the United States. Eligible candidates must have a valid bachelor's degree, meet the required language proficiency, and be from a participating country. The deadline for application is usually in October. The Chevening scholarship provides full funding for master's studies in the United Kingdom. Eligible candidates must have a valid bachelor's degree, meet the required language proficiency, and be from a participating country. The deadline for application is usually in November. For more information, visit the [Chevening website](https://www.chevening.org/).

The [DAAD scholarship](https://migrantscholar.com/universities/daad) provides full funding for undergraduate, master's, and PhD studies in Germany.
