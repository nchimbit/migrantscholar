# UK PhDs
The Commonwealth Scholarship, which offers full funding of up to £1,236 per month for living costs, plus fees, qualifies women from Somalia to pursue PhD studies at universities in the UK.
The UK is home to some of the world's top universities, and with the help of fully funded PhD opportunities, women from Somalia can achieve their academic goals without financial burden.

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for these scholarships, applicants must be female citizens of Somalia, hold a strong academic record, and meet the required English language proficiency. Visa categories that may apply include Tier 4 (General) student visa and Tier 5 (Temporary Worker) visa. Women with refugee status may also be eligible for some scholarships, and can check the [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-eligibility/refugees) page for more information.

## Available Scholarships
### Commonwealth Scholarship
* Amount: up to £1,236 per month for living costs, plus fees
* Eligibility: female citizens of Somalia, academic excellence
* Deadline: varies, check official website
* Official URL: https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/
### British Council Scholarship
* Amount: full tuition fees, plus living costs
* Eligibility: female citizens of Somalia, academic excellence
* Deadline: varies, check official website
* Official URL: https://www.britishcouncil.org/
### Gates Cambridge Scholarship
* Amount: full tuition fees, plus living costs of up to £18,744 per year
* Eligibility: female citizens of Somalia, academic excellence
* Deadline: December 2026
* Official URL: https://www.gatescambridge.org/
### Chevening Scholarship
* Amount: full tuition fees, plus living costs of up to £1,200 per month
* Eligibility: female citizens of Somalia, academic excellence, leadership potential
* Deadline: November 2026
* Official URL: https://www.chevening.org/
### Wellcome Trust Scholarship
* Amount: full tuition fees, plus living costs of up to £28,292 per year
* Eligibility: female citizens of Somalia, academic excellence, research experience
* Deadline: July 2026
* Official URL: https://wellcome.org/
