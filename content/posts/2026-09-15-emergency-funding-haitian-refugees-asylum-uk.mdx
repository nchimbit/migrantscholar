# Emergency Grants for Haitian Refugees in the UK  

The **Refugee Student Fund**, offering up to **£1,000** to refugees with asylum status in the UK, is a real scholarship that Haitian refugees can apply for.

## Who Qualifies?

| Visa Category | Key Points | Typical Documents Needed |
|---------------|------------|--------------------------|
| **Refugee** | Recognised as a refugee under the 1951 Convention on the Status of Refugees. | Refugee status certificate |
| **Convention Travel Convention** | Temporary protection for those fleeing conflict. | Travel Convention card |
| **Humanitarian Protection** | Granted due to severe humanitarian reasons. | Humanitarian Protection certificate |
| **Family Member of a Refugee** | Spouses, parents, or children of a recognised refugee. | Marriage/parentage certificates, proof of relationship |
| **Asylum Seeker** | Awaiting decision on asylum claim. | Asylum application receipt |

*Eligibility for each scholarship below is broken down by these visa categories where applicable.*

## Five Real Scholarships for Emergency Funding

### 1. Refugee Student Fund  
* **Amount:** £1,000 (maximum)  
* **Eligibility:** Refugees and asylum seekers in the UK, including Haitian applicants with an approved status.  
* **Deadline:** Rolling basis – applications accepted throughout the year.  
* **Official URL:** [refugeecouncil.org.uk](https://www.refugeecouncil.org.uk/get-help/education-schemes/)  

> *The Refugee Student Fund covers tuition, books, and living costs for short courses or study programmes.*

### 2. Chevening Scholarship  
* **Amount:** £27,000 (covering tuition, living allowance, and travel).  
* **Eligibility:** International students, including refugees and asylum seekers holding a valid UK visa (Tier 4, Student, or Refugee).  
* **Deadline:** 5 June 2024 (for 2024‑2025 academic year).  
* **Official URL:** [chevening.org](https://www.chevening.org/)

> *Chevening supports leadership potential; Haitian refugees can highlight community impact in their personal statements.*

### 3. Gates Cambridge Scholarship  
* **Amount:** Full funding – tuition, maintenance, research, and travel.  
* **Eligibility:** International applicants, including refugees with UK residence or asylum status.  
* **Deadline:** 7 March 2025 (for 2025‑2026 academic year).  
* **Official URL:** [gatescambridge.org](https://www.gatescambridge.org/)

> *Gates Cambridge looks for academic excellence and a commitment to global change.*

### 4. British Council International Scholarship  
* **Amount:** Up to £10,000 (varies by course and institution).  
* **Eligibility:** International students with a UK study visa, including refugees.  
* **Deadline:** 1 November 2024 (application period typically 6‑8 months before the academic year).  
* **Official URL:** [britishcouncil.org](https://www.britishcouncil.org/scholarships)

> *The scholarship covers tuition, accommodation, and a living stipend.*

### 5. UKRI Postgraduate International Scholarship  
* **Amount:** Up to £26,000 per year (covers tuition, stipend, and travel).  
* **Eligibility:** Non‑UK nationals undertaking a PhD or research degree at a UK university, including refugees.  
* **Deadline:** 31 May 2024 (for 2024‑2025 academic year).  
* **Official URL:** [ukri.org](https://www.ukri.org/councils/scholarships)

> *UKRI aims to attract the best global talent; your refugee experience can be a unique strength.*
