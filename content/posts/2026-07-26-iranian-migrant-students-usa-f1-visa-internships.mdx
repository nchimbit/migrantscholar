# US Research
Iranian migrant students on an F1 visa can apply for research internships in the USA, with various scholarships available to support their studies. These internships are typically offered in STEM fields and provide students with hands-on research experience. Please check the official scholarship website for the latest information on funding amounts.

## Who Qualifies
* F1 visa holders
* Iranian migrant students
* Students pursuing undergraduate or graduate degrees in STEM fields
* Students with a strong academic record and research experience

## What the Scholarship Covers
The research internships typically cover:
* A stipend to support living expenses
* Tuition fees (in some cases)
* Research-related expenses
* Access to research facilities and equipment
* Mentorship and guidance from experienced researchers

## How to Apply
1. Search for research internships on the official websites of universities or organizations in the USA.
2. Review the eligibility criteria and application requirements for each internship.
3. Prepare a strong application package, including a resume, cover letter, and transcripts.
4. Submit the application package before the deadline.
5. Wait for the selection committee to review the application.
6. Participate in an interview or assessment (if required).
7. Receive notification of the application outcome.
8. Apply for an F1 visa (if not already holding one) and make travel arrangements to the USA.

## Required Documents
* Passport
* F1 visa (if already holding one)
* I-20 form (if already holding one)
* Transcripts
* Resume
* Cover letter
* Letters of recommendation (if required)
* Proof of English proficiency (if required)
