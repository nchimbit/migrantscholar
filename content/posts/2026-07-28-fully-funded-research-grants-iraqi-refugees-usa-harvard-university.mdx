## Who Qualifies
To qualify for these research grants, applicants must hold a valid US visa, such as F-1, J-1, or asylum status, and be Iraqi refugees. 
They must also meet the eligibility criteria for each specific scholarship, which may include academic performance, research experience, and language proficiency. 
Applicants with [DAAD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/universities/daad) experience may also be considered.
