# Study in Germany
The Friedrich Ebert Foundation Scholarship exists and qualifies Syrian asylum seekers in Germany with up to €1,200 per month funding for undergraduate and graduate studies. 
As a Syrian asylum seeker, you can apply for various healthcare scholarships to pursue a career in the medical field in Germany. 
To be eligible for these scholarships, you must hold a valid visa, such as a residence permit or asylum seeker visa, and meet specific requirements set by each scholarship provider. 
Some common visa categories for asylum seekers in Germany include the temporary residence permit, permanent residence permit, and asylum seeker visa. 
You can check the official government website for more information on visa categories and requirements.

As a Syrian asylum seeker in Germany, you may be eligible for various healthcare scholarships, including those that offer [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded).

## Application Steps
1. Review the eligibility criteria for each scholarship to ensure you meet the requirements.
2. Gather all required documents, including your visa, passport, and academic transcripts.
3. Fill out the application form for each scholarship, usually available on the provider's website.
4. Write a personal statement or motivation letter explaining why you are applying for the scholarship.
5. Submit your application before the deadline, either online or by post.
6. Wait for the scholarship provider to review your application and notify you of the outcome.
7. If selected, accept the scholarship offer and review the terms and conditions.
8. Use the scholarship funding to pursue your studies in Germany.
