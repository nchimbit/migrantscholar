The Australian Government's National Health and Medical Research Council (NHMRC) Postgraduate Scholarships exist for migrant researchers in Australia, with funding of up to $114,375 per annum, qualifying international students and Australian permanent residents.

## Who Qualifies
Migrant researchers in Australia with a valid visa, including but not limited to, subclass 500 (Student), subclass 457 (Temporary Business), subclass 482 (Temporary Skilled), subclass 485 (Temporary Graduate), and subclass 820 (Partner) may be eligible for various healthcare research grants. 

## Healthcare Research Grants
### NHMRC Postgraduate Scholarships
The NHMRC Postgraduate Scholarships provide funding of up to $114,375 per annum for a maximum of 3 years, for research in the field of health and medical research. Eligibility includes Australian citizens, permanent residents, and international students. Deadline varies, [check the official website](https://www.nhmrc.gov.au/funding/research-funding/nhmrc-postgraduate-scholarships) for details.

### Australian Rotary Health Research Grants
Australian Rotary Health Research Grants offer funding of up to $65,000 per annum for PhD students and up to $40,000 per annum for honours students, for research in the field of mental health, indigenous health, and rural health. Eligibility includes Australian citizens, permanent residents, and international students. Deadline is July 31st, [visit the official website](https://australianrotaryhealth.org.au/for-researchers/) for more information.

### Westpac Future Leaders Scholarship
The Westpac Future Leaders Scholarship provides funding of up to $120,000 for a maximum of 2 years, for research in the field of technology and innovation, including healthcare. Eligibility includes Australian citizens, permanent residents, and international students. Deadline is July 31st, [check the official website](https://www.westpac.com.au/about-westpac/scholarships/future-leaders-scholarship/) for details.

### CSIRO Postgraduate Scholarships
CSIRO Postgraduate Scholarships offer funding of up to $28,597 per annum for a maximum of 3 years, for research in the field of science and technology, including healthcare. Eligibility includes Australian citizens, permanent residents, and international students. Deadline is October 31st, [visit the official website](https://www.csiro.au/en/Careers/Studentships-Scholarships/Postgraduate-scholarships) for more information.

### Heart Foundation Health Professional Scholarship
The Heart Foundation Health Professional Scholarship provides funding of up to $15,000 per annum for a maximum of 2 years, for research in the field of cardiovascular health. Eligibility includes Australian citizens, permanent residents, and international students. Deadline is April 30th, [check the official website](https://www.heartfoundation.org.au/research/our-research-scholarships/health-professional-scholarship) for details.

## Application Steps
1. Review the eligibility criteria for each scholarship to ensure you qualify.
2. Prepare your documentation, including transcripts, proof of visa status, and evidence of language proficiency.
3. Research the field of study and identify potential supervisors.
4. Develop a research proposal, outlining the aims, methodology, and expected outcomes of your project.
5. Submit your application, including all required documentation, before the deadline.
6. Wait for the selection committee to review your application.
7. Receive notification of the outcome of your application.
8. Accept the offer and commence your research project.
