The German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD) Scholarships exist for Indian migrants in STEM fields, providing funding of up to €1,200 per month for postgraduate studies. 

## Who Qualifies
Indian migrants who hold a visa under the categories of job seeker, student, or research visa are eligible to apply for these STEM scholarships. The scholarships are specifically designed for international students, including [Indian migrants](https://migrantscholar.com/countries/India), who wish to pursue their higher education in [Germany](https://migrantscholar.com/countries/Germany). The DAAD scholarships are part of the [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-funding/fully-funded) offered to international students.

## Available Scholarships
The following are some of the available scholarships for Indian migrants in STEM fields:
### Development-Related Postgraduate Courses
* Amount: €1,200 per month
* Eligibility: Indian migrants with a Bachelor's degree in a STEM field
* Deadline: varies depending on the course
* Official URL: https://www.daad.de/en/study-and-research-in-germany/scholarships/development-related-postgraduate-courses/
### Postgraduate Scholarships for International Students
* Amount: €750 per month
* Eligibility: Indian migrants with a Bachelor's degree in a STEM field
* Deadline: October 1st
* Official URL: https://www.daad.de/en/study-and-research-in-germany/scholarships/postgraduate-scholarships/
### Research Grants for Doctoral Candidates
* Amount: €1,200 per month
* Eligibility: Indian migrants with a Master's degree in a STEM field
* Deadline: varies depending on the research project
* Official URL: https://www.daad.de/en/study-and-research-in-germany/scholarships/research-grants-doctoral-candidates/
### Study Scholarships for International Graduates in the Fields of Fine Art, Design, Visual Communication and Film
* Amount: €750 per month
* Eligibility: Indian migrants with a Bachelor's degree in a STEM field
* Deadline: October 1st
* Official URL: https://www.daad.de/en/study-and-research-in-germany/scholarships/study-scholarships-international-graduates/
### Bilateral Exchange of Academics
* Amount: €2,000 per month
* Eligibility: Indian migrants with a PhD in a STEM field
* Deadline: varies depending on the exchange program
* Official URL: https://www.daad.de/en/study-and-research-in-germany/scholarships/bilateral-exchange-academics/
