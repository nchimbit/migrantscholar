Scholarships for Syrian refugees exist in Germany, providing opportunities for those seeking to navigate the job market and pursue higher education. The DAAD Scholarship, for instance, offers funding of up to €750 per month for master's students. Please check the official scholarship website for the latest information on eligibility and funding.

## Quick Facts
| Award Name | Funding Amount | Deadline | Eligibility | Study Level | IELTS Required | Official Website |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DAAD Scholarship | up to €750 per month | varies | Syrian refugees, asylum seekers | Master's | yes | [www.daad.de](http://www.daad.de) |

## Who Qualifies
* Syrian nationals with refugee or asylum seeker status
* Holders of a valid German residence permit
* Applicants with a bachelor's degree or equivalent
* Those who have not stayed in Germany for more than 15 months at the time of application
* Individuals with a strong academic record and language proficiency in English or German

## What the Scholarship Covers
The DAAD Scholarship provides funding for master's students, covering:
* Monthly stipend of up to €750
* Health, accident, and personal liability insurance
* Travel allowance for scholarship holders from non-EU countries
* One-off study allowance for the purchase of study materials
* Rent subsidy for scholarship holders who do not live with their parents or spouse

## How to Apply
1. Review the eligibility criteria and required documents
2. Choose a suitable master's program at a German university
3. Submit an application to the university, including all required documents
4. Apply for the DAAD Scholarship online through the DAAD portal
5. Upload all required documents, including language proficiency certificates and academic transcripts
6. Submit the application and wait for the review process to begin
7. Receive an email with the result of the application, including an invitation to an interview if necessary
8. Confirm acceptance of the scholarship and begin preparations for studying in Germany

## Required Documents
* Completed application form
* CV in English or German
* Motivation letter in English or German
* Academic transcripts and degree certificates
* Language proficiency certificates (English or German)
* Copy of passport and residence permit
* Proof of financial support (if applicable)
