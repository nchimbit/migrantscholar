The DAAD Helmut-Schmidt-Programme qualifies African migrants for a fully funded Master's program in Germany with a funding amount of up to €934 per month. 
This program provides opportunities for African migrants to pursue their higher education in Germany, with various fields of study available. 
To be eligible, African migrants must have a Bachelor's degree and meet specific language requirements. 
African migrants can apply for [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded) in Germany, which cover tuition fees, living expenses, and other study-related costs. 
The [DAAD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/universities/daad) are a popular choice among African migrants, offering a range of programs and funding opportunities.

## Who Qualifies
African migrants who hold a visa category of job seeker, student, or family reunification may be eligible for fully funded Master's programs in Germany. 
The visa categories are as follows: 
- Job seeker visa: for those seeking employment in Germany
- Student visa: for those enrolled in a German university
- Family reunification visa: for those joining family members in Germany
African migrants with a [scholarship for refugees](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-eligibility/refugees) can also apply for these programs. 
To check the [scholarship deadlines](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/deadlines), visit the official website of the scholarship provider.

### DAAD Helmut-Schmidt-Programme
The DAAD Helmut-Schmidt-Programme offers a funding amount of up to €934 per month, and the eligibility criteria include a Bachelor's degree and language proficiency in English or German. 
The deadline for application is usually in July, and the official URL is https://www.daad.de/en/study-and-research-in-germany/scholarships/daad-scholarships/helmut-schmidt-programme/. 
African migrants can apply for [Canada scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/countries/Canada) as an alternative, but the DAAD Helmut-Schmidt-Programme is a more popular choice.

### Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung Scholarship
The Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung Scholarship offers a funding amount of up to €1,200 per month, and the eligibility criteria include a Bachelor's degree and language proficiency in English or German. 
The deadline for application is usually in July, and the official URL is https://www.kas.de/en/scholarships/. 
This scholarship is suitable for African migrants who are interested in [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-level/phd) but want to pursue a Master's degree first.

### Friedrich-Ebert-Stiftung Scholarship
The Friedrich-Ebert-Stiftung Scholarship offers a funding amount of up to €1,200 per month, and the eligibility criteria include a Bachelor's degree and language proficiency in English or German. 
The deadline for application is usually in July, and the official URL is https://www.fes.de/en/scholarships/. 
African migrants who are interested in [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-eligibility/refugees) can also apply for this scholarship.

### Heinrich Böll Foundation Scholarship
The Heinrich Böll Foundation Scholarship offers a funding amount of up to €1,200 per month, and the eligibility criteria include a Bachelor's degree and language proficiency in English or German. 
The deadline for application is usually in March, and the official URL is https://www.boell.de/en/scholarships. 
This scholarship is suitable for African migrants who are interested in pursuing a Master's degree in a field related to [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded).

### Deutschlandstipendium
The Deutschlandstipendium offers a funding amount of up to €300 per month, and the eligibility criteria include a Bachelor's degree and language proficiency in English or German. 
The deadline for application is usually in July, and the official URL is https://www.deutschland-stipendium.de/en/. 
African migrants can apply for this scholarship as a supplement to their main [DAAD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/universities/daad).
