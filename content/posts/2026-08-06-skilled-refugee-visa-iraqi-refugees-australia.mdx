The Australia Awards Scholarships exist and qualify Iraqi refugees for funding up to AUD 30,000 per year to pursue healthcare careers in Australia.
To qualify, applicants must hold a Skilled Refugee Visa, also known as the subclass 189 or 491 visa, which allows them to live and work in Australia permanently.
The Australian Government's Skilled Refugee Visa provides a pathway to employment for Iraqi refugees, and there are several scholarships available to support their education and career goals.

## Who Qualifies
Iraqi refugees who have been granted a Skilled Refugee Visa or other eligible visa categories may qualify for scholarships to pursue healthcare careers in Australia.
The visa categories that may qualify include:
* Skilled Refugee Visa (subclass 189 or 491)
* Temporary Graduate Visa (subclass 485)
* Skilled Independent Visa (subclass 189)
* Other visa categories may also be eligible, depending on the specific scholarship program.
