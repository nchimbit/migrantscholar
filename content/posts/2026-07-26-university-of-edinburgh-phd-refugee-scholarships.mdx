The University of Edinburgh PhD Scholarships for Refugee Students exist, and refugee students who are pursuing a PhD in the UK are eligible for a fully funded scholarship amounting to £28,750 per year. 
The University of Edinburgh offers various [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-funding/fully-funded) to support refugee students in their academic pursuits. 
Refugee students who are interested in pursuing a [PhD](https://migrantscholar.com/by-level/phd) in the UK can also explore other [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.com/by-eligibility/refugees) offered by the University of Edinburgh.

## Who Qualifies
Refugee students who hold a visa under the following categories are eligible to apply: Humanitarian Protection, Limited Leave to Remain, or Discretionary Leave to Remain. 
These students can apply for various [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-level/phd) offered by the University of Edinburgh. 
It is essential to note that students with other types of visas, such as student visas or work visas, are not eligible for these scholarships.
