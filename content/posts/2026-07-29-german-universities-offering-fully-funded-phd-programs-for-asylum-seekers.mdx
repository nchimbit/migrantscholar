The Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung Scholarship qualifies asylum seekers for a funding amount of up to €1,200 per month to pursue a PhD program at German universities.

## Who Qualifies
Asylum seekers with a university degree and a strong academic record, who have been granted a residence permit in the category of §23 or §24 of the German Residence Act, are eligible to apply for fully funded PhD programs in Germany. Visa categories such as subsidiary protection or humanitarian admission may also be considered. Asylum seekers who have been accepted into a PhD program at a German university can apply for [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-funding/fully-funded) to support their studies.
