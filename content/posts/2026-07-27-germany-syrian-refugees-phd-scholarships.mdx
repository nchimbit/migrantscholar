## Who Qualifies
To qualify for fully funded PhD opportunities in Germany for Syrian refugees, you must have a valid visa category such as a residence permit for the purpose of studying or a visa for humanitarian reasons. Common visa categories for Syrian refugees in Germany include:
- Subsidiary protection 
- Refugee status
- Leave to remain 
- Family reunification

## Scholarships
### Helmut-Schmidt-Programme 
The Helmut-Schmidt-Programme by DAAD offers up to €1,200 per month to Syrian refugees for PhD studies in STEM fields with an application deadline of July 31 and can be found at https://www.daad.de/en/study-and-research-in-germany/scholarships/development-related-postgraduate-courses/helmut-schmidt-programme/. 
To find more [DAAD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/universities/daad), you can visit the DAAD official website.

### Friedrich-Ebert-Stiftung Scholarship 
The Friedrich-Ebert-Stiftung Scholarship is available to Syrian refugees for PhD studies in STEM fields with a funding amount of up to €1,200 per month, eligibility includes a strong academic record and social commitment, and the deadline is on September 30, check https://www.fes.de/en/scholarship for more information.
