To be eligible, students must be currently enrolled at an Australian university and hold a valid visa.

Yemeni refugees in Australia who hold a valid visa, such as a Temporary Protection Visa (TPV) or a Permanent Protection Visa (PPV), may be eligible for emergency funding.
