ANU Scholarships
Pakistani migrants in STEM fields are eligible for the Australian National University (ANU) Scholarships, with funding amounts varying up to full tuition fees. The scholarships are designed to support international students, including migrants, in pursuing their higher education goals in Australia. The ANU Scholarships provide an excellent opportunity for Pakistani migrants to study in a world-renowned university.

## Who Qualifies
* Pakistani migrants holding a valid visa, including but not limited to:
  * Skilled Independent visa (subclass 189)
  * Skilled Nominated visa (subclass 190)
  * Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485)
  * Student visa (subclass 500)
* Migrants who have completed their previous degree in a STEM field (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics)
* Migrants who meet the English language proficiency requirements (IELTS 6.5 or equivalent)
* Migrants who meet the admission requirements for their chosen course at ANU

## What the Scholarship Covers
The ANU Scholarships provide varying funding amounts up to full tuition fees for the duration of the course. The scholarship does not cover other expenses such as living costs, accommodation, and travel. The funding breakdown is as follows:
* Undergraduate students: Up to AUD 25,000 per year
* Postgraduate students: Up to AUD 30,000 per year
* PhD students: Up to AUD 40,000 per year
The scholarship is paid in installments, with the first installment paid upon commencement of the course.

## How to Apply
1. Check the official ANU website for the latest information on the scholarships and eligibility criteria.
2. Review the admission requirements for your chosen course and ensure you meet the English language proficiency requirements.
3. Submit an application for admission to ANU, including all required documents and transcripts.
4. Once you have received an offer of admission, you can apply for the ANU Scholarships.
5. Complete the scholarship application form and submit it along with all required documents.
6. Ensure you meet the visa requirements and apply for a student visa (subclass 500) if necessary.
7. Wait for the scholarship selection committee to review your application and make a decision.
8. If successful, accept the scholarship offer and commence your course at ANU.

## Required Documents
* Passport
* Visa (if applicable)
* Birth certificate
* Academic transcripts and certificates
* English language proficiency test results (IELTS or equivalent)
* Proof of completion of previous degree in a STEM field
* Proof of admission to ANU
* Scholarship application form
