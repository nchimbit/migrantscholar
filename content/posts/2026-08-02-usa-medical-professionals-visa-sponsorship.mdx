## Who Qualifies
International medical professionals can apply for USA visa sponsorship under various categories, including:
- H-1B visa for specialty occupations
- J-1 visa for exchange visitors
- O-1 visa for individuals with extraordinary abilities
To be eligible, applicants must meet specific requirements, such as having a bachelor's degree in a healthcare field, proficiency in English, and relevant work experience.

## Scholarships
### Fulbright Scholarship
* Amount: up to $100,000
* Eligibility: international medical professionals with a bachelor's degree in a healthcare field
* Deadline: varies by country
* Official URL: https://fulbrightscholars.org/
### National Health Service Corps Scholarship
* Amount: up to $170,000
* Eligibility: students pursuing primary care careers
* Deadline: April 25
* Official URL: https://nhsc.hrsa.gov/scholarships/index.html
### American Medical Association Foundation Scholarship
* Amount: up to $10,000
* Eligibility: medical students underrepresented in the medical profession
* Deadline: June 15
* Official URL: https://www.ama-assn.org/about/ama-foundation
### Association of American Medical Colleges Scholarship
* Amount: up to $10,000
* Eligibility: medical students from underrepresented groups
* Deadline: October 15
* Official URL: https://www.aamc.org/
### Harvard Medical School Scholarship
* Amount: up to $60,000
* Eligibility: international medical students
* Deadline: March 15
* Official URL: https://hms.harvard.edu/

## Application Steps
1. Choose a scholarship program and review the eligibility criteria
2. Prepare the required documents, including transcripts, letters of recommendation, and a personal statement
3. Submit the application form and supporting documents before the deadline
4. Take the required English language proficiency test, such as TOEFL or IELTS
5. Take the Medical College Admission Test (MCAT) for medical school applicants
6. Apply for a student visa (F-1 or J-1) or work visa (H-1B or O-1)
7. Attend a visa interview at the US embassy or consulate in the home country
8. Receive the visa and travel to the USA to begin studies or work
