# Melbourne Scholar
The Melbourne Humanitarian Scholarship exists, and it is offered to Afghan nationals, providing a funding amount of up to $110,000 over three years, and is available at the University of Melbourne for undergraduate studies. 
The University of Melbourne offers various fully funded undergraduate scholarships, including the Melbourne Humanitarian Scholarship, to support Afghan migrants and asylum seekers in Australia. 
To qualify for these scholarships, applicants must hold a valid Australian visa, such as a Temporary Protection Visa (subclass 785), a Safe Haven Enterprise Visa (subclass 790), or a Permanent Protection Visa (subclass 866).

## Who Qualifies
The scholarships are available to Afghan nationals who are asylum seekers or migrants with a valid Australian visa, including:
- Temporary Protection Visa (subclass 785)
- Safe Haven Enterprise Visa (subclass 790)
- Permanent Protection Visa (subclass 866)
Applicants must also meet the academic requirements for their chosen undergraduate course at the University of Melbourne.
