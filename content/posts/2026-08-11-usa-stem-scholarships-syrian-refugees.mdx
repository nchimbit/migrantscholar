# STEM Scholarships
The Open Society Foundations Scholarship exists for Syrian refugees, providing up to $25,000 in funding for undergraduate and graduate studies in the USA. 
The Open Society Foundations Scholarship is one of the many [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded) available for Syrian refugees in the USA, where they can pursue [STEM](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/subjects/stem) fields such as engineering, computer science, and mathematics.

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for these scholarships, Syrian refugees must hold a valid visa category such as F-1, J-1, or asylum status. They must also meet the specific eligibility criteria set by each scholarship provider, which may include academic performance, language proficiency, and community involvement. For more information on [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-eligibility/refugees), visit our website.

## Application Steps
To apply for these scholarships, follow these steps:
1. Review the eligibility criteria and ensure you meet the requirements.
2. Gather all necessary documents, including transcripts, letters of recommendation, and visa documents.
3. Fill out the online application form and submit it by the deadline.
4. Prepare for and take any required language proficiency exams, such as TOEFL or IELTS.
5. Submit your application and supporting documents to the scholarship provider.
6. Wait for the scholarship committee to review your application and make a decision.
7. If selected, receive and accept the scholarship award.
8. Use the scholarship funding to pursue your academic goals in the USA.

## Documents Checklist
When applying for scholarships, make sure to have the following documents ready:
* Valid visa or green card
* Transcripts and diplomas from previous academic institutions
* Letters of recommendation from academic or professional mentors
* Language proficiency exam results, if required
* Proof of financial need, if required
* Personal statement or essay, if required

## Frequently Asked Questions
We have compiled a list of frequently asked questions to help you navigate the scholarship application process:
1. What is the difference between a grant and a scholarship?
A grant is typically need-based, while a scholarship is merit-based.
2. Can I apply for multiple scholarships at the same time?
Yes, you can apply for multiple scholarships, but make sure to review the eligibility criteria and deadlines carefully.
3. What is the average amount of a scholarship award?
The average amount varies depending on the scholarship provider, but it can range from $1,000 to $50,000 or more.
4. How do I increase my chances of getting a scholarship?
Make sure to meet the eligibility criteria, submit a strong application, and follow up with the scholarship provider if necessary.
5. Can I use a scholarship to pursue a [PhD](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-level/phd) degree?
Yes, many scholarships are available for PhD students, including the [DAAD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/universities/daad).
6. What is the deadline for most scholarship applications?
Deadlines vary, but most scholarship applications are due between December and April, so check the [scholarship deadlines](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/deadlines) page for more information.
7. Can I apply for a scholarship if I am already enrolled in a university?
Yes, many scholarships are available for currently enrolled students, including [Canada scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/countries/Canada).
8. How do I know if a scholarship is legitimate?
Research the scholarship provider and read reviews from previous recipients to ensure it is a legitimate opportunity.
