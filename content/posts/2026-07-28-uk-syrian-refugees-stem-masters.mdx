# UK STEM Scholarships
The Chevening Scholarship exists and qualifies Syrian refugees with a bachelor's degree for a fully funded master's degree in STEM fields, covering up to £18,000 in living costs and £14,440 in tuition fees. 
This prestigious award is a great opportunity for Syrian refugees to pursue their higher education dreams in the UK. 
For those interested in [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded), the UK offers a wide range of options, including [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-level/phd) and [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-eligibility/refugees).

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for these scholarships, applicants must have a bachelor's degree in a STEM field, meet the English language requirements, and have a valid visa category such as Tier 4 student visa, family visa, or asylum seeker. 
Additionally, they must have been displaced from Syria and have refugee status in the UK.

## Available Scholarships
### Chevening Scholarship
The Chevening Scholarship covers up to £18,000 in living costs and £14,440 in tuition fees, is open to Syrian refugees with a bachelor's degree in a STEM field, and the deadline is in November, [https://www.chevening.org/](https://www.chevening.org/).
### Commonwealth Scholarship
The Commonwealth Scholarship covers full tuition fees, living costs, and travel expenses, is open to Syrian refugees with a bachelor's degree in a STEM field, and the deadline is in December, [https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/](https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/).
### Imperial College London President's PhD Scholarship
The Imperial College London President's PhD Scholarship covers full tuition fees and a stipend of £21,000 per year, is open to Syrian refugees with a bachelor's degree in a STEM field, and the deadline is in January, [https://www.imperial.ac.uk/study/pg/fees-and-funding/scholarships-a-z/presidents-phd-scholarship/](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/study/pg/fees-and-funding/scholarships-a-z/presidents-phd-scholarship/).
### University of Cambridge Schlumberger Foundation Faculty for the Future Fellowship
The University of Cambridge Schlumberger Foundation Faculty for the Future Fellowship covers up to $50,000 per year, is open to Syrian refugees with a bachelor's degree in a STEM field, and the deadline is in November, [https://www.facultyforthefuture.net/](https://www.facultyforthefuture.net/).
### University of Oxford Weidenfeld-Hoffmann Scholarships
The University of Oxford Weidenfeld-Hoffmann Scholarships cover full tuition fees and a stipend of £14,000 per year, is open to Syrian refugees with a bachelor's degree in a STEM field, and the deadline is in January, [https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/fees-and-funding/graduate-scholarships/university-of-oxford-scholarships/weidenfeld-hoffmann-scholarships](https://www.ox.ac.uk/admissions/graduate/fees-and-funding/graduate-scholarships/university-of-oxford-scholarships/weidenfeld-hoffmann-scholarships).
