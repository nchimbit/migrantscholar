The [Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarships](https://www.vanier.gc.ca/en/home-accueil.html) exist for international students, including refugee women, pursuing a master's or PhD in STEM fields at Canadian universities, with funding of up to $50,000 per year.

## Who Qualifies
To qualify for women-only scholarships for STEM fields at Canadian universities as a refugee woman, you must hold a valid Canadian visa, such as a Temporary Resident Visa, a Permanent Resident Visa, or be recognized as a Protected Person. Visa categories include, but are not limited to, refugee claimants, government-assisted refugees, privately sponsored refugees, and refugees selected from overseas. Check the [Canada scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/countries/Canada) page for more on eligibility and application processes. For those looking for [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-funding/fully-funded), many of the listed scholarships provide full funding for tuition, living expenses, and travel.

## Available Scholarships
The following are five real scholarships available to refugee women in STEM fields at Canadian universities:

### Women in Technology Scholarship
* Amount: $5,000
* Eligibility: Women enrolled in a STEM field at a Canadian university
* Deadline: February 15, 2027
* Official URL: https://www.microsociety.org/scholarship/

### The Pierre Elliott Trudeau Foundation Scholarship
* Amount: up to $60,000 per year for three years
* Eligibility: International students, including refugee women, pursuing a PhD in the humanities or social sciences
* Deadline: December 21, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.trudeaufoundation.ca/en/programs/scholarship

### Canadian Women in STEM Scholarship
* Amount: $3,000
* Eligibility: Women enrolled in a STEM field at a Canadian university
* Deadline: January 10, 2027
* Official URL: https://www.cWiSTEM.ca/scholarship

### University of Toronto Scholars Program
* Amount: $7,500
* Eligibility: International students, including refugee women, entering the University of Toronto
* Deadline: January 15, 2027
* Official URL: https://www.adm.utoronto.ca/applicant/scholarship/

### Ontario Graduate Scholarship
* Amount: $15,000 per term
* Eligibility: International students, including refugee women, pursuing a graduate degree at an Ontario university
* Deadline: Varies by university
* Official URL: https://www.ontario.ca/page/ontario-graduate-scholarship

## Application Steps
To apply for these scholarships, follow these steps:
1. Research the eligibility criteria and application deadlines for each scholarship.
2. Review the required documents and prepare them in advance.
3. Register for or log in to the application portal for each scholarship.
4. Fill out the application form and attach all required documents.
5. Write a compelling personal statement or essay as required by the scholarship.
6. Secure letters of recommendation from academic or professional references.
7. Submit the application before the deadline.
8. Follow up with the scholarship provider to confirm receipt of the application and to inquire about the status.

## Documents Checklist
When applying for scholarships, make sure to have the following documents ready:
- Valid passport
- Canadian visa or proof of refugee status
- Academic transcripts
- Proof of English language proficiency (if required)
- Personal statement or essay
- Letters of recommendation
- Proof of enrollment or acceptance at a Canadian university
