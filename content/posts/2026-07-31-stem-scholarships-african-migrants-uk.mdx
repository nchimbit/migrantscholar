# STEM UK
The Commonwealth Scholarships are available to African migrants in the UK, providing up to £1,347 per month for living costs, with eligibility for applicants who hold a first degree of at least upper second class, and meet the UK visa requirements. African migrants who are looking to pursue STEM courses in the UK can benefit from this scholarship. 

## Who Qualifies
African migrants who are looking to pursue STEM courses in the UK and hold a valid visa, including Tier 2 General visa, Tier 4 General student visa, Tier 5 Temporary worker visa, or have a family visa, can apply for these scholarships. The eligibility criteria may vary depending on the scholarship, but generally, applicants should have a good academic record and meet the language requirements.
