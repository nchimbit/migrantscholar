Refugee professionals can pursue healthcare career opportunities in Australia and Germany through various scholarships. ## Who Qualifies
Refugee professionals who hold a valid visa, such as a Temporary Protection Visa (TPV) or a Permanent Protection Visa (PPV) in Australia, or a residence permit in Germany, may be eligible for these scholarships. Additionally, asylum seekers who have been recognized as refugees by the United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees (UNHCR) or the Australian or German government may also be eligible. [Scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-eligibility/refugees) can provide opportunities for career advancement and professional development in the healthcare sector.
