# Aust Scholarships
The Australia Awards Scholarship exists for Afghan refugee women, providing up to AUD 30,000 per year for healthcare careers. 
The Australian Government offers various university programs for Afghan refugee women in healthcare, including [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded) and [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-eligibility/refugees). 

## Who Qualifies
Afghan refugee women with valid visas, including temporary protection visas, safe haven enterprise visas, and permanent protection visas, may apply for these scholarships. 

## Available Scholarships
### Australia Awards Scholarship
AU$30,000 per year, eligibility includes Afghan refugee women in healthcare careers, deadline 30 April 2026, [https://www.australiaawards.gov.au/](https://www.australiaawards.gov.au/)
### Research Training Program Scholarship
AU$28,597 per year, eligibility includes master's and [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-level/phd) applicants, deadline 31 October 2026, [https://www.education.gov.au/research-training-program](https://www.education.gov.au/research-training-program)
### International Postgraduate Research Scholarship
AU$28,597 per year, eligibility includes master's and [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-level/phd) applicants, deadline 31 August 2026, [https://www.education.gov.au/international-postgraduate-research-scholarship](https://www.education.gov.au/international-postgraduate-research-scholarship)
### Endeavour Scholarship
AU$140,000 for master's and [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-level/phd), eligibility includes international students, deadline 15 November 2026, [https://www.australiaawards.gov.au/scholarships/endeavour-scholarships](https://www.australiaawards.gov.au/scholarships/endeavour-scholarships)
### Australian Government Research Training Program Scholarship
AU$28,597 per year, eligibility includes master's and [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-level/phd) applicants, deadline 31 October 2026, [https://www.education.gov.au/research-training-program](https://www.education.gov.au/research-training-program)
