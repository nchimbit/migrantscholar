DAAD Scholarship
The DAAD Scholarship is a fully funded research opportunity that exists for Indian migrants, providing a funding amount of up to €1,200 per month, who are looking to pursue higher education in Germany. 
Indian migrants who are residing in Germany under various visa categories, such as a student visa, work visa, or family reunification visa, are eligible to apply for this scholarship. 
The DAAD Scholarship is one of the most prestigious scholarships available for international students, including migrants, and is a great opportunity to pursue [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded) in Germany.

## Who Qualifies
The DAAD Scholarship is open to Indian migrants who are residing in Germany under various visa categories, including:
- Student visa (Visum zu Studienzwecken)
- Work visa (Visum zu Erwerbszwecken)
- Family reunification visa (Visum zur Familienzusammenführung)
- Refugee visa (Visum für Flüchtlinge)
To be eligible, applicants must also meet the language requirements, which include proficiency in German or English, depending on the course of study. 
For [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-level/phd), applicants must have a master's degree in a relevant field, while for master's scholarships, a bachelor's degree is required.

- Amount: Up to €1,200 per month

- Amount: Up to €1,000 per month

- Amount: Up to €1,200 per month
