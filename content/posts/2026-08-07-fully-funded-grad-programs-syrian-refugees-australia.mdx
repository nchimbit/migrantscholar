# AusAid
The Australia Awards Scholarships exist and qualify Syrian refugees for fully funded graduate programs at Australian universities with funding up to $30,000 per year. 
Syrian refugees can apply for [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded) to pursue higher education in Australia. 

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for fully funded graduate programs, Syrian refugees must hold a valid Australian visa, such as the Temporary Protection Visa (TPV) or the Safe Haven Enterprise Visa (SHEV). They must also meet the eligibility criteria for each scholarship, including academic requirements and language proficiency. 
Refugees can check their eligibility for [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-eligibility/refugees) and [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-level/phd) on the official websites of Australian universities.

## Fully Funded Scholarships
### Australia Awards Scholarships
* Amount: up to $30,000 per year
* Eligibility: Syrian refugees with a valid Australian visa
* Deadline: April 30, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.australiaawards.gov.au/
### University of Sydney Scholarship
* Amount: up to $40,000 per year
* Eligibility: Syrian refugees with a bachelor's degree and a valid Australian visa
* Deadline: July 31, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.sydney.edu.au/scholarships/
### Monash University Scholarship
* Amount: up to $30,000 per year
* Eligibility: Syrian refugees with a bachelor's degree and a valid Australian visa
* Deadline: October 31, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.monash.edu/graduate-research/future-students/scholarships
### Australian National University Scholarship
* Amount: up to $28,000 per year
* Eligibility: Syrian refugees with a bachelor's degree and a valid Australian visa
* Deadline: November 30, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.anu.edu.au/study/scholarships
### University of Melbourne Scholarship
* Amount: up to $32,000 per year
* Eligibility: Syrian refugees with a bachelor's degree and a valid Australian visa
* Deadline: December 31, 2026
* Official URL: https://scholarships.unimelb.edu.au/

## Application Steps
1. Check eligibility for each scholarship
2. Prepare required documents, including academic transcripts and language proficiency test results
3. Submit an application for a valid Australian visa
4. Apply for the scholarship through the official website
5. Submit a personal statement and CV
6. Provide proof of English language proficiency, such as IELTS or TOEFL test results
7. Apply for admission to an Australian university
8. Wait for the scholarship outcome and prepare for departure to Australia
