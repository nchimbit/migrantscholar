The Asylum Seeker Scholarship qualifies Iranian refugees in the USA with up to $10,000 funding amount. 
The Asylum Seeker Scholarship is designed to support individuals who are seeking asylum in the United States, including Iranian refugees, with a focus on providing emergency funding to help them settle and pursue their education.

## Who Qualifies
Iranian refugees in the USA who hold certain visa categories, such as asylum status, refugee status, or Special Immigrant Visa (SIV), may be eligible for emergency funding. Eligible visa categories include:
- Asylum status
- Refugee status
- Special Immigrant Visa (SIV)
- Temporary Protected Status (TPS)
- Deferred Enforced Departure (DED)

## Application Steps
1. Review the eligibility criteria for each scholarship
2. Gather required documents, such as proof of asylum status and academic transcripts
3. Fill out the application form, providing detailed information about your background and educational goals
4. Submit the application, ensuring all required documents are attached
5. Wait for the review process, which may take several weeks or months
6. Receive notification of the decision, either approved or rejected
7. If approved, receive the scholarship award and use it for educational purposes
8. Report back to the scholarship provider, providing updates on your academic progress

## Documents Checklist
- Proof of asylum status or refugee status
- Academic transcripts
