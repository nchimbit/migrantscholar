The DAAD Scholarship, which qualifies asylum seekers pursuing healthcare careers in Germany, offers funding of up to €750 per month. As an asylum seeker in Germany, you can access various scholarships to support your education and career goals in the healthcare sector. If you are looking for [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded) or [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-eligibility/refugees), there are several options available.

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for these scholarships, you must have a valid visa, such as a residence permit or a temporary residence permit, and be pursuing a degree in a healthcare-related field. Some common visa categories for asylum seekers in Germany include:
- Subsidiary protection
- Refugee status
- Temporary residence permit
- Humanitarian residence permit
Asylum seekers with these visa categories can apply for [DAAD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/universities/daad) or other scholarships that support international students and refugees.

## Available Scholarships
### DAAD Scholarship
The DAAD Scholarship offers up to €750 per month to international students, including asylum seekers, pursuing a degree in Germany. Eligibility includes a bachelor's degree, proficiency in German, and admission to a German university. Deadline: Varies, [https://www.daad.de](https://www.daad.de).
### Friedrich Ebert Foundation Scholarship
The Friedrich Ebert Foundation Scholarship provides up to €750 per month to international students, including asylum seekers, pursuing a degree in Germany. Eligibility includes a bachelor's degree, proficiency in German, and admission to a German university. Deadline: Varies, [https://www.fes.de](https://www.fes.de).
### Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung Scholarship
The Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung Scholarship offers up to €750 per month to international students, including asylum seekers, pursuing a degree in Germany. Eligibility includes a bachelor's degree, proficiency in German, and admission to a German university. Deadline: Varies, [https://www.kas.de](https://www.kas.de).
### Heinrich Böll Foundation Scholarship
The Heinrich Böll Foundation Scholarship provides up to €750 per month to international students, including asylum seekers, pursuing a degree in Germany. Eligibility includes a bachelor's degree, proficiency in German, and admission to a German university. Deadline: Varies, [https://www.boell.de](https://www.boell.de).
### Deutscher Akademischer Austauschdienst (DAAD) Helmut-Schmidt-Programme
The Deutscher Akademischer Austauschdienst (DAAD) Helmut-Schmidt-Programme offers up to €750 per month to international students, including asylum seekers, pursuing a master's degree in Germany. Eligibility includes a bachelor's degree, proficiency in German, and admission to a German university. Deadline: October 31, [https://www.daad.de](https://www.daad.de).

## Application Steps
1. Research the scholarship: Look for scholarships that match your eligibility criteria and career goals.

3. Prepare your documents: Gather all required documents, including transcripts, language proficiency certificates, and letters of recommendation.
