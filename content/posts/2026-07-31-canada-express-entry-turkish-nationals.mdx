## Who Qualifies
Turkish nationals can qualify for various visa categories under Canada's Express Entry program, including the Federal Skilled Worker Program, Federal Skilled Trades Program, and Canadian Experience Class. To be eligible, applicants must meet the requirements for one of these programs, which include having relevant work experience, language proficiency, and education.

## Application Steps
1. Determine which visa category you are eligible for under the Express Entry program.
2. Create an online profile and express your interest in immigrating to Canada.
3. If selected, submit a complete application for permanent residence.
4. Pay the required fees, including the application fee and biometrics fee.
5. Submit supporting documents, including language test results and educational credentials.
6. Undergo a medical examination and obtain a police certificate.
7. Attend an interview with a Canadian immigration officer, if required.
8. Receive a decision on your application and, if approved, obtain a permanent resident visa.
