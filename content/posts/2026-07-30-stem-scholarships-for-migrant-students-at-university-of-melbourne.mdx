# STEM Scholarships
The Melbourne School of Engineering Scholarship exists for migrant students, including those holding a Student visa (subclass 500), and provides funding of up to $10,000 per year. 
The University of Melbourne offers various STEM scholarship opportunities for migrant students, including those on [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.com/by-eligibility/refugees) and [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-funding/fully-funded). 

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for these scholarships, migrant students must hold a valid Australian visa, such as a Student visa (subclass 500), Graduate visa (subclass 485), or a Bridging visa. 
They must also meet the specific eligibility criteria for each scholarship, including academic requirements and English language proficiency. 
Some scholarships may also require students to be enrolled in a specific course or program, such as [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-level/phd) in STEM fields.

## Scholarships
### Melbourne School of Engineering Scholarship
The Melbourne School of Engineering Scholarship provides up to $10,000 per year for migrant students enrolled in an undergraduate or graduate degree in the Melbourne School of Engineering. 
The eligibility criteria include a minimum weighted average mark of 80% and proficiency in English language. 
The deadline for applications is usually in February, and students can apply through the [University of Melbourne website](https://scholarships.unimelb.edu.au/). 

### International Undergraduate Scholarship
The International Undergraduate Scholarship offers up to $56,000 over four years for migrant students commencing an undergraduate degree at the University of Melbourne. 
The eligibility criteria include a minimum ATAR of 90 and completion of an Australian Year 12 or equivalent. 
The deadline for applications is usually in December, and students can apply through the [University of Melbourne website](https://scholarships.unimelb.edu.au/).

### Graduate Research Scholarship
The Graduate Research Scholarship provides a stipend of up to $32,000 per year for migrant students enrolled in a graduate research degree at the University of Melbourne. 
The eligibility criteria include a master's degree or equivalent and proficiency in English language. 
The deadline for applications is usually in October, and students can apply through the [University of Melbourne website](https://scholarships.unimelb.edu.au/).

### Science Graduate Scholarship
The Science Graduate Scholarship offers up to $10,000 per year for migrant students enrolled in a graduate degree in the Faculty of Science at the University of Melbourne. 
The eligibility criteria include a minimum weighted average mark of 75% and proficiency in English language. 
The deadline for applications is usually in February, and students can apply through the [University of Melbourne website](https://scholarships.unimelb.edu.au/).

### Australia Awards Scholarship
The Australia Awards Scholarship provides up to $30,000 per year for migrant students from developing countries enrolled in an undergraduate or graduate degree at the University of Melbourne. 
The eligibility criteria include a minimum ATAR of 80 and completion of an Australian Year 12 or equivalent. 
The deadline for applications is usually in April, and students can apply through the [Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade website](https://www.dfat.gov.au/people-to-people/australia-awards/Pages/australia-awards-scholarships.aspx).
