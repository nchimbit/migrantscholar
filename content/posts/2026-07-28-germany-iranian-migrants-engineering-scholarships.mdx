The Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung Scholarship exists for Iranian migrants, providing funding of up to €1,200 per month for students pursuing engineering fields in Germany.

## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for these scholarships, Iranian migrants must hold a valid visa, which can be a student visa, job seeker visa, or a visa for humanitarian reasons. The most common visa categories for Iranian migrants in Germany include:
- Student visa (Visum zu Ausbildungszwecken)
- Job seeker visa (Visum zum Zwecke der Arbeitsplatzsuche)
- Visa for humanitarian reasons (Visum aus humanitären Gründen)
For more information on visa categories and requirements, visit the [Germany visa website](https://migrantscholar.com/countries/Germany).

## Scholarships
### DAAD Scholarship
The DAAD Scholarship is a fully funded scholarship that provides Iranian migrants with up to €750 per month for undergraduate, master's, and PhD programs in engineering fields. Eligible candidates must have a strong academic record and a valid visa. The deadline for application is usually in October of each year. For more information, visit the [official DAAD website](https://www.daad.de/en/).
### Deutschlandstipendium
The Deutschlandstipendium provides Iranian migrants with a monthly stipend of up to €300 for undergraduate and master's programs in engineering fields. Eligible candidates must have a strong academic record and a valid visa. The deadline for application is usually in April of each year. For more information, visit the [official Deutschlandstipendium website](https://www.deutschlandstipendium.de/en/).
### Heinrich Böll Foundation Scholarship
The Heinrich Böll Foundation Scholarship provides Iranian migrants with up to €750 per month for undergraduate, master's, and PhD programs in engineering fields. Eligible candidates must have a strong academic record and a valid visa. The deadline for application is usually in March of each year. For more information, visit the [official Heinrich Böll Foundation website](https://www.boell.de/en/foundation/scholarships).
### Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung Scholarship
The Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung Scholarship provides Iranian migrants with up to €1,200 per month for undergraduate, master's, and PhD programs in engineering fields. Eligible candidates must have a strong academic record and a valid visa. The deadline for application is usually in July of each year. For more information, visit the [official Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung website](https://www.kas.de/en/).
### Friedrich Ebert Foundation Scholarship
The Friedrich Ebert Foundation Scholarship provides Iranian migrants with up to €750 per month for undergraduate, master's, and PhD programs in engineering fields. Eligible candidates must have a strong academic record and a valid visa. The deadline for application is usually in November of each year. For more information, visit the [official Friedrich Ebert Foundation website](https://www.fes.de/en/).
