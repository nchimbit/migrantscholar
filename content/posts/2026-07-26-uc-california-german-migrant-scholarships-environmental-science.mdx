# UC Scholarships
The University of California President's Fellowship for German migrant students in environmental science exists and provides up to $25,000 in funding to qualified applicants. This fellowship is designed for master's or doctoral students in environmental science who are from Germany and have a strong academic record.

## Who Qualifies
To qualify for the University of California President's Fellowship, German migrant students must meet certain eligibility criteria, including having a valid F-1 or J-1 visa category. Other visa categories, such as F-2 or J-2, may also be eligible, but applicants should check with the university's admissions office to confirm.

## Available Scholarships
The following scholarships are available to German migrant students in environmental science:
### University of California President's Fellowship
* Amount: up to $25,000
* Eligibility: master's or doctoral students in environmental science from Germany
* Deadline: January 15, 2027
* Official URL: https://www.universityofcalifornia.edu/academics/president-fellowship
### Environmental Defense Fund (EDF) Fellowship
* Amount: $50,000
* Eligibility: graduate students in environmental science or related fields
* Deadline: March 1, 2027
* Official URL: https://www.edf.org/jobs/fellowships
### National Science Foundation (NSF) Graduate Research Fellowship Program (GRFP)
* Amount: $34,000
* Eligibility: graduate students in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM) fields, including environmental science
* Deadline: October 24, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.nsf.gov/funding/pgm_summ.jsp?pims_id=6201
### Fulbright Scholarship
* Amount: up to $100,000
* Eligibility: graduate students from Germany who are pursuing a degree in environmental science or related fields
* Deadline: October 11, 2026
* Official URL: https://us.fulbrightonline.org/
### German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD) Scholarship
* Amount: up to €1,200 per month
* Eligibility: graduate students from Germany who are pursuing a degree in environmental science or related fields

* Official URL: https://www.daad.de/en/
