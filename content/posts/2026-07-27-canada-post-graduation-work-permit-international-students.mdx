Canada Post-Grad Work
International students who complete a post-secondary program in Canada may qualify for a Post-Graduation Work Permit, which can lead to permanent residency in Canada. The permit allows students to work in Canada for up to three years, depending on the length of their study program. ## Who Qualifies
* International students who have completed a post-secondary program at a designated learning institution in Canada
* Students who have completed a program of at least eight months in duration
* Students who have a valid study permit or had a valid study permit at the time of application
* Students who apply within 90 days of receiving their final marks or transcript
* Students who have not previously been issued a Post-Graduation Work Permit following any other program of study

## What the Scholarship Covers
The Post-Graduation Work Permit allows international students to work in Canada for up to three years, depending on the length of their study program. The permit is an open work permit, which means that students can work for any employer in any location in Canada. The permit does not cover tuition fees or living expenses, but it provides international students with the opportunity to gain work experience in Canada and potentially transition to permanent residency.

## How to Apply
1. Ensure that you meet the eligibility criteria for the Post-Graduation Work Permit.
2. Complete your post-secondary program and receive your final marks or transcript.
3. Apply for the Post-Graduation Work Permit within 90 days of receiving your final marks or transcript.
4. Submit your application online or by paper, depending on your circumstances.
5. Pay the required application fee.
6. Provide all required documents, including a valid passport, proof of completion of studies, and proof of language proficiency (if required).
7. Wait for processing of your application, which may take several weeks or months.
8. Receive your Post-Graduation Work Permit, if your application is approved, and begin working in Canada.

## Required Documents
* Valid passport
* Proof of completion of studies (transcript or diploma)
* Proof of language proficiency (if required)
* Proof of identity
* Proof of study permit or authorization to study in Canada
* Other documents as required by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada
