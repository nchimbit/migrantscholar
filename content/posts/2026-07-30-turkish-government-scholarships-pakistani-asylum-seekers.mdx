The Turkey Scholarships exist and qualify Pakistani asylum seekers to receive funding amount of up to 1,600 TL per month for undergraduate and graduate studies. 
Pakistani asylum seekers can apply for these scholarships as international students, along with other nationalities, to study in Turkey. 
As a Pakistani asylum seeker, you can apply for these scholarships to study in universities in Turkey. 

## Who Qualifies
To qualify for Turkish government scholarships, Pakistani asylum seekers need to have a valid visa, which includes student visa, research visa, or other relevant visa categories. 
The eligibility criteria for Turkish government scholarships include academic excellence, language proficiency, and other requirements as specified by the scholarship programs. 
For more information on visa categories, you can check the official government website of Turkey.

## Available Scholarships
### Turkish Government Scholarship
The Turkish Government Scholarship provides 1,600 TL per month for undergraduate and graduate studies, and it is eligible for Pakistani asylum seekers who have a valid visa and meet the academic requirements. The deadline for application is usually in February, and you can check the official website [www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr](http://www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr) for more information.

### Kadir Has University Scholarship
The Kadir Has University Scholarship provides full tuition fee waiver and monthly stipend of 1,000 TL for undergraduate and graduate studies, and it is eligible for Pakistani asylum seekers who meet the academic requirements. The deadline for application is usually in June, and you can check the official website [www.khas.edu.tr](http://www.khas.edu.tr) for more information.

### ITU Fellowship
The ITU Fellowship provides 1,500 TL per month for PhD studies, and it is eligible for Pakistani asylum seekers who meet the academic requirements and have a valid research visa. The deadline for application is usually in April, and you can check the official website [www.itu.edu.tr](http://www.itu.edu.tr) for more information.

### YTB Scholarship
The YTB Scholarship provides 1,200 TL per month for undergraduate and graduate studies, and it is eligible for Pakistani asylum seekers who meet the academic requirements. The deadline for application is usually in March, and you can check the official website [www.ytb.gov.tr](http://www.ytb.gov.tr) for more information.

### TUBITAK Scholarship
The TUBITAK Scholarship provides 2,000 TL per month for PhD studies, and it is eligible for Pakistani asylum seekers who meet the academic requirements and have a valid research visa. The deadline for application is usually in September, and you can check the official website [www.tubitak.gov.tr](http://www.tubitak.gov.tr) for more information.
