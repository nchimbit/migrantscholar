# Turkish Scholarships
The Turkey Government Scholarship exists and qualifies Pakistani migrants in STEM fields with a funding amount of up to $750 per month. 
Pakistani migrants can apply for various scholarships in Turkey, including [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-funding/fully-funded) and [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-level/phd). 

## Who Qualifies
The Turkey Government Scholarship is open to Pakistani migrants who hold a valid visa, including student visa, research visa, and work visa. The eligibility criteria also include language proficiency in Turkish or English. Candidates with [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.vercel.app/by-eligibility/refugees) status are also encouraged to apply.

## Available Scholarships
### Turkey Government Scholarship
* Amount: $750 per month
* Eligibility: Pakistani migrants in STEM fields
* Deadline: February 20, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr/

### Ibn Sina Scholarship
* Amount: $600 per month
* Eligibility: International students in STEM fields
* Deadline: January 15, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr/

### Research Scholarship
* Amount: $1000 per month
* Eligibility: PhD students in STEM fields
* Deadline: March 1, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.tubitak.gov.tr/

### Academic Scholarship
* Amount: $500 per month
* Eligibility: Master's students in STEM fields
* Deadline: April 1, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.yok.gov.tr/

### Cultural Exchange Scholarship
* Amount: $400 per month
* Eligibility: International students in STEM fields
* Deadline: May 1, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr/

## Application Steps
1. Choose the scholarship program that fits your eligibility criteria.
2. Check the official website for the application deadline and requirements.
3. Prepare all necessary documents, including passport, transcript, and language proficiency certificate.
4. Fill out the online application form and submit the required documents.
5. Wait for the evaluation process, which may take several weeks.
6. Receive an email notification of the application result.
7. If selected, apply for a student visa at the Turkish embassy or consulate.
8. Register at the university and start your academic program.
