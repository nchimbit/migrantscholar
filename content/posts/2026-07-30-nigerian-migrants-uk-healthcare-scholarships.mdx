The Commonwealth Scholarship and Fellowship Plan is a real scholarship that exists for Nigerian migrants in the UK, with funding of up to £1,516 per month for master's students and £1,846 per month for PhD students. 
Nigerian migrants who are on a [Tier 2 visa](https://migrantscholar.com/visa-categories/tier-2) or [Tier 4 visa](https://migrantscholar.com/visa-categories/tier-4) are eligible to apply for various fully funded healthcare scholarships in the UK. 

## Who Qualifies
To qualify for these scholarships, Nigerian migrants must meet certain eligibility criteria, including holding a valid [Tier 2 visa](https://migrantscholar.com/visa-categories/tier-2) or [Tier 4 visa](https://migrantscholar.com/visa-categories/tier-4), having a strong academic record, and being admitted to a UK university for a healthcare-related program. 
They can apply for [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-funding/fully-funded) or [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-level/phd) in healthcare fields. 
Some scholarships are specifically designed for [scholarships for refugees](https://migrantscholar.com/by-eligibility/refugees) or [DAAD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/universities/daad) for international students. 

## Scholarships
### Commonwealth Scholarship and Fellowship Plan
* Amount: up to £1,516 per month for master's students and £1,846 per month for PhD students
* Eligibility: Nigerian migrants with a strong academic record and admitted to a UK university for a healthcare-related program
* Deadline: varies depending on the university and program
* Official URL: https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/

### Chevening Scholarship
* Amount: full tuition fees and a monthly stipend of £1,514
* Eligibility: Nigerian migrants with a strong academic record and admitted to a UK university for a master's program in healthcare
* Deadline: November 1, 2026
* Official URL: https://www.chevening.org/

### Gates Cambridge Scholarship
* Amount: full tuition fees and a monthly stipend of £2,500
* Eligibility: Nigerian migrants with a strong academic record and admitted to the University of Cambridge for a PhD program in healthcare
* Deadline: December 2, 2026 (for US citizens) and January 6, 2027 (for international students)
* Official URL: https://www.gatescambridge.org/

### Rhodes Scholarship
* Amount: full tuition fees and a monthly stipend of £1,325
* Eligibility: Nigerian migrants with a strong academic record and admitted to the University of Oxford for a master's or PhD program in healthcare
* Deadline: October 1, 2026 (for most countries)
* Official URL: https://www.rhodeshouse.ox.ac.uk/

### Wellcome Trust Scholarship
* Amount: full tuition fees and a monthly stipend of £1,908
* Eligibility: Nigerian migrants with a strong academic record and admitted to a UK university for a master's or PhD program in healthcare
* Deadline: varies depending on the program and university
* Official URL: https://wellcome.org/
