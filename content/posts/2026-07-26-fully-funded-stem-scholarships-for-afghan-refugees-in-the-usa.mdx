STEM Scholarships
The Albert Einstein Scholarship is a fully funded scholarship for Afghan refugees in the USA, offering $50,000 per year for the duration of a STEM undergraduate or graduate program. This scholarship is open to Afghan refugees who hold a valid asylum or refugee visa. Please check the official scholarship website for the latest information on funding and eligibility.

## Who Qualifies
* Afghan refugees who hold a valid asylum or refugee visa (AS-1 or AS-2)
* Applicants who have completed secondary school or have a bachelor's degree
* Those who are admitted to a STEM undergraduate or graduate program at a US university
* Candidates who demonstrate financial need and a strong academic record
* English proficiency is required, and applicants must submit IELTS or TOEFL scores

## What the Scholarship Covers
The Albert Einstein Scholarship covers the following expenses:
* Tuition fees for the duration of the undergraduate or graduate program
* Living expenses, including accommodation and food
* Health insurance for the duration of the scholarship
* Travel expenses to and from the US for the scholarship recipient

## How to Apply
1. Check the eligibility criteria on the official scholarship website
2. Review the list of participating US universities and their STEM programs
3. Submit an online application form, which includes personal and academic information
4. Upload required documents, such as transcripts, diplomas, and English proficiency test scores
5. Submit a personal statement outlining academic and career goals
6. Provide two academic or professional references
7. Submit a copy of the valid asylum or refugee visa
8. Review and submit the application before the deadline

## Required Documents
* Valid asylum or refugee visa (AS-1 or AS-2)
* Passport
* Birth certificate
* Transcripts and diplomas from previous academic institutions
* English proficiency test scores (IELTS or TOEFL)
* Personal statement
* Two academic or professional references
* Proof of admission to a US university
