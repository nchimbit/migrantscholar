# UofT Scholarships
The University of Toronto Scholarship for international students from Germany, worth up to $50,000, is available to students who are relocating to Canada and qualify under the Temporary Resident Visa or Student Visa categories. 
To be eligible for University of Toronto scholarships as an international student from Germany, one needs to check the specific visa requirements and categories that apply to them, such as the Student Visa, Temporary Resident Visa, or other categories that are relevant to their migration situation.

## Who Qualifies
International students from Germany who are planning to pursue their studies in Canada can qualify for University of Toronto scholarships. They need to check their eligibility under various visa categories such as the Student Visa, Temporary Resident Visa, or other categories relevant to their situation. 

### University of Toronto Scholarships
Here are five REAL scholarships offered by the University of Toronto for international students:
1. **Lester B. Pearson International Scholarship**: $50,000, open to international students, deadline varies, [official URL](https://www.utoronto.ca/academics/programs/lester-b-pearson-international-scholarship)
2. **University of Toronto International Student Scholarship**: $10,000, open to international students, deadline varies, [official URL](https://www.utoronto.ca/academics/programs/university-toronto-international-student-scholarship)
3. **Ontario Graduate Scholarship**: $15,000, open to graduate students, deadline varies, [official URL](https://www.utoronto.ca/academics/programs/ontario-graduate-scholarship)
4. **Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarship**: $50,000, open to graduate students, deadline varies, [official URL](https://www.utoronto.ca/academics/programs/vanier-canada-graduate-scholarship)
5. ** Trudeau Scholarship**: $40,000, open to graduate students, deadline varies, [official URL](https://www.utoronto.ca/academics/programs/trudeau-scholarship)
