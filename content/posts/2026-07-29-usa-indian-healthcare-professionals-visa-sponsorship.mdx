The Fogarty International Center's [Global Health Program for Fellows and Scholars](https://www.fic.nih.gov/) exists and qualifies Indian healthcare professionals for funding of up to $50,000. 
Indian nationals who are healthcare professionals can apply for visa sponsorship in New York to work in the healthcare sector.

## Who Qualifies
To qualify for visa sponsorship, Indian healthcare professionals must have a valid passport, a degree in a healthcare field, and relevant work experience. They can apply under various visa categories, including H-1B, L-1, O-1, and J-1. [Canada scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/countries/Canada) and [fully funded scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-funding/fully-funded) can also provide an alternative option for those who are not eligible for a US visa.
