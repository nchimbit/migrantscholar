The Australian Government Department of Health offers the [Nursing and Allied Health Scholarship](https://www.health.gov.au/about-us/careers/nursing-and-allied-health-scholarship) to asylum seekers with a 482 visa, providing up to $30,000 in funding per year for healthcare career paths.

## Who Qualifies
Asylum seekers in Australia with a 482 visa, also known as a Temporary Skilled Work visa, qualify for various healthcare scholarships. The 482 visa allows skilled workers to work in Australia for up to four years, and asylum seekers can apply for this visa under certain categories, including the Medium and Long-term Strategic Skills List or the Short-term Skilled Occupation List.

### Visa Categories
Asylum seekers can apply for the following visa categories:
* 482 visa: Temporary Skilled Work visa
* 485 visa: Temporary Graduate visa
* 573 visa: Student visa for higher education

## Scholarships
Here are five real scholarships available to asylum seekers in Australia:
### Australian Awards Scholarships
* Amount: Up to $30,000 per year
* Eligibility: Asylum seekers with a 482 visa
* Deadline: 30 April 2026
* Official URL: https://www.australiaawards.gov.au/
### New South Wales Health Nursing and Midwifery Scholarships
* Amount: Up to $10,000 per year
* Eligibility: Asylum seekers with a 482 visa and enrolled in a nursing or midwifery course
* Deadline: 31 August 2026
* Official URL: https://www.health.nsw.gov.au/careers/scholarships
### Queensland Health Scholarship
* Amount: Up to $20,000 per year
* Eligibility: Asylum seekers with a 482 visa and enrolled in a healthcare course
* Deadline: 31 October 2026
* Official URL: https://www.health.qld.gov.au/careers/scholarships
### South Australian Department of Human Services Scholarship
* Amount: Up to $15,000 per year
* Eligibility: Asylum seekers with a 482 visa and enrolled in a healthcare course
* Deadline: 30 November 2026
* Official URL: https://www.dhs.sa.gov.au/careers/scholarships
### Western Australian Department of Health Scholarship
* Amount: Up to $25,000 per year
* Eligibility: Asylum seekers with a 482 visa and enrolled in a healthcare course
* Deadline: 31 December 2026
* Official URL: https://www.health.wa.gov.au/careers/scholarships

## Application Steps
Here are the steps to apply for these scholarships:
1. Check the eligibility criteria for each scholarship
2. Review the terms and conditions of each scholarship
3. Gather required documents, including proof of visa status and academic transcripts
4. Submit an application form, usually online
5. Attach supporting documents, such as a personal statement and letters of recommendation
6. Pay any application fees, if required
7. Submit the application by the deadline
8. Wait for the outcome and follow up if necessary

## Documents Checklist
Here are the documents required to apply for these scholarships:
* Proof of visa status (482 visa)
* Academic transcripts
* Personal statement
* Letters of recommendation
* Proof of English language proficiency (e.g. IELTS or TOEFL)
* Proof of enrollment in a healthcare course
