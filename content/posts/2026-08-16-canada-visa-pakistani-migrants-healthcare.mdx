## Scholarships
### Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarships
The Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarships provide funding of up to $50,000 per year for three years to doctoral students, and are eligible to Canadian citizens, permanent residents, and international students, with a deadline of November 1st, and can be found on the official [Vanier Canada Graduate Scholarships](https://vanier.gc.ca/en/home-accueil.html) website.
### Queen Elizabeth II Diamond Jubilee Scholarship
The Queen Elizabeth II Diamond Jubilee Scholarship provides funding of up to $10,000 to undergraduate and graduate students, and is eligible to Canadian citizens and permanent residents, with a deadline of February 1st, and can be found on the official [Queen Elizabeth II Diamond Jubilee Scholarship](https://www.ualberta.ca/university-of-alberta-international/awards-and scholarships/queen-elizabeth-ii-diamond-jubilee-scholarship) website.
### Pierre Elliott Trudeau Foundation Scholarship
The Pierre Elliott Trudeau Foundation Scholarship provides funding of up to $60,000 per year for three years to graduate students, and is eligible to Canadian citizens and permanent residents, with a deadline of December 21st, and can be found on the official [Pierre Elliott Trudeau Foundation Scholarship](https://www.trudeaufoundation.ca/en/programs/scholarships) website.
### Canada Graduate Scholarships - Master's Program
The Canada Graduate Scholarships - Master's Program provides funding of up to $17,500 to master's students, and is eligible to Canadian citizens and permanent residents, with a deadline of December 1st, and can be found on the official [Canada Graduate Scholarships - Master's Program](https://www.nserc-crsng.gc.ca/Students-Etudiants/PG-CS/CGSM-BESCM_eng.asp) website.
### Ontario Graduate Scholarship
The Ontario Graduate Scholarship provides funding of up to $15,000 per year to graduate students, and is eligible to Canadian citizens and permanent residents, with a deadline of October 30th, and can be found on the official [Ontario Graduate Scholarship](https://osap.gov.on.ca/AID/GUIDE/ENGLISH/shtml) website.

## Application Steps
1. Research and choose a scholarship that matches your eligibility and interests.
2. Review the application requirements and deadlines.
3. Prepare and gather all required documents, including transcripts, letters of recommendation, and language proficiency test results.
4. Submit your application through the scholarship provider's website or mailing address.
5. Ensure you meet the language proficiency requirements, such as IELTS or TOEFL for English, or TEF for French.
6. Apply for a study permit and visa, if required.
7. Provide proof of financial support, such as a bank statement or scholarship award letter.
