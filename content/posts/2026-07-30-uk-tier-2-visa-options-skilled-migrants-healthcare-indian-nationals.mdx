## Who Qualifies
To be eligible for a UK Tier 2 visa, Indian nationals must meet specific requirements, including having a job offer from a UK-based employer, meeting the required language proficiency, and having the necessary qualifications and experience in their field. The main visa categories for healthcare professionals include Tier 2 General, Tier 2 Intra-Company Transfer, and Tier 2 Sportsperson. [Canada scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/countries/Canada) and [PhD scholarships](https://migrantscholar.com/by-level/phd) are also available for Indian nationals.

## Available Scholarships
The following scholarships are available for Indian nationals seeking to pursue a career in healthcare in the UK:
### Chevening Scholarship
* Amount: up to £18,000 per year
* Eligibility: Indian nationals with a bachelor's degree and at least two years of work experience
* Deadline: November 1st
* Official URL: https://www.chevening.org/
### Commonwealth Scholarships
* Amount: up to £15,000 per year
* Eligibility: Indian nationals with a bachelor's degree and at least two years of work experience
* Deadline: December 1st
* Official URL: https://csc.ukri.org/
### Erasmus Mundus Scholarship
* Amount: up to €1,400 per month
* Eligibility: Indian nationals with a bachelor's degree and at least two years of work experience
* Deadline: January 15th
* Official URL: https://ec.europa.eu/programmes/erasmus-mundus/
### Wellcome Trust Scholarship
* Amount: up to £30,000 per year
* Eligibility: Indian nationals with a bachelor's degree and at least two years of work experience in a relevant field
* Deadline: February 15th
* Official URL: https://wellcome.org/
### UK Government Scholarship
* Amount: up to £10,000 per year
* Eligibility: Indian nationals with a bachelor's degree and at least two years of work experience
* Deadline: March 1st
* Official URL: https://www.gov.uk/
