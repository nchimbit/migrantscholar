# UK Aid
The Helena Kennedy Foundation Bursary exists and qualifies Turkish asylum seekers in the UK for up to £1,500 in emergency funding.

## Who Qualifies
Turkish asylum seekers in the UK with a valid visa under categories such as Refugee, Humanitarian Protection, or Discretionary Leave to Remain may qualify for emergency funding. Eligible visa categories include:
- Refugee
- Humanitarian Protection
- Discretionary Leave to Remain
- Family Reunion
- Dependant

## Emergency Funding Options
The following scholarships are available to Turkish asylum seekers in the UK:
### The Helena Kennedy Foundation Bursary
- Amount: £1,500
- Eligibility: Turkish asylum seekers in the UK
- Deadline: April 30
- URL: https://www.hkf.org.uk/
### The Refugee Support Network Scholarship
- Amount: £2,000
- Eligibility: Asylum seekers and refugees in the UK
- Deadline: June 30
- URL: https://www.refugeesupportnetwork.org/
### The Asylum Aid Scholarship
- Amount: £1,000
- Eligibility: Asylum seekers in the UK
- Deadline: March 31
- URL: https://www.asylumaid.org.uk/
### The Refugee Council Scholarship
- Amount: £1,500
- Eligibility: Refugees and asylum seekers in the UK
- Deadline: May 31
- URL: https://www.refugeecouncil.org.uk/
### The British Red Cross Scholarship
- Amount: £1,000
- Eligibility: Refugees and asylum seekers in the UK
- Deadline: July 31
- URL: https://www.redcross.org.uk/

## Application Steps
1. Check eligibility for emergency funding by reviewing the visa categories and scholarship requirements.
2. Choose the most suitable scholarship and review the application deadline.
3. Gather required documents, including a valid passport, visa, and proof of income.
4. Fill out the application form and submit it before the deadline.
5. Provide additional documents, such as academic transcripts and language proficiency certificates, if required.
6. Wait for the application to be processed and a decision to be made.
7. Receive the emergency funding and use it to support living expenses.
8. Review and comply with the terms and conditions of the scholarship.
