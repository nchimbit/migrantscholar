# Women Aid
The Herbert Quandt Foundation offers emergency funding for women-only initiatives, specifically targeting Syrian asylum seekers in Germany, with a funding amount of up to €5,000. This initiative aims to support women in need, providing them with the necessary resources to pursue their goals. Please check the official scholarship website for the latest information on eligibility and application deadlines.

## Who Qualifies
* Syrian asylum seekers with a valid residence permit in Germany
* Women who have been affected by the Syrian conflict and are in need of emergency funding
* Applicants must demonstrate a strong commitment to their goals and provide a detailed plan for the use of the funding
* Priority is given to women who are single parents, have disabilities, or are otherwise disadvantaged

## What the Scholarship Covers
The Herbert Quandt Foundation Emergency Funding covers a range of expenses, including:
* Living costs, such as rent and utilities
* Education and training expenses, including course fees and materials
* Healthcare costs, including medical treatment and medication
* Other essential expenses, such as food and transportation
